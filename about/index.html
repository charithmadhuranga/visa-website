Our Story

From Classroom in Sri Lanka to Campus Abroad

Lanka Global Access Consultancy was founded with a simple belief: every student deserves clear, honest, and expert guidance when pursuing a student visa — from choosing the right course to landing with approval in hand.

From our office in Nugegoda, we have guided hundreds of Sri Lankan students through university admissions, student visa filings, interview preparation, financial documentation, and refusal appeals — building a reputation for transparency and results along the way.

Today we send students to Australia, Canada, the UK, New Zealand and the USA — with end-to-end support covering everything from IELTS/PTE preparation and Genuine Student (GS) statements to pre-departure briefings.
