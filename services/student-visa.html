How Our Student Visa Support Works

Lanka Global Access (LGA Visa) is a student visa consultancy based in Nugegoda, Sri Lanka. We help students choose a suitable course and university, prepare and lodge applications, assemble visa-ready documentation and prepare for visa interviews.

Two milestones matter and they are separate: a university offer letter and a visa decision. A university admission confirms a place on a course; only the destination country's immigration authority can grant a visa. Our role is to prepare both applications to the highest possible standard — we do not control, and never promise, a visa outcome.

Student Visa Documents & Preparation

The core document set most Sri Lankan applicants need. Your final checklist depends on the destination, course and visa subclass — we confirm yours during counselling.

Academic Documents

  • O/L & A/L certificates (or degree transcripts)
  • English test score (IELTS/PTE/TOEFL)
  • Passport (valid beyond the course end date)
  • CV / work experience letters
  • Statement of Purpose (we refine it)
  • Reference letters ×2

Financial Documents

  • Bank statements / fixed deposits (typically a 28-day history)
  • Sponsor letter & income proof
  • Education loan approval (if applicable)
  • GIC / tuition payment receipts (Canada)
  • Property / asset valuations (supporting)
  • CA statement summarising funds

Understanding Financial Requirements

Every destination requires proof that you can pay tuition and support yourself. Amounts, acceptable evidence and holding periods differ by country and change over time — for example, Canada's proof-of-funds rules (which often include a GIC for faster processing), Australia's financial evidence rules and the UK's maintenance funds each set their own thresholds, and the UK requires funds to be held for a qualifying period (commonly 28 consecutive days) before applying.

We structure your funds early, confirm the current official figures against the relevant government website before lodgement, and prepare a clear source-of-funds narrative so a visa officer can follow every rupee. Never move large sums into your account days before applying without professional guidance — unexplained deposits are a common refusal reason.

Always verify current thresholds on the official immigration website of your destination before applying. Rules and fees change without notice.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a student visa take from Sri Lanka?

Processing times depend on the destination, season and completeness of your file, and they change. We prepare complete applications to avoid rework and keep you updated, but the decision timeline is set by each immigration authority.

Do you guarantee a visa approval?

No honest consultant can. Only a government immigration officer decides a visa. What we guarantee is a thorough, accurate and well-prepared application — and honest advice about your chances before you spend on fees.

Can I work while studying abroad?

Most destinations allow student visa holders to work limited hours during term and full-time during breaks, with specific conditions. We explain the current work rules for your destination before you apply.

What if my visa is refused?

A refusal is not the end. We review the refusal reasons, correct the weaknesses in the file and help you reapply or appeal where the route allows. Many of our clients succeeded on a second, stronger application.

How much does a student visa application cost?

You will pay university application fees, the government visa fee, and often health insurance and biometrics. Costs vary by country and course. We give you a transparent breakdown during your free consultation — with no hidden charges.

Do I need IELTS or PTE for a student visa?

Almost always, yes — universities and immigration authorities require proof of English proficiency, with the required band or score varying by course and country. We advise your target score and offer IELTS/PTE preparation through partner institutes.
