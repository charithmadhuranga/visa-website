Australia FAQs

Can my spouse join me?

Yes — dependants can usually accompany Master's/PhD students, with full work rights for spouses of Master's students.

How long does the visa take?

Most subclass 500 decisions arrive within 4–8 weeks of a complete lodgement. Early preparation keeps you on track for your intake.
