Australia FAQs

Can my spouse or children join me?

Dependant rules tightened in 2024 — eligible dependants are now generally limited to students in research master's and doctoral (PhD) courses. We check the current rules for your specific course before you apply.

How long does the visa take?

Processing times vary by season, application volume and how complete your file is — Home Affairs publishes current estimates but never guarantees a date. Early, complete preparation is the best protection against delays.
