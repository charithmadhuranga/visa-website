USA FAQs

How hard is the F-1 interview?

It's a 2–5 minute conversation about your study plans, funding and intent to return home. Our mock interviews prepare you for every common question pattern.

Can I get scholarships?

Yes — merit awards at private universities and assistantships at graduate level can cut costs dramatically. We identify realistic options in your shortlist.

Community college first?

A smart budget route: 2 years at a community college, then transfer into a university's 3rd year to complete your bachelor's degree.
