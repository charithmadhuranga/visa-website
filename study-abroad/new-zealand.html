New Zealand FAQs

Are scholarships available?

Yes — universities offer merit-based tuition discounts for international students, and some NZ government scholarships target specific countries. We match you to what you qualify for.

Can I work full-time during breaks?

Most tertiary students can work full-time during scheduled vacation periods if their programme is a full-time course of one academic year or longer.

Is the visa interview required?

NZ student visas are largely paper/online assessments — interviews are rare but Immigration NZ may request more information. Complete files rarely face issues.
