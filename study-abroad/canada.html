Why Canada?

Education That Leads to Residency

  • Affordable excellence
    Lower tuition and living costs than the US, UK or Australia for comparable quality.
  • PR-friendly policies
    Express Entry and PNP streams reward Canadian graduates with residency routes.
  • Co-op & internships
    Many programs include paid co-op placements built into the curriculum.
  • Safe & inclusive
    Consistently ranked among the safest, most multicultural countries on earth.

Key Requirements

  • Letter of Acceptance from a Designated Learning Institution (DLI)
  • IELTS 6.0–6.5 band (or PTE equivalent) usually required
  • GIC / proof of funds for tuition + living expenses
  • Strong Statement of Purpose explaining your study plan
  • Upfront medical examination
  • Study permit application filed with LGA support

Canada FAQs

What is a GIC?

A Guaranteed Investment Certificate (approx. CAD 20,635 + first-year tuition) that serves as proof of funds and releases money back to you monthly after arrival.

Can I get PR after studying?

Yes — most graduates qualify for a Post-Graduation Work Permit, then apply through Express Entry (CEC) or Provincial Nominee Programs.

College vs University in Canada?

Colleges offer hands-on diplomas with strong job outcomes; universities offer degrees. Both can lead to a PGWP if the institution is a DLI. We help you pick based on goals.
