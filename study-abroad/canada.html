Canada FAQs

Can I get PR after studying?

Many graduates qualify for a Post-Graduation Work Permit, then apply through Express Entry (CEC) or a Provincial Nominee Program. PGWP eligibility — including field-of-study rules for non-degree programs — depends on your institution and course, so we check this before you commit.

College vs University in Canada?

Colleges offer hands-on diplomas with strong job outcomes; universities offer degrees. Both can lead to a PGWP if the institution is a DLI and you meet current eligibility. We help you pick based on goals.

Official Canadian Government Sources
