UK FAQs

What is a CAS?

A Confirmation of Acceptance for Studies — an electronic reference from your university required for the visa application, issued after you accept your offer.

Can dependants come with me?

Rules changed in 2024: dependants are generally limited to students on research postgraduate programmes (PhD) and government-funded courses. We'll advise based on your course.

Is IELTS mandatory?

Most universities accept alternatives like PTE or internal assessments, but some visas require UKVI-approved tests. We check the exact requirement for your chosen route.
