About University of West London (UWL)

The University of West London draws on a heritage of over 150 years in teaching and professional education. From the outset, it has played, and continues to play, a significant role in the educational, cultural, and economic life of its region. Over the years, leading academic institutions, career-focused training providers, and industry specialists have come together to shape what it is today—a university dedicated to students’ careers.

Students are taught by industry professionals with connections to global networks in their chosen fields, and many courses offer the opportunity to undertake placement years where applicable, alongside lifelong careers advice after graduation. The University offers a wide range of high-quality facilities, including an industry-standard Boeing 737 flight simulator, radio, TV and film production studios, nursing simulation centres, music recording suites, and professional kitchens. Beyond the classroom, London becomes an extended campus, offering access to world-class museums, theatres, and landmarks, and the chance to experience one of the most diverse cultural environments in the world.

Why University of West London (UWL)

  • London Location, Local Community: Studying at the University of West London’s West London Campus offers the best of both worlds, with a vibrant local community and beautiful parks nearby, alongside easy access to the energy of central London just a few stops away on the Tube.
  • Great Employment Prospects: Industry-led teaching and specialist careers advice make studying at the University of West London a strong choice for future career success. Students have opportunities to search for placements, engage with employers to gain hands-on experience, and receive tailored, lifelong careers counselling and support.
  • Learning with Industry Experts: Courses at the University of West London are taught by industry experts, and students benefit from their insight, experience, and support as they prepare for their future careers. The University’s consistently high standard of teaching has been recognised with a Silver award in the Teaching Excellence Framework (TEF).
  • Personal Support: Starting university brings a range of new opportunities and challenges, which is why the University of West London provides support every step of the way, offering advice on careers, placements, finances, accommodation, health, faith, and study skills. Even before students begin life at UWL, support is available with guidance on applications, funding, and accommodation.

MBA · Business & Management

Enhanced Extended Masters in Business Administration (MBA)

19 months – 20 months Next intake: Feb 2027 Apply by 31 Jan 2027

The Extended Masters and Enhanced Extended Masters programmes at the University of West London are intended for students who already have an undergraduate degree but do not meet the entry requirements to study for a Masters degree. Over a full-time semester, students will study four modules specially designed to improve their academic and English language skills:

  • Advanced Study Skills
  • Critical Thinking, Analysis and Evaluation
  • Statistics
  • English (Business English on the Enhanced course)

The Enhanced Extended Masters is similar to the Extended Masters, but with a greater focus on improving students’ English skills. It also includes a Business English module, which will allow students to gain a full understanding of this critical subject area.

Once completed, students will progress directly onto the core Masters course.

The MBA at the University of West London has been designed to support students’ career progression, preparing them to carry out managerial roles successfully and to build their own business. Students will study with a cohort of experienced professionals drawn from a range of industries, building their network and gaining insights into best practice.

Students will explore issues that have a significant impact within organisations by working with others and building on both personal individual experiences, as well as comparing and contrasting with their cohort members. They will carry out projects and assignments designed to help them critique and apply the theories, tools and techniques they learn.

Much of the learning is developed through the establishment of collaborative learning communities of programme participants and tutors, and students will become part of that community of learning. Their understanding is developed around the key skills, processes and practices of business, leadership and management.

Students can choose to study this course on a day-release basis to help them balance their studies with other commitments.

Accommodation options

Applicants to the University of West London can benefit from guaranteed accommodation designed to be secure, supportive, and social—helping them feel at home throughout their studies. The University recognises that accommodation is a key part of the student experience and has partnered with local, purpose-built providers to offer competitive rates that are often better than booking directly. By arranging accommodation through the University, applicants also receive additional support throughout the booking process.

Chapter White City, located in East Acton, provides convenient access to Ealing Broadway station as well as the University’s shuttle bus, which serves the Ealing and Brentford campuses. With nearby Central Line stations, central London is just 20 minutes away.

The Paragon, opening in September 2026, offers modern student accommodation just a 10-minute journey from St Mary’s Road. A free and frequent shuttle service connects the Paragon to the Ealing campus, making travel to lectures straightforward and stress-free.
