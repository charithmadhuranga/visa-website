About Universal Higher Education

Universal Higher Education UK (UHE UK) is a London-based higher education study centre operated as part of the Oxford International Education Group network. UHE UK delivers undergraduate and postgraduate higher education programmes in partnership with established UK universities, including the University of Roehampton and the University of Bradford. Its programmes are designed to provide students with recognised UK qualifications alongside a supportive, career-focused learning experience.

UHE UK has a diverse international student community and focuses on accessible teaching, small class sizes and personalised academic support. Students can benefit from guidance from tutors and student-support staff throughout their studies, helping them build academic knowledge, confidence and career-ready skills.

Why Universal Higher Education UK?

  • Supportive Learning Environment:UHE UK focuses on a student-centred learning experience with small class sizes and accessible tutors. Academic and student-support teams help students settle into their studies, remain engaged and make progress towards their academic goals.
  • International Student Community: UHE UK welcomes students from around the world, creating a multicultural learning environment where students can interact with people from different countries and backgrounds. The institution highlights its international community as an important part of the student experience.
  • Recognised UK University Partnerships: UHE UK delivers programmes in partnership with established UK universities. Undergraduate programmes are designed and awarded by the University of Roehampton, while postgraduate programmes are designed and awarded by the University of Bradford.

Master's · Business & Management

Master of Science – International Business and Management

1 year Next intake: Jan 2027 Apply by 5 Dec 2026

The MSc International Business and Management is an internationally recognised degree, awarded by the University of Bradford and delivered at UHE UK in London. The programme is designed to provide students with the skills and knowledge required to pursue a wide range of global management opportunities.

Designed to support both career advancement and career change, the programme develops the knowledge, skills and professional capabilities required for effective management in complex and global organisational environments. Graduates are equipped to progress into specialist and leadership roles across a broad range of sectors and international industries.

Students also benefit from the Professional Excellence Accelerator (PEA), which supports the development of professional skills, reflective practice and global competencies required for success in international business careers.

Key Features:

  • International Business & Strategic Management – Students develop expertise in global business, leadership, strategy, marketing, economics and organisational management.
  • Research-Led, Industry-Relevant Learning – Students learn from experienced academics through a curriculum informed by contemporary research, professional practice and current global business challenges.
  • Professional Excellence Accelerator (PEA) – Students enhance their academic performance, leadership potential, employability and career readiness through dedicated weekly professional development sessions.
  • Applied Global Business Experience – Students develop practical problem-solving, analytical and intercultural management skills through real-world business scenarios and contemporary case studies.Research Dissertation – Students complete an independent research project that demonstrates their critical thinking, analytical abilities and specialist knowledge in international business and management.

Master's · Engineering & Construction

Master of Science – Project Planning and Management

1 year Next intake: Jan 2027 Apply by 5 Dec 2026

The MSc Project Planning and Management is an internationally recognised degree, awarded by the University of Bradford and delivered at UHE UK in London. The programme is designed to equip students with the knowledge and practical skills required to plan, manage and deliver successful projects across global industries.

Students develop expertise in key areas such as project design, financial appraisal, implementation and risk management, alongside essential leadership and communication skills. The programme combines academic theory with real-world application through case studies, workshops and industry-informed teaching. A final dissertation also allows students to specialise in a chosen area of interest.

Students also benefit from the Professional Excellence Accelerator (PEA), which supports their academic and professional development. Graduates are well prepared for project planning and management roles across international development, government, business and the non-profit sector worldwide.
