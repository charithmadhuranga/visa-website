About Birmingham City University (BCU)

The Birmingham City University offers a range of subjects and specialisms across six Schools – Arts; Business; Law and Social Sciences; Architecture, Built Environment, Computing and Engineering; Life and Health Sciences; and Nursing and Midwifery – as well as the Royal Birmingham Conservatoire. The university’s early history can be traced back to 1843 with the founding of the Birmingham Society of Arts and School of Design. Over time, five individual colleges were brought together to form The City of Birmingham Polytechnic in 1971, which later became Birmingham City University.

With around 31,000 students from 100 countries (HESA Student Record 2021/22 © Higher Education Statistics Agency Limited), Birmingham City University is recognised as a large and diverse institution. The university places students at the centre of its activities, focusing on providing them with the best opportunities for future success.

Why Birmingham City University (BCU)

  • Industry-Standard Facilities: BCU has invested over £500 million across its campuses and facilities. The university’s developments include the state-of-the-art Parkside Building, which houses four film and TV studios, and the simulated operating theatre and hospital wards at the City South Campus. These investments aim to provide students with the best possible learning environments.
  • International Scholarships: BCU provides a variety of undergraduate and postgraduate scholarship opportunities for international students, aimed at supporting them throughout their studies.
  • Employability Support: While studying in the UK, students at BCU have the opportunity to gain valuable, paid work experience through flexible on-campus jobs. Many courses also include the option of completing an industry placement, providing practical experience and helping students develop skills relevant to their future careers. The BCU award recognises students’ extracurricular achievements, demonstrating to employers that they possess more than just academic qualifications.
  • At The Heart Of A Vibrant, Diverse City: Located in the heart of the United Kingdom, Birmingham provides an ideal setting for students. The city offers a vibrant atmosphere with lively street markets, a wide variety of restaurants, and numerous sports and entertainment venues, ensuring there is something to suit every interest. As BCU is centrally located within the city of Birmingham, it offers an extensive range of accommodation options, providing students with a variety of choices to suit their preferences and needs.

Bachelor of Arts (Honours) – Business Management

3 years Next intake: Sep 2027 Apply by 1 Sep 2027

The BA (Hons) Business Management degree is designed to provide practical, skills-based learning that prepares students for a wide range of career opportunities within the dynamic and evolving world of business. The programme develops knowledge across core business and management functions, building confidence and enabling graduates to pursue careers in areas such as business consultancy, government, or entrepreneurship.

A strong emphasis is placed on emerging technologies, including artificial intelligence, as well as employability and sustainability, ensuring students are well prepared for modern business environments. The course aims to develop adaptable, innovative, and agile professionals capable of responding to change and driving organisational success.

Students benefit from the use of an Assessment Centre that mirrors recruitment processes used by major organisations, helping them to develop key employability skills such as CV writing, interview techniques, and psychometric testing. The programme also supports entrepreneurial ambitions through dedicated modules, as well as access to business incubation and mentorship opportunities via STEAMhouse.

The course is accredited by the Chartered Management Institute (CMI), providing students with industry-relevant knowledge and the opportunity to gain a professional certificate upon successful completion.

Teaching is delivered through interactive and experiential learning methods, including simulations and engagement with real-world business clients. Students develop skills in negotiation, data analysis, leadership, and project management, while also gaining an understanding of contemporary and international business markets. The programme explores key areas such as human resource management, finance, strategic planning, and cultural awareness, alongside the impact of digital transformation and the ethical considerations associated with modern business practices.

The course also offers an optional professional placement year, allowing students to gain valuable industry experience after completing their second year. Students are supported in securing placements through the university’s careers services and employer networks, and may transfer to the placement version of the course once a suitable opportunity is obtained.

Master's · Business & Management

Master of Science – Management and International Business

1 year Next intake: Jan 2027 Apply by 1 Jan 2027

The MSc Management and International Business degree at Birmingham City University is designed to equip students with the essential skills and attributes required to become effective international managers. Accredited by the Chartered Management Institute (CMI), the course develops students’ ability to make strategic and tactical business decisions, understand cultural differences in global contexts, and manage and motivate teams across international organisations.

Throughout the programme, students work collaboratively in diverse, international teams on projects that mirror real-world professional practice. These projects involve producing reports and presentations as part of module assessments, helping students build practical skills. The course team provides ongoing support to help students develop their knowledge, confidence, and career readiness.

In the first stage of the course, students explore key challenges faced by global businesses, gaining insight into the complexity of leadership and management. This stage focuses on developing organisational, communication, and teamwork skills, particularly within culturally diverse environments.

In the second stage, students examine international operations management alongside corporate social responsibility, sustainability, and innovation. They also study international strategy and enhance their research skills in preparation for their final project.

The final stage consists of a Major Project tailored to individual career goals. Students can choose from three options: a work-based study linked to a current employer, a consultancy project addressing a real international business problem for a client, or a traditional dissertation involving in-depth research. This flexibility allows students to specialise in areas aligned with their career ambitions. Previous projects have explored topics such as international marketing strategy, global supply chain management in the food industry, and sustainability in cotton sourcing.
