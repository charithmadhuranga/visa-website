About Health Sciences University (HSU)

Health Sciences University (HSU) is a specialist university transforming lives through high quality education, research, clinical and consultancy services in allied health, sport and exercise, rehabilitation and social science disciplines.

Its newly established Health Business School offers bespoke programmes for students wishing to establish or develop a career in healthcare management. Programmes are designed and delivered by highly-regarded academics and professionals from the health and business sectors to provide unique insights into different healthcare management examples and their applications.

Located at HSU’s central London campus, the Health Business School is ideally located for developing professional networks and making the most of everything this international city has to offer.

Why Health Sciences University (HSU)

  • Location: Study and live in the world's best student city. Located in the heart of London close to iconic landmarks. The Health Business School is located at the London campus on Borough High Street, in a vibrant neighbourhood just a short walk from London Bridge and the River Thames.
  • Staff Expertise: Teaching staff are drawn from a variety of health and business backgrounds to offer unique insights for students. Course structure, academic content and teaching methods equip Business School graduates to launch a successful and rewarding career contributing positively to the future of healthcare across the world.
  • Exceptional Student Support: From academic support to pastoral care, students are treated with care and compassion. Health Sciences University puts people and well-being at the heart of everything they do. They aim to deliver a positive student experience and good graduate outcomes through expertise, care and compassion.
  • An inclusive learning environment: London campus is home to students from diverse backgrounds and different life stages. Combining big city living with a small, supportive learning community, Health Sciences University offers the best of both worlds.
  • Industry-ready: Programmes are designed to meet the needs of students and the evolving healthcare industry. Learners at Health Sciences University in Central London are well placed to leverage connections and opportunities with world-leading management, commercial and healthcare institutions as they study and after they graduate.

MBA · Health & Nursing

Master of Business Administration (MBA) – Management with Health Data Analytics and AI

15 months Next intake: Jan 2027 Apply by 1 Jan 2027

The MBA Management with Health Data Analytics and AI at Health Sciences University combines strategic healthcare management with applied knowledge of data analytics and responsible artificial intelligence. Students develop the ability to interpret complex evidence, make informed decisions, lead multidisciplinary teams, and support improvements in healthcare through practical learning.

As data, digital technologies, and AI become increasingly important in healthcare, the programme develops strategic management and leadership capabilities alongside specialist knowledge of analytics, data governance, and technology-enabled innovation. It emphasises responsible decision-making and the safe use of emerging technologies.

Students learn to interpret evidence and apply it to strategic, operational, and clinical decisions. They also examine governance and ethical considerations associated with adopting AI in healthcare management. Learning is considered across public, private, and third-sector healthcare settings, including service providers and organisations that supply healthcare products.

Specialist study is complemented by core subjects in global health policy, people management and leadership, and marketing strategy. This enables students to connect their knowledge of data and technology with broader organisational priorities.

Delivered through a combination of on-campus and online study in London, the 15-month programme incorporates healthcare case studies, live organisational briefs, and consultancy activities. A dedicated three-month component gives students the option of completing a university-based live consultancy project or undertaking an external professional practice placement. External placements are competitive, are not guaranteed, and may involve additional costs.

MBA · Health & Nursing

Master of Business Administration (MBA) – Management with Health Economics and Finance

15 months Next intake: Jan 2027 Apply by 1 Jan 2027

The MBA Management with Health Economics and Finance at Health Sciences University develops specialist knowledge of healthcare economics alongside strategic management skills. Students explore healthcare funding, resource allocation, and financial risk, learning to use economic evidence to inform decisions about service spending, the sustainability of health systems, and healthcare delivery.

The programme examines how healthcare decision-makers balance population needs, patient outcomes, and financial sustainability. Students develop an understanding of economic analysis and financial management, with specialist study in healthcare funding, budgeting, resource allocation, systems risk, and governance.

Drawing on the management expertise of the Health Sciences University Health Business School, students study comparative health economics and policy alongside leadership, people management, and marketing strategy. International examples enable them to compare healthcare systems, assess spending priorities, and evaluate funding risks.

The knowledge developed is relevant to roles in healthcare organisations, regulatory bodies, government, and consultancy. Students learn to connect economic evidence with practical management decisions.

Delivered through a combination of on-campus and online study in London, the 15-month programme includes applied research and a dedicated three-month work-based learning component. Students can investigate an organisational challenge and present recommendations through a live consultancy project based at the university, or undertake a relevant external professional practice placement. External placements are competitive, are not guaranteed, and may involve additional costs.

MBA · Health & Nursing

Master of Business Administration (MBA) – Management with Healthcare Supply Chain Management

15 months Next intake: Jan 2027 Apply by 1 Jan 2027

The MBA Management with Healthcare Supply Chain Management at Health Sciences University develops management expertise in healthcare procurement, logistics, and facilities operations. Students explore how supplier selection, resource coordination, and sustainable supply chains contribute to effective healthcare delivery, applying their learning to real organisational challenges.

The programme examines how healthcare services rely on effective sourcing, dependable logistics, and well-managed facilities. Students develop knowledge of supplier analysis, tendering, resource management, and budgeting, and consider how operational decisions can support safe and sustainable healthcare services.

Specialist study in healthcare supply chain management, project management, and systems thinking is complemented by subjects taught through the Health Business School, including global health policy, leadership, people management, and marketing strategy. Learning addresses governance, health and safety, and sustainability across hospitals, pharmaceutical organisations, healthcare product manufacturers, and suppliers. International supply chains are also explored, particularly in relation to organisations that produce and distribute healthcare products.

Delivered through a combination of on-campus and online study in London, the 15-month programme includes applied research and a dedicated three-month work-based learning component. Students can investigate an organisational challenge and present recommendations through a live consultancy project based at the university, or pursue a relevant external professional practice placement. External placements are competitive, are not guaranteed, and may involve additional costs.
