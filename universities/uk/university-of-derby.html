Overview

About University of Derby

The University of Derby is pushing the boundaries of knowledge to create a better, fairer, and more sustainable future for everyone. Their thriving community of researchers, professionals, and practitioners influence the creation of new knowledge by sharing their expertise. The university's students learn in state-of-the-art facilities, and the university plays a key role in the communities of Derby and Derbyshire.

Research lives in the University of Derby's classrooms. They explore, innovate, and discover solutions to the challenges facing all now and in the future – in the city, region, and across the world. The University applies this in their teaching, powering the next generation of gamechangers, nurturing talent, and helping their students to develop the skills, resilience, and curiosity they will need to make an impact.

Why University of Derby - Derby

  • Location: The Derby Campus is made up of several different sites. Getting between them and the halls of residence couldn't be easier. Whether students are heading to lectures or into town, they can hop on to the University's Unibuses or enjoy a walk to unwind. Derby sits in the centre of England, and it is close to the M1 and M6 motorways, as well as being within easy reach of East Midlands Airport. By train, students can get to London in 90 minutes or to Birmingham in 35 minutes.
  • Facilities: Kedleston Road in Derby is home to the University's fantastic Sports Centre, four-storey library, and spacious study areas. Students will also find plenty of places to eat and socialise with their friends, including Starbucks, Subway, the Union of Students' café and bar, and much more.
  • Research and Innovation: The University of Derby's Innovation and Research Strategy (IRS) drives its ambition to be internationally recognised for high-quality research. Through six Academic Research Themes, they unite expertise across colleges to enhance research excellence and tackle global challenges.
  • Recognition: The University of Derby has been recognised as 2nd for employability and course quality by Uni Compare in 2026. It was also recognised as 1st in the East Midlands for International by Whatuni Student Choice Awards 2025, and no.1 for Accommodation by the Global Student Living Awards 2024, 2023, 2022.
  • Investment in the Future of Business Education: Opened in September 2025, the £75 million Cavendish Building is a seven-storey development in Derby city centre. It has been purpose-built to drive collaboration between students, academics, and industry, with a focus on solving real-world challenges. It boasts state-of-the-art, flexible learning spaces that promote collaboration, creativity, and connectivity, including a high-tech Creativity Lab to unlock new ideas, a fully equipped Trading Floor with Bloomberg and London Stock Exchange Group terminals, a specialist Supply Chain Lab, and an immersive Extended Reality Suite to develop learning in a virtual environment.

Master's · Business & Management

Master of Science – International Business

1 year Next intake: Jan 2027 Apply by 1 Jan 2027

The MSc International Business at the University of Derby focuses on applied analysis in economics, strategy, law, finance, marketing, and human resources, all examined from a global perspective. The programme provides opportunities to engage with regional experts and international business leaders through guest lectures, consultancy projects, case studies, and professional mentoring. Students can enhance their employability and build their CVs through the Futures Award in Leadership in International Business, and gain practical experience using real-world data via Bloomberg to support research into companies and markets worldwide. The programme also offers specialist variants including MSc International Business and Finance, MSc International Business and Marketing, and MSc International Business and HRM.

Designed to meet the needs of today’s business environment, the MSc International Business equips students with a thorough understanding of the economic, financial, legal, and cultural factors affecting international business. The course integrates disciplines such as international business strategy, management and marketing, international economics, and international trade legislation. Drawing on real-world case studies, students develop skills in quantitative analysis and applied research while examining all aspects of business operations in an international context.

The programme maintains high-profile partnerships with leading industry names, including Lloyds Bank Corporate, the Bank of England Midlands Office, HSBC Nottingham, Eversheds, Price Waterhouse Cooper, PKF Cooper Parry, Smiths of Derby, GI Insight, and Kitchencraft, with many contributing through guest lectures. Company visits provide additional insights, with tours of the iPro Stadium, Derby Arena, Crown Derby, and high-performance engineering company epm:technology. Students can extend professional networks through events such as Institute of Directors workshops, company taster days, and the University’s Network of Entrepreneurs.

Professional mentoring is provided to guide career development. Mentors include international trade advisors, banking specialists, and senior managers from partner companies, offering insights into career paths and business practice. Career development planning is embedded within the course, with each student supported by a personal tutor and access to the University’s professional career services, even after graduation.

The programme also prioritises the development of transferable skills and real-world business experience. Students gain expertise using the Bloomberg database, a leading global business resource providing financial and company information, economic data, and market analysis, and can pursue the Bloomberg Market Concepts Certificate. Opportunities are provided to participate in competitions such as the IBM University Business Challenge and the University Trading Challenge, as well as to contribute to regional specialist projects like the economic impact study of the Buxton Festival and the Derby Low Carbon Initiative. Research can also be published in the College of Business Economic Bulletin or in the academic journal Critical Perspectives on Business Management.

The MSc culminates in an Independent Study, allowing students to select a theme aligned with their interests and career ambitions, developing in-depth research and critical thinking skills. Recent research topics have included knowledge transfer in the South African hotel sector, consumer complaint behaviour in Chinese online shopping, leadership challenges in SMEs in the UK and USA, foreign market analysis for Spanish exporters, and social media strategies in the beauty industry.

In addition, students may undertake the Futures Award in Leadership in International Business, which recognises leadership abilities and consultancy skills through extra-curricular activities, including business consultancy projects, volunteering, fundraising, and networking opportunities. Past participants have had the chance to hear from influential regional leaders such as Mel Morris, Chairman of Derby County FC and an early investor in King Digital Entertainment, the company behind the global game phenomenon Candy Crush.

MBA · Business & Management

Master of Business Administration (MBA) – Global

1 year Next intake: Jan 2027 Apply by 1 Jan 2027

The Master of Business Administration (MBA Global) at Derby provides in-depth, applied knowledge of digital platforms, artificial intelligence, and sustainable business practices, preparing graduates to become responsible, authentic, and future-ready business leaders.

The programme offers the opportunity to undertake a six-month social impact project, enabling the practical application of skills to address current organisational and societal challenges while delivering tangible value to real businesses. Students study alongside peers from around the world and build strong professional networks across the private, public, and third sectors through collaborative, practice-based learning. Teaching is delivered by academics engaged in cutting-edge research and consultancy with leading global ‘blue-chip’ organisations, ensuring that learning remains current, relevant, and informed by real industry practice. Full-time international students also have the option to undertake a 9–12 month placement in a relevant sector in the UK, gaining hands-on professional experience and significantly enhancing employability within a competitive global job market.

The contemporary business and commercial environment is evolving at an exponential pace, driven by disruptive technologies and increasing demands for responsible and sustainable business development. Organisations seeking to succeed in this rapidly changing landscape require leaders and managers equipped with advanced knowledge, professional skills, ethical traits, and strong behavioural capabilities to navigate uncertainty, add strategic value, and support organisational growth. The MBA Global programme is designed to cultivate these competencies, with a focus on digital innovation, artificial intelligence, modern working practices, and the embedding of sustainability into business decision-making and leadership.

For full-time international students, the degree may be studied with a 9–12 month UK-based industry placement integrated into the programme, subject to successful completion of all taught modules. Dedicated support is provided through the Careers and Employment Service alongside the MBA Global programme team, including tailored guidance for placement applications, networking events, CV development workshops, and interview skills training to enhance professional readiness.

The curriculum is aligned to key global initiatives, including the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals, ensuring a strong emphasis on responsible leadership and ethical business practice. Students develop a comprehensive understanding of how value can be created in ways that enhance organisations, employees, consumers, wider society, and the natural environment. The programme aims to produce graduates capable of generating innovative insights and proposing effective solutions to contemporary business challenges while demonstrating the values and professional standards expected by organisations and society both now and in the future.

MBA · Business & Management

Master of Business Administration (MBA) – Global Block Delivery

1 year Next intake: Oct 2027 Apply by 1 Oct 2027

The MBA Global is an innovative programme designed for working professionals seeking to gain an internationally recognised management qualification without taking a career break. The course combines short, intensive on-campus study periods with flexible off-site learning, enabling participants to balance academic commitments with professional responsibilities regardless of global location. The programme is accredited by the Chartered Management Institute (CMI), allowing graduates to apply for Chartered Manager status upon completion. An alternative to the traditional dissertation is offered through a corporate placement project, which enables participants to address a significant real-world organisational challenge with direct practical impact. Teaching is delivered by a highly experienced team of business and management specialists, and students study within small, professionally diverse cohorts, supported by enhanced academic tutoring designed to ensure consistent progress throughout the programme.

The MBA Global is structured to meet the increasing global demand for managers capable of performing, thinking, and leading from an international perspective. It aims to nurture creative, dynamic, and innovative leaders equipped to succeed in senior positions worldwide. This contemporary MBA programme challenges participants both academically and practically, developing the ability to analyse and resolve complex business problems that are directly relevant to professional practice, while encouraging positive change within organisations and industry sectors.

The block delivery format typically attracts senior managers and professional executives from a wide array of industries, including construction, energy, aviation, and the public sector. Lecturers bring substantial business and management experience, combining academic expertise with real-world practice. Their ongoing engagement in influential research and consultancy ensures that teaching content remains current and industry-relevant. Programme delivery is further strengthened by partnerships with regional, national, and international employers and leading professional bodies, including organisations such as Rolls-Royce, Boots, JCB, Toyota, Bombardier, DHL, the NHS, and international government ministries.

A strong emphasis is placed on global business thinking, reflecting the growing need for leaders to adopt perspectives that extend beyond domestic markets. Each module advances applied knowledge of business within international and global contexts. Teaching takes place within small but highly diverse classroom environments, encouraging active discussion, shared learning, and the exchange of professional best practice among participants from a wide range of cultural and professional backgrounds. The MBA Global attracts students from across mainland Europe and worldwide, including the United States, India, China, Pakistan, Nigeria, and the Gulf States, fostering a rich international learning community.

The programme curriculum covers key areas such as responsible and authentic leadership, entrepreneurship and innovation, strategy, finance and business transformation, sustainable business operations, research methods, digital marketing strategies for international markets, business analytics for strategic decision-making, independent dissertation study, and a social impact project.
