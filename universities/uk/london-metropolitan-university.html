Overview

About London Metropolitan University

Based in one of the world's most exciting capital cities, London Metropolitan University is home to a diverse community of inspiring and determined learners, teachers and innovative thinkers. They offer a wide range of courses, from undergraduate and postgraduate degrees to short courses and professional qualifications. London Metropolitan University takes pride in helping students reach their goals and succeed in their careers. All the undergraduate courses include some form of work-related learning, such as a placement, client project or on-campus work experience, and many courses are accredited by professional bodies.

At London Metropolitan University, students are placed at the centre of everything. London Metropolitan University is constantly reviewing their courses to respond to the demands of the job market and give students a diverse choice. London Metropolitan University is committed to providing students with the best possible support and resources.

London Metropolitan University's dedicated careers and employability team will support and advise students both during and after their studies. London Metropolitan University runs a mentoring scheme, which can put students in touch with a professional from their chosen sector, as well as a graduate internship scheme, which offers around 30 paid internships each year. Many courses include work placement modules that count towards a final degree and many are accredited by professional bodies, which will give student qualifications extra weight.

London Metropolitan University is proud of their vibrant, multi-ethnic and socially diverse community, and encourage students to join them – they currently have more than 120 nationalities studying at the university.

Pre-Master's and Extended Master's programs are available at London Met via the Education Center Australia (ECA) and are not available to apply to directly via LMU

Why London Metropolitan University

  • Facilities: The University boasts state-of-the-art learning, library and IT facilities where students can hone their skills – from a mock courtroom, purpose-built art, design and recording studios to a £100,000 journalism newsroom and the £30 million Science Centre, which is one of the largest science teaching labs in Europe.
  • Student Services: London Metropolitan University provides students with accommodation, funding and general career advice. They also offer disability and dyslexia support and an on-site counselling service. The support services are designed to ensure that students receive all the guidance they can during their time at London Metropolitan University.
  • Sport and Fitness: London Metropolitan University runs an extensive programme of fitness classes and has a modern gym with state-of-the-art cardiovascular equipment. Their sports facilities include a full-sized basketball court, complete with scoreboard, and a dance studio.

This four-year Business Management (Foundation Year) BA (Hons) degree has a built-in foundation year (Year 0) that provides students with an alternative route into higher education if they don't have traditional qualifications or can't meet the business management entry requirements.

During the foundation year, students will engage with business challenges in a stimulating environment, while boosting their confidence and acquiring crucial academic skills. This acts as excellent preparation for the subsequent three years of study, which will allow students to specialise in more specific and advanced areas of business.

The innovative Business Management (Foundation Year) BA (Hons) degree has been devised with valuable industry input, giving students an edge when it comes to seeking that all-important job when they graduate.

At London Metropolitan University, students will acquire key business skills and knowledge in the classroom but will be provided with a wide range of opportunities outside of the classroom too. London Metropolitan University's Business Management and Marketing BA degree is a joint honours programme that will provide students with expertise in both disciplines, offering them a greater range of career opportunities on graduation. London Metropolitan University's course is accredited by the Chartered Institute of Marketing (CIM) and students will receive exemption from two out of three units that are necessary to obtain the CIM Certificate in Professional Marketing. This is a highly sought-after qualification in the marketing industry, especially if students are considering a career in marketing management. The professional certificate means students will be rewarded with two qualifications by the time they finish their studies. This would give students a ‘competitive edge’ in the job market.

As well as developing the skills and knowledge required for either a career in business management or as a professional marketer, the business management and marketing course will provide students with entrepreneurial skills and understanding that will allow them to start their own business.

If students are passionate about starting their own business, they will not only gain the necessary academic knowledge and skills on the course, but also receive encouragement and support through the Accelerator. The Accelerator is a business incubator that helps students make their business dreams a reality by offering mentoring, advice and workshops. Accelerator has supported many successful budding entrepreneurs and can support students with developing any business idea they might have. Through the Accelerator, students will also get the opportunity to enter the Big Idea Challenge for a chance to win a share of the £30,000 prize to launch their business.

This programme is uniquely comprehensive among business management and marketing courses in the UK. In addition to learning broad marketing skills that apply to the commercial world, the programme also provides students with opportunities to develop new specialist marketing skills to help improve society through the relatively new field of Social Marketing. In this new field, students will learn how to apply the latest marketing techniques to social problems, such as obesity, drunk-driving, discrimination and domestic violence. Lecturers will introduce the concepts and history of social marketing based on their own innovative research. Completing this module will give students an advantage when competing for jobs in the third and public sector.

As part of the course, students will have the opportunity to undertake short and long accredited work placements. Past students on the module have found that it has helped them gain recognition from the Institute of Sales and Marketing Management (ISMM), a professional organisation that represents sales professionals in the UK.

This business management and marketing programme not only aims to improve students' career opportunities, but also seeks to accelerate their cultural and personal growth. Students will find opportunities to study abroad for a semester in places such as Europe, Japan or the USA, where they will be immersed in a new culture and acquire new perspectives. Master of Arts – Education

1 year Next intake: Jan 2027 Apply by 23 Nov 2026

This Education MA degree is designed to provide students with a broad range of knowledge and skills within the field of education. This course will provide students with opportunities to develop and enhance their professional skills and understanding, so that students can progress within or embark on a career within the sector.

This diverse course covers a range of disciplines, drawing from areas such as curriculum theory, psychology of learning and human potential, sociology of gender, social class, race, philosophy and the history of education.

This course aims to introduce students to key academic and professional debates within the field, helping them to develop a professional voice and standpoint. Upon successful completion of this course, London Metropolitan University hopes that students will feel enthused about returning to the field and feel intellectually empowered to initiate change.

A key theme embedded in this master’s degree is social justice. This course is aligned with the Education for Social Justice (ESJ) strategy to ensure equity in the curriculum practice.

The modules have been developed to broaden the knowledge and meaning of the term ‘education’, as well as its purpose from being a ‘product’ to understanding how the curriculum is managed and led. Students will also explore its influence on children’s learning, assessment and life chances. This degree has five core 20 credit modules:

  • Curriculum, Pedagogy and Assessment
  • Curriculum Leadership
  • Critical Theory and Education
  • Research Methods in Education
  • Education Dissertation

Specialist option modules allow students to explore subjects that are of particular interest to them in more detail, such as:

  • Applying Learning Technologies
  • Issues in Language Learning: An Intercultural Approach
  • Reflective Practice
  • Social Justice Education
  • Web-based Learning and Teaching
  • Understanding the Language Classroom

If students have a particular interest in studying women abuse, they are also able to choose from the following option modules:

  • Sexual Violence: Causes, Consequences and Interventions
  • Violence against Women: Issues, Research and Policy

If there’s a specific topic students would like to study in more detail that does not appear here, they can choose an alternative education-focused topic in agreement with a subject supervisor.

MBA · Business & Management

Master of Business Administration (MBA) – Master of Business Administration

1 year Next intake: Jan 2027 Apply by 23 Nov 2026

The world of business and work is changing rapidly. With automation, AI, online working, climate change and diversity, students will need to be prepared to face and respond to change.

The MBA has been designed using advice and insight from large organisations and business entrepreneurs. This course aims to prepare students for greater business responsibility, switching industries or job roles, or to start their own business.

Students will gain practical skills and experience through business simulations, where they will be making real business decisions to ensure the imitation businesses make a profit and can operate. The first simulation focuses on understanding business finance and accounting, while the second focuses on global strategy. These will involve analysing finances, the supply chain and operations. Most importantly, students will gain experience of working in a team to make decisions in a competitive environment and then deal with the results. London Metropolitan University is one of the few universities in the UK that incorporates business simulation activities into the course.

Students will refine their complex problem solving skills and develop their critical thinking abilities. The MBA will help develop their management skills, how to work effectively in teams, take on the leadership of a diverse group and influence others.

The business world is a volatile environment. Students will study the impact of accelerating climate change, technology development and global politics on the UK’s business economy, learning how to make fundamental business decisions in uncertain times and with incomplete data.

Insights into the wider role of businesses in addressing societal problems in a sustainable manner will also develop their understanding of ethical management and governance.

Students may have had a break from studies and been in the work environment for some time. To help them prepare for the MBA, London Metropolitan University has developed a one-week bootcamp module to help build students develop their skills and knowledge prior to starting the course, so that they can really maximise their learning potential on the course. This bootcamp will help students establish what their specific goals are and serve as a personal development plan to help keep them on track throughout their studies. It’s completed online the week before students start their first module.

Students will be taught by industry specialists in finance, strategy, creativity, marketing, operations coaching and networking, who have years of practitioner experience.

The aim is for students to graduate with a broad understanding of how businesses operate, how they are managed and how they’re governed. This MBA will prepare students for a more senior role in business, a change in career direction or to go on and start their own business.

There are a wide variety of homestay options available, and our partners do their best to match students and hosts according to their interests and preferences. Homestay hosts include single people, young couples with children and pets, and older couples. All homestay accommodations have been inspected, and all adults in the home have completed a required criminal reference check.
