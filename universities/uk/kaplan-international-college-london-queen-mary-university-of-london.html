About Kaplan International College London: Queen Mary, University of London

Students can build their futures by studying in the UK with a pathway course at Kaplan International College (KIC) London. Situated in the world's top academic city (QS Best Student Cities 2019), KIC London is one of the most-respected international colleges in London. The College offers a wide selection of preparatory courses for students looking to pursue a university degree in the UK. Upon successful completion of a pathway program, students may progress to one of several universities, including the Queen Mary, University of London (Queen Mary).

Queen Mary is one of the UK’s leading research-focused higher education institutions. It is ranked 13th in the UK and 110th in the world (The Times and Sunday Times Good University Guide 2021). A broad range of subjects are taught and researched at the university, including humanities, social sciences, law, medicine and dentistry, and science and engineering.

Queen Mary is home to England's first official medical school, which was founded in 1785. Since, then it has been a place where some of the brightest minds have studied and researched, with 9 staff and alumni having received Nobel prizes. It is one of the largest University of London colleges, with around 28,000 students - 35% of whom are international students from over 160 countries. In 2017, Queen Mary was voted one of the most internationally diverse schools in the world by Times Higher Education World University rankings.

Why Kaplan International College London: Queen Mary, University of London

  • Facilities: KIC London is located in Palace House, which offers students flexible study spaces, private huddle rooms, lots of areas for socializing, and high-speed WiFi throughout the building. Classrooms have been designed to enhance the student experience.
  • Location: Voted the world's premier student city (QS Best Student Cities 2019), London offers students iconic venues, including entertainment, restaurants and bars, shopping, major tourist attractions and more. Just a short walk away from London Bridge station, KIC London is ideally located for exploring the city and all its attractions.
  • Guaranteed progression: Students are guaranteed entry to the university of their choosing when they pass their Kaplan International College London course at the required level with good attendance.
  • Convenient Accommodation: When students study at KIC London, they'll be able to stay in comfortable, modern accommodation that is easy to reach their classes from. Students will enjoy shared social spaces and quiet study areas, as well as their own private bedroom.
  • Staff Support: From extending the student's visa to dealing with homesickness, College staff will be happy to help with any issues students may face. Students will benefit from frequent events arranged by the College, so they can meet new friends and make the most of their time in London.

The Foundation Certificate for Science and Engineering at Kaplan International College London is ideal for students looking to enter to the 1st year of an undergraduate degree at university. The course length for students is determined mainly by their English language level.

All students take a set of common or similar modules to gain key skills for university. If their course is longer than 2 terms, they will take extra modules focusing on one or more of English language, academic skills and basic subject knowledge

With computer science and artificial intelligence being one of the most rapidly growing technological developments of our time, Students could be at the forefront of transforming the world around us. Student life & support

Work while studying

International students can work part-time alongside their studies (subject to visa conditions).

Co-op & placements

Offers co-op or work-placement options in selected programs.

On-campus housing

Students typically arrange off-campus or homestay accommodation.

Accommodation options

Students need to plan their accommodations well in advance of their arrival as all accommodation options fill up quickly, well before the start of the semester.

On-Campus Residence Accommodations

On-Campus residence accommodations are provided per discretion by the school.

Off-Campus Accommodations

The local area has a variety of off-campus rental housing options including single homes, duplexes, apartments and rooms for rent. Students wishing to live off-campus need to research availability on their own and should arrive well before the start of term to do so.
