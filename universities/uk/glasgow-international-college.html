About Glasgow International College (at the University of Glasgow)

The University of Glasgow is the 4th-oldest university in the English speaking world and it has been shaping the minds of bright and ambitious students for more than 550 years. It is a place where the past meets the present in truly exciting ways and attending the University of Glasgow means becoming a member of an academic community with a long history of teaching and research excellence. Follow the footsteps of alumni who have changed the world, including 7 Nobel laureates and 3 British Prime Ministers.

In partnership with the University of Glasgow, the University of Glasgow International College offers a wide selection of pathway programs at undergraduate and postgraduate levels for students who require additional preparation for a degree program. Pathway programs are delivered right on campus and students get to build their academic and English skills with other international students in group classes and through one-on-one tutoring. Upon successful completion of the pathway program, students are guaranteed progression to a degree program and are well-equipped to continue with their studies.

Why Glasgow International College (at the University of Glasgow) (Kaplan International Pathways)

  • Campus: The university's diverse community is made up of students and faculty from over 140 countries. There are more than 150 clubs, societies, and sports teams for students to make new friends with shared interests and hobbies.
  • Facilities: Students in pathway programs not only get to walk through a campus with stunning architecture that dates back hundreds of years, but they also are issued university students cards that grant them access to the university's modern resources which include Europe's largest library and a state-of-the-art gym.
  • Location: Scotland's largest city, Glasgow, is a welcoming place where international students can feel at home. The city offers a great range of dining, shopping, and recreational activities and it is located approximately 1 hour away from Scotland's capital, Edinburgh.
  • Teaching and Research Excellence: The University of Glasgow is a member of the prestigious Russell Group and it is highly ranked in the UK and worldwide - Top 15 in the UK (Times and Sunday Times Good University Guide 2021), University of the Year (Times Higher Education Awards 2020), and Top 80 in the world (QS World University Rankings 2021).

Student life & support

Work while studying

International students can work part-time alongside their studies (subject to visa conditions).

Co-op & placements

Co-op options are limited — ask us about placement-friendly alternatives.

On-campus housing

Students typically arrange off-campus or homestay accommodation.

Accommodation options

Students need to plan their accommodations well in advance of their arrival as all accommodation options fill up quickly, well before the start of the semester.

On-Campus Residence Accommodations

On-Campus residence accommodations are provided per discretion by the school.

Off-Campus Accommodations

The local area has a variety of off-campus rental housing options including single homes, duplexes, apartments and rooms for rent. Students wishing to live off-campus need to research availability on their own and should arrive well before the start of term to do so.
