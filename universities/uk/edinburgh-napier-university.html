About Edinburgh Napier University

At Edinburgh Napier they nurture talent and create knowledge that shapes communities all around the world. Their innovative research provides solutions to society's challenges, their graduates leave ready for the workplace, and they have wide-ranging links with employers and business. Edinburgh Napier comprises six schools with an international focus, each committed to growing their academic reputation, delivering an excellent personalised student experience and building innovation, enterprise and citizenship.

ENU has three campuses in Edinburgh offering advanced facilities for learning, teaching and research. Based in an iconic, prosperous city and a stunning, ambitious forward thinking country, Edinburgh Napier is a great place to live, work and study. Home to the Business School, Craiglockhart campus blends the old and the new. Set within attractive grounds overlooking Edinburgh, facilities include 200- and 400-seat lecture theatres and language and multimedia labs. It's also home to ENU’s innovation and enterprise centre.

Why Edinburgh Napier University - Craiglockhart Campus?

  • Facilities: Students will find all the facilities required to produce graduates ready to succeed in the world of business including: 200-seat Lindsay Stewart lecture theatre; Second 400-seat lecture theatre; Multimedia and language labs; Extensive library and access to more than 100 computers; Light and airy double-height atrium with Starbucks coffee shop; Bright Red Triangle business incubator and Bright Red Ventures consultancy; and The War Poets Collection
  • Academic and Research Excellence: ENU is the top ranked Scottish modern university in the Times/Sunday Times Good University Guide 2021. More than half of their research was rated either “world-leading” or “internationally excellent” in the 2014 Research Excellence Framework (REF).
  • Environmental Sustainability: ENU is working to minimise both their wider and legacy carbon footprint and influence sustainable practices within and beyond their campuses. They are working to achieve net carbon zero in our operations by 2030.
  • Accommodation: ENU currently houses 1,240 students over three sites in and around the city centre. Their accommodation is affordable, modern and supported by a dedicated team of University staff.
  • Student Support: At Edinburgh Napier, they offer students a wide range of support to help them fulfil their potential, keep healthy and make the most of their time there. ENU is the number one university in Edinburgh for student satisfaction (National Student Survey 2020

Student life & support

Work while studying

International students can work part-time alongside their studies (subject to visa conditions).

Co-op & placements

Offers co-op or work-placement options in selected programs.

On-campus housing

Students typically arrange off-campus or homestay accommodation.

Accommodation options

Students need to plan their accommodations well in advance of their arrival as all accommodation options fill up quickly, well before the start of the semester.

On-Campus Residence Accommodations

On-Campus residence accommodations are provided per discretion by the school.

Off-Campus Accommodations

The local area has a variety of off-campus rental housing options including single homes, duplexes, apartments and rooms for rent. Students wishing to live off-campus need to research availability on their own and should arrive well before the start of term to do so.

Homestay

There are a wide variety of homestay options available, and our partners do their best to match students and hosts according to their interests and preferences. Homestay hosts include single people, young couples with children and pets, and older couples. All homestay accommodations have been inspected, and all adults in the home have completed a required criminal reference check.
