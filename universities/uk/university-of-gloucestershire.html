About University of Gloucestershire

The University of Gloucestershire offers a wide range of degrees, courses and apprenticeships at undergraduate and postgraduate levels. Typically, students learn theory from industry experts and put their learning into practice in course work, real-world projects, placements and field trips. The school has a 150-year tradition of education dating back to 1847​. There are over 100 academic courses to choose from and approximately 10,000 students studying at the three campuses in Gloucestershire.

​​​​​Located in the heart of Gloucester, Oxstalls is just 15 minutes’ walk from the city centre giving students easy access to the city’s clubs, bars, shops, theatres, venues and cafes. Oxstalls is also just around the corner from Kingsholm rugby stadium, home to Gloucester Rugby and host to a number of Rugby World Cup matches in 2015. ​In 2018, the University of Gloucestershire's School of Business and Technology moved to the brand-new, state-of-the-art home at the Oxstalls campus. The £18 million development is home to the school's Business, Marketing, Human Resource Management, Law and Accounting and Finance courses.

Why University of Gloucestershire

  • Campus: Located in the heart of Gloucester, the students of Oxstalls benefit from connections to an extensive network of clubs, sports teams, theatres, festivals and venues.
  • Student Life: Explore everything the university has to offer at Freshers' Fayre. This includes BBQs, fancy dress parties and the Freshers' Ball. The Freshers' Fayre is where students can sign up to clubs and societies - and get lots of freebies.
  • Clubs: Get involved in anything of interest including snow club, ukulele, the Bee Project, film, comedy, creative writing, paintballing, Afro-Caribbean and Dumbledore's Army. Students can even start up their own.
  • Athletics: Students who love sport will love Oxstalls' labs and facilities, including the onsite performance lab, strength and conditioning suite, sports therapy clinics, fitness suite, and the all-weather pitch.

NOTE: This school may use UCAS as an external portal for application for some programs. A £20 (single application) or £25 (up to 5 applications) application fee may apply.

Master's · Education & Teaching

Master by Research – Education (Professional)

2 years Next intake: Sep 2027 Apply by 1 Jul 2027

The Professional Master’s by Research in Education adopts an active learning approach with a strong emphasis on real-world application, offering a practical and professionally oriented course tailored for international students. The programme encourages interactive learning through discussions, case studies, guest lectures, and collaborative projects, fostering meaningful engagement between students and lecturers, as well as among peers. Designed to be inclusive, the course promotes integration and collaboration among students from diverse cultural and academic backgrounds.

A distinctive feature of the programme is its strong focus on empirical research. Students are given the opportunity to undertake a research project that critically applies educational theories to real-world challenges. This experience enhances their knowledge and understanding while strengthening critical and analytical thinking skills essential for a global career in education.

While lectures and seminars form part of the teaching approach, students are encouraged to engage in small communities of practice—collaborative peer groups based on shared research interests or methodologies. Peer review and constructive feedback are integrated into the learning process to support the development of reflective listening, critical evaluation, and a commitment to action.

The programme promotes the use of technology in learning to maximise engagement, build transferable skills, and enrich the overall educational experience. A ‘bring your own device’ policy encourages students to develop digital literacies, contributing to innovative learning design and dynamic learning activities.

The course experience is further enhanced through live briefs, real-world case studies, staff-led research initiatives, guest speakers, and strong links to industry. These elements provide students with direct pathways to future career opportunities and ensure a rich and applied learning journey.

Social workers support and empower individuals to make positive changes and improve their lives. They work to protect vulnerable people while respecting and promoting human rights. With an emphasis on experiential learning, the programme includes working in small tutorial and seminar groups, simulations, role play, group work, and opportunities to develop reflective and critical debate.

Student life & support

Work while studying

International students can work part-time alongside their studies (subject to visa conditions).

Co-op & placements

Offers co-op or work-placement options in selected programs.

On-campus housing

Students typically arrange off-campus or homestay accommodation.

Accommodation options

Students need to plan their accommodations well in advance of their arrival as all accommodation options fill up quickly, well before the start of the semester.

On-Campus Residence Accommodations

On-Campus residence accommodations are provided per discretion by the school.

Off-Campus Accommodations

The local area has a variety of off-campus rental housing options including single homes, duplexes, apartments and rooms for rent. Students wishing to live off-campus need to research availability on their own and should arrive well before the start of term to do so.

Homestay

There are a wide variety of homestay options available, and our partners do their best to match students and hosts according to their interests and preferences. Homestay hosts include single people, young couples with children and pets, and older couples. All homestay accommodations have been inspected, and all adults in the home have completed a required criminal reference check.
