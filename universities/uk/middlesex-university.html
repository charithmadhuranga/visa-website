Overview

About Middlesex University

At Middlesex University, students learn in a global community of ambitious students like themselves as well as academics, professionals and artists, industry leaders and entrepreneurs. The courses will equip students with the right skills, knowledge and networks to achieve success in their future career. Choose from undergraduate and postgraduate courses including: Accounting and Finance, Computing, Advertising and Media, Business, Education, Law, Public Relations, Psychology and Counselling and IT.

Students get a quality education while being surrounded with the right opportunities and career support to get them where they want to go. Middlesex teaches by using examples of innovative practice and new research. What students learn on their course is relevant to what’s happening now and will help them make an impact on the future. This means students build specialist skills for their subject and develop a flexible outlook for their career.

Why Middlesex University

  • Campus Location: Everything students need for learning is in one place on the north London campus. Plus the campus is just 30 minutes from central London - the best city in the world for students. London is voted the best student city in the world for studying abroad (QS Best Student Cities 2018).
  • Social Life: For socializing, London has great shopping, from department stores to boutiques and vintage stores. In the evening, there’s always somewhere new to eat and hang out.
  • Facilities: Middlesex' 24/7 libraries have flexible spaces for private and group study and there are plenty of social spaces to meet with lecturers and friends. Facilities include a purpose-built centre for art, design, media and performing arts students, as well as dedicated labs and studios for those studying science, technology and health courses.
  • Real World Experience: Students can find work placements and internships during their course with top employers including banks, law firms and media companies based in London.

Bachelor of Arts (Honours) – Business Management (Finance)

3 years – 4 years Next intake: Sep 2027 Apply by 1 Sep 2027

Understanding contemporary corporate finance is the key to understanding modern business. Covering all areas of finance including investment analysis and capital management, students will have the tools to solve financial problems faced by business today.

This course is tailored towards ambitious students who want a management and finance degree, and they will learn in a live environment. Using data from the London Stock Exchange, students will form portfolios of listed companies and compare it against the market. Students will take on the challenge of a work placement, developing their theory and practical skills to an employable level, as well as developing their understanding of financial organisations.

During this BA in Business Management (Finance), students will develop key business skills to help them in a management or financial career. Focusing on topics such as resource management, leadership, business finance and analysis, students will have the ability to work effectively as part of a team. Presenting and communicating their ideas will give them confidence in a fast paced business environment, and they'll have specialist knowledge in business strategy and process.

Through this course students can become a student member of the Chartered Management Institute, leading to full membership upon graduation. They’ll also successfully train to get Bloomberg and DataStream certificates during their degree, a key part of the course.

Program Description

Environmental health is the branch of public health concerned with how people's living, working and leisure environments affect their health. It's a gripping subject to study and a fascinating field to work in. Whether students are helping improve the living conditions of society's poorest, or working with a company to improve health and safety for staff and customers, their work will have a huge, life-changing and often life-saving impact.

This course is highly work-focused, with a strong emphasis on practical laboratory work and real-life case studies and students will be able to take advantage of Middlesex University's close links within the industry.

The course is centred on UK law and practice, but is designed to be flexible and provide education and training that will be valuable internationally. Students will have access to a range of excellent facilities including a microbiology laboratory and pestology materials. They will be able to study specialist modules areas such as food safety, and health and safety which are particularly valued by both local authorities and the private sector.

Many of the teaching staff are chartered environmental health practitioners and under their expert guidance, previous students have done research into a wide variety of areas, including listeria in food, the effects of heavy smoking, skin lightening, water pollution and the attitudes of people living in high-rise buildings towards recycling.
