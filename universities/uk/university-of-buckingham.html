About University of Buckingham

The University of Buckingham combines academic excellence with independence in a supportive, student-focused environment. Founded on academic freedom, it nurtures a diverse international community, encouraging critical thinking through small-group tutorials instead of large lectures. Buckingham offers flexible pathways, including September and January starts, two-year undergraduate degrees, and 4.5-year Medicine programs, enabling faster graduation for postgraduate study or workforce entry. Since launching the UK’s first two-year degrees in 1983, it has refined its programs to provide the widest range of accelerated degrees in the country.

Ranked 1st for Lecturer and Teaching Quality and Student Support in Southeast England (WUSCAs 2025), Buckingham reflects a commitment to high-quality teaching and personalized care. Guided by a vision of academic freedom and a mission to inspire individuals through learning, research, and entrepreneurship, Buckingham equips students to become future leaders and innovators.

  • Two-Year Bachelor’s Degree: Innovative two-year programmes add an additional summer term, allowing students to complete the same teaching weeks as a traditional three-year degree without increased workload. Students can progress directly to a master’s programme, completing both degrees in three years.
  • Save Time and Money: The fast-tracked schedule enables students to graduate and enter the workforce up to a year earlier than at most universities, offering savings of around 20% compared to traditional courses.
  • Small Group Teaching: With smaller class sizes, students receive individual attention from personal tutors. Academics integrate their latest research into teaching, ensuring students graduate with current, industry-relevant knowledge. With about 2,700 students, the University fosters a close-knit environment.
  • Culture: Buckingham has a diverse community with students from over 100 nationalities, resembling a global village where cross-cultural connections thrive.
  • Values: Defined by independence and a distinctive ethos, Buckingham continues to uphold the principle of independent thinking established by its founders.
  • Location: Set in a picturesque riverside campus, the University is near a vibrant market town with easy access to shops, cafés, and excellent transport links, including Milton Keynes train station for fast connections to major cities. Students can join various clubs and societies catering to diverse interests.

Master of Business Administration (MBA)

1 year Next intake: Jan 2027 Apply by 1 Jan 2027

At University of Buckingham, the student-centred approach cultivates collaboration and real-world applications, empowering students to excel. They will Immerse themselves in an agile learning environment, honing teamwork, and transferable skills crucial for leadership. Accredited by the Chartered Management Institute (CMI), the Master of Business Administration (MBA) offers the opportunity to gain CMI’s professional qualification in Strategic Management and Leadership in Practice upon successful completion of the MBA. Join us to gain knowledge and to be inspired and empowered for leadership in the ever-evolving business landscape.

The program focuses on 13 main areas of learning, equipping students with skills needed for modern leadership in the dynamic globalized world of business:

  • Decision-making and Leadership
  • Strategic analysis
  • Digital Technology and Disruption
  • Sustainability and Ethics
  • Interpersonal and People Management
  • Negotiation and Culture Management
  • Marketing and Innovation
  • Financial and Managerial Accounting analysis
  • Personal and Critical Self-awareness
  • Operation and Supply Chain Management
  • Risk Management
  • Project Management
  • Research and Time Management.

Student life & support

Work while studying

International students can work part-time alongside their studies (subject to visa conditions).

Co-op & placements

Co-op options are limited — ask us about placement-friendly alternatives.

On-campus housing

Students typically arrange off-campus or homestay accommodation.

Accommodation options

Students need to plan their accommodations well in advance of their arrival as all accommodation options fill up quickly, well before the start of the semester.

On-Campus Residence Accommodations

On-Campus residence accommodations are provided per discretion by the school.

Off-Campus Accommodations

The local area has a variety of off-campus rental housing options including single homes, duplexes, apartments and rooms for rent. Students wishing to live off-campus need to research availability on their own and should arrive well before the start of term to do so.
