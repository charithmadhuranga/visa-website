Overview

About Bangor University International College

Bangor University International College (BUIC) is a specialist Embedded College offering Incorporated Programmes, where international students can begin their studies at Bangor University. BUIC delivers the first stages of the International Incorporated Bachelors Degree, International Year One, and the International Incorporated Masters. Whilst Oxford International holds responsibility for the academic provision, all quality assurance and maintenance of quality standards for all teaching at the Bangor University International College remains strictly with Bangor University.

Student's time at Bangor University International College is part of their degree. Following successful completion of Stage 1 BUIC, students will continue their studies directly with Bangor University. This means by attending the college, students can complete their degree in four years at the undergraduate level, or two years at postgraduate level.

Why Bangor University International College

  • Small Class Sizes: Smaller class sizes mean that students will receive ample individual support and attention from their tutors as they acquire knowledge and understanding of core subjects.
  • Diverse Academic Community: BUIC hosts students from all over the world, so students will have the opportunity to learn about different cultures, broaden their horizons, and make life-long friendships.
  • Location: Students can live in one of the most scenic locations in the UK as they study. Nestled between the mountains and the sea, Bangor is more than just beautiful: it's a playground for nature lovers.

Master's · Business & Management

1-Term International Incorporated Masters (IIM) – Transfer to Master of Arts – Business and Marketing

15 months Next intake: Jun 2027 Apply by 1 Jun 2027

The International Incorporated Masters programme are designed for students who do not qualify for direct entry to the masters programmes at Bangor University, this could be because of the grades achieved in their bachelor degree; their current qualifications are not equivalent to a United Kingdom Bachelor degree or because they have a different background.

The Pre-Masters or Stage 1 of the International Incorporated Masters (IIM) programme is delivered at Bangor University International College (BUIC) and focuses on equipping students with the academic English language skills they need to complete their postgraduate study at Bangor University.

After successfully completing the Pre-Masters Stage at Bangor University International College students will continue their IIM in Business and Management at Bangor Business School - or their IIM in Psychology at Bangor University's School of Psychology.

Student life & support

Work while studying

International students can work part-time alongside their studies (subject to visa conditions).

Co-op & placements

Offers co-op or work-placement options in selected programs.

On-campus housing

Students typically arrange off-campus or homestay accommodation.

Accommodation options

Students need to plan their accommodations well in advance of their arrival as all accommodation options fill up quickly, well before the start of the semester.

On-Campus Residence Accommodations

On-Campus residence accommodations are provided per discretion by the school.

Off-Campus Accommodations

The local area has a variety of off-campus rental housing options including single homes, duplexes, apartments and rooms for rent. Students wishing to live off-campus need to research availability on their own and should arrive well before the start of term to do so.

Homestay

There are a wide variety of homestay options available, and our partners do their best to match students and hosts according to their interests and preferences. Homestay hosts include single people, young couples with children and pets, and older couples. All homestay accommodations have been inspected, and all adults in the home have completed a required criminal reference check.
