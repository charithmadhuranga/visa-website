About Cardiff University

Founded in 1883, Cardiff University is established as one of Britain's leading research universities. Cardiff is a global, ambitious and innovative university with a bold and strategic vision located in a beautiful and thriving capital city.

They have a diverse student population with students coming from more than 100 countries and with a range of backgrounds. Academic staff are driven by creativity and curiosity and many are leaders in their fields, creating a stimulating environment for learning. They work in partnership with our students to create a stimulating and supportive environment in which all our students can thrive.

Why Cardiff University - Cathays Park

  • Research: Cardiff University excels in producing high quality, innovative research which translates into benefits both locally and worldwide. Their research expertise and facilities are used by industry and commercial partners, government bodies and other organisations internationally. Their research earns grants and investments totalling tens of millions of pounds and attracts top researchers from around the world. Cardiff University's research contracts have a total value of £500M.
  • Sought-After Graduates: 95% of Cardiff's graduates were in employment and/or further study, due to start a new job or course, or doing other activities, such as travelling. Cardiff University has 205,000 alumni in 202 countries.
  • Location: Cardiff University is made up of two large, well-located campuses, both located in Cardiff. The Cathays Park campus is where most of the Academic Schools are based. With wide tree-lined avenues and elegant Portland stone buildings, it's an inspiring place to study. Healthcare-related courses (excluding Pharmacy and Optometry) are based at Heath Park. It's just a mile from the city centre and next to 100 acres of parkland and playing fields.

Master's · Law & Criminology

Bar Training Course: (LLM)

1 year Next intake: Sep 2027 Apply by 1 Sep 2027

The LLM Bar Training Course offers an intensive, postgraduate-level program for individuals who aspire to become barristers and wish to pursue research at the Master's level. This course is typically taken after completing undergraduate studies and before engaging in work-based learning, known as pupillage.

The program equips students with the necessary skills and knowledge of procedure and evidence to meet the criteria set by the Bar Standards Board (BSB), as outlined in their Professional Statement for Barristers.

The primary objective of our program is to cultivate a professional and ethical approach to practicing as a barrister. Additionally, it provides students with a comprehensive understanding of the daily work and challenges faced by legal professionals in today's world.

Classes are generally scheduled from Monday to Thursday, allowing ample time for personal study. Advocacy and conferencing sessions are conducted intensively in two-hour intervals, with no more than six students per session. Students will also have the opportunity to pursue a specialized advocacy option.

Furthermore, students will be given the chance to undertake the BSB centrally set assessments in December, April, and August during their academic year of enrollment.

Upon successful completion of the program, students will be eligible to be called to the Bar of England and Wales, provided they fulfill the qualifying session requirement of their Inn of Court.

The MA News Journalism program offers individuals the opportunity to acquire the fundamental skills necessary for discovering, producing, and publishing stories using the most effective and relevant tools currently available. It is expected that students remain engaged with current events and demonstrate regular consumption of news through reading, watching, and listening. Master of Business Administration (MBA)

The Cardiff MBA program is delivered in immersive blocks that are specifically designed to stimulate the curiosity of the students, inform their process of inquiry, and strengthen the application of their learning. Each block of learning consists of a diverse range of activities, such as company visits, industry speakers, time-limited challenges, role-play, and simulations.

The students benefit from small group work and block teaching, which allows them to master specific interdisciplinary approaches before having the opportunity to develop a specialization through the accelerator project. The program concludes with a capstone module that brings together the various areas of expertise acquired throughout the MBA experience.

The diversity of the student cohort and academic staff is considered a valuable asset. Students have the chance to collaborate with their peers, academics, and practitioners in order to challenge their existing knowledge structures, assumptions, values, and skills. Similar to the workplace, students are required to complete assessment tasks individually and as part of a team, covering areas such as strategy, personnel, consultancy, innovation, and crisis.

This program structure provides students with the opportunity to customize their MBA experience through six challenge-focused modules, offering flexibility to align with their career aspirations.

Student life & support

Work while studying

International students can work part-time alongside their studies (subject to visa conditions).

Co-op & placements

Offers co-op or work-placement options in selected programs.

On-campus housing

Students typically arrange off-campus or homestay accommodation.

Accommodation options

Students need to plan their accommodations well in advance of their arrival as all accommodation options fill up quickly, well before the start of the semester.

On-Campus Residence Accommodations

On-Campus residence accommodations are provided per discretion by the school.

Off-Campus Accommodations

The local area has a variety of off-campus rental housing options including single homes, duplexes, apartments and rooms for rent. Students wishing to live off-campus need to research availability on their own and should arrive well before the start of term to do so.

Homestay

There are a wide variety of homestay options available, and our partners do their best to match students and hosts according to their interests and preferences. Homestay hosts include single people, young couples with children and pets, and older couples. All homestay accommodations have been inspected, and all adults in the home have completed a required criminal reference check.
