About University of Greenwich International College (UGIC)

University of Greenwich International College (UGIC) is an embedded college delivered on the main campus at University of Greenwich, in London. UGIC provides the first stages of the International Incorporated Bachelors and the International Incorporated Masters programs. During the pathway program, students are prepared for university-level studies by learning core subject knowledge while developing their academic English level and study skills. With experienced teachers and small class sizes, students will receive first-class teaching as well as the individual guidance needed to adjust to their new life as a UK university student. UGIC students enjoy full University of Greenwich student status, with access to all University facilities and services.

Following successful completion of a pathway program at UGIC, students can continue their studies directly at University of Greenwich. Established in 1890, University of Greenwich is situated on the banks of the River Thames in London. University of Greenwich is a modern institution with strong industry connections that emphasizes preparing students for careers in a rapidly-changing world. University of Greenwich is part of the University Alliance group and is silver rated in the Teaching Excellence Framework and its renowned research has been globally received and endorsed by nine Times Higher Education Awards and four Queen’s Anniversary Prizes for Higher & Further Education.

Why University of Greenwich International College?

  • Campus: The University of Greenwich's international community includes students from over 140 countries and UGIC is a supportive and welcoming home for international students to learn and make new friends.
  • Facilities: UGIC is based on one of the university's three campuses, where students have full access to facilities, including labs, studios, social and teaching spaces, sports and recreational facilities, and a high-tech library.
  • Location: London is the #1 city for students in the world, home to some of the world's top educational institutions, headquarters of international businesses and organizations, major tourist attractions, as well as entertainment, restaurants and bars, and shopping options that appeal to all tastes. UGIC is only 25 minutes from central London by train.
  • Small Class Sizes: Students are prepared for university studies in the best teaching environment: small classes led by experienced tutors who give each student the support they need!
  • Seamless Transition: Upon successful completion of their studies at UGIC, students can expect a direct and seamless transition onto their course at University of Greenwich.

MBA · Business & Management

1-Term Pathway – International Incorporated Masters (IIM) – Transfer to Master of Business Administration – MBA Global

27 months Next intake: Sep 2027 Apply by 1 Sep 2027

The MBA Global with placement programme is designed to enable students to enhance their knowledge of global business. It provides a comprehensive understanding of global issues and supports students to become effective global leaders. Learning on the programme will rely mostly on real-world industry scenarios. This will ensure that participants are equipped with the necessary skills and theoretical knowledge required to address contemporary business-related issues. The programme will explore a wide range of subject areas including:

  • dynamics of global business and practice
  • global entrepreneurship and innovation
  • ethics and global corporate citizenship
  • leading and managing change
  • international data analytics
  • financial resource management
  • simulated learning projects
  • business consultancy projects
  • global supply chains
  • strategic management and leadership
  • marketing and sales in the future economy
  • work-based project

After the first year of study, students on the programme will embark on a mandatory one-year industry placement with any organisation of their choice. The placement year will provide students with the opportunity to apply the knowledge gained in their first year of study in a practical context. Students will be able to establish the link between academic theories and professional practice and in the process sharpen their skills. Student can find out more about placements here.

The MBA Global with placement programme responds to the needs and expectations of employers by equipping students with the hard and soft skills required to thrive and take up higher management positions as global business leaders.

Student life & support

Work while studying

International students can work part-time alongside their studies (subject to visa conditions).

Co-op & placements

Offers co-op or work-placement options in selected programs.

On-campus housing

Students typically arrange off-campus or homestay accommodation.

Accommodation options

Students need to plan their accommodations well in advance of their arrival as all accommodation options fill up quickly, well before the start of the semester.

On-Campus Residence Accommodations

On-Campus residence accommodations are provided per discretion by the school.

Off-Campus Accommodations

The local area has a variety of off-campus rental housing options including single homes, duplexes, apartments and rooms for rent. Students wishing to live off-campus need to research availability on their own and should arrive well before the start of term to do so.
