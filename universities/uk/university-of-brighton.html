Overview

About University of Brighton

University of Brighton is a progressive, modern school in one of Britain's most exciting, lively cities. It is career-focused with excellent facilities and business connections and, at its heart, it is an inventive institution with a creative spirit. The university offers degrees in a variety of subjects and its main campus, Moulsecoomb, is home to programs in applied sciences, architecture, business, law, and engineering. Over the last few years, this inspiring campus has undergone a significant transformation, and now features new residence halls and learning facilities, fitness and student union facilities, numerous green spaces, and an accessible pedestrian bridge that links both sides of campus.

A big part of the academic culture at University of Brighton is about unlocking each student's potential by empowering them to do something they really love and succeed at it. Degrees at Brighton are generally quite flexible, and students are often can choose optional modules, work placements and volunteering opportunities that work to their strengths.

Why University of Brighton?

  • Campus: Brighton's community is made up of students and staff from over 150 countries. There are more than 100 societies for students to expand their interests, meet like-minded people, and do something they love.
  • Facilities: The university has 4 campuses, all with superb facilities, including sports halls, a swimming pool, dance studios, a newsroom, Psychology labs, world-class Design Archives, and more!
  • Location: Brighton is a student-friendly, artistic, and bustling city with famous beaches and a diverse and inclusive community. It has an abundance of stores, green spaces, restaurants, bars, and entertainment options, and is a short train ride from London.
  • Placement Opportunities: An important part of many programs at Brighton is the opportunity for students to gain work or volunteer experience in a professional environment.
  • Professional Accreditation: The majority of students study programs that are accredited by professional and statutory bodies. Successful completion of a professionally accredited course indicates that the student has reached the occupational standard required to practice that profession.

Bachelor's · Law & Criminology

Bachelor of Laws (LLB) – Law

3 years Next intake: Sep 2027 Apply by 1 Sep 2027

Program Description

The qualifying Law LLB(Hons) degree combines academic study with opportunities to experience law in practice, preparing students for a career as a legal professional.

Taken over three years, or four years with an optional salaried placement, core learning provides practical skills in presentation, negotiation, oration, mooting and advocacy, and a thorough understanding of the English legal system.

This degree is recognised by the Solicitors Regulation (SRA) Authority and the Bar Standards Board. It is a qualifying law degree, which means when students graduate they will have completed the academic stage of training for entry into the legal profession.

The SRA are introducing the new Solicitors Qualification Examination (SQE), which will replace the existing Legal Practice Course in autumn 2021.

MBA · Business & Management

Global Master of Business Administration (MBA)

1 year Next intake: Jan 2027 Apply by 12 Dec 2026

Business leaders today are facing unprecedented challenges, including economic uncertainty, technological change and shifting international markets. The Global MBA prepares students to address these challenges and succeed in an increasingly interconnected world.

Designed for ambitious professionals and graduates seeking to accelerate their careers, the MBA focuses on global challenges, digital transformation and responsible leadership. Students explore how organisations operate across international markets, examine the impact of emerging technologies such as AI and blockchain, and learn how businesses can respond to ethical and economic challenges on a global scale.

Through practical learning, expert teaching from international lecturers and a live consultancy project, students develop the confidence and skills needed to lead organisations through complexity and change. The Global MBA provides a flexible and practical route to developing advanced business and leadership skills.

Students study a series of 30-credit modules that build knowledge and skills across key areas of management before applying their learning to a live consultancy project.

Instead of completing a traditional dissertation, students work on a real business challenge for an organisation. This approach enables students to develop practical, evidence-based solutions in a professional setting rather than focusing solely on a theoretical exercise. Students gain experience in applying their thinking to genuine deadlines and graduate with a real-life example of the value they can bring to an organisation.

The forward-thinking Global MBA focuses on real-world business challenges, embedding international perspectives and AI across the modules to reflect the realities of modern business.

Key areas of study include

  • Core business foundations: Develops a strong grounding in finance, marketing, operations, people and strategy.
  • AI and digital transformation: Explores how digital technologies are reshaping industries and decision-making and how they can drive smarter business outcomes.
  • Global perspective: Develops an international mindset while examining how culture, geopolitics and economics influence global business.
  • Leadership in practice: Strengthens the ability to lead teams, manage complex situations and communicate effectively.
  • Entrepreneurial thinking: Develops innovative and agile approaches to problem-solving.
  • Personal development: Builds confidence and resilience while supporting professional development and clearer career direction.
  • Ethical business: Develops responsible leadership skills through an understanding of the ethical, social and sustainability challenges facing businesses today.
