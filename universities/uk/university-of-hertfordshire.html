Overview

About University of Hertfordshire

The University of Hertfordshire is the UK's leading business-facing university and an exemplar in the sector. It is innovative and enterprising and challenges individuals and organizations to excel. The University of Hertfordshire is one of the region's largest employers with over 2,700 staff and a turnover of more than £238 million. With a student community of over 25,300 including more than 2,800 international students from 100 different countries, the University has a global network of over 210,000 alumni. It continues to rise up the national rankings.

Why the University of Hertfordshire

  • High-Quality Education: The University is of the highest quality in the UK as it has been awarded Gold in the Teaching Excellence and Student Outcomes Framework. Over 96.5% of Herts students are employed within the first six months of graduating.
  • Physical Well-Being: Active Students is an excellent program designed to help students of all backgrounds stay fit and healthy on campus. There is a wide variety of classes to choose from, including Pilates, Weight Training, Smash HIIT, and so much more.
  • Convenient Campus and Facilities: Herts students are surrounded by culture and entertainment to suit all tastes and interests! Some of these include: Access to great music and theatre communities, restaurants on and off-campus, museums and galleries, live sporting events, and outside spaces and parks on and off-campus.

Bachelor of Arts (Honours) – Accounting

3 years – 4 years Next intake: Sep 2027 Apply by 1 Aug 2027

Accounting is a vital and fascinating part of today’s global economy. Studying it at Hertfordshire brings students face to face with current trends, practices and concepts – not just within accountancy itself, but in the wider world of business.

This BA (Hons) Accounting degree is a highly practical, relevant course. It gives students the sought-after skills, latest academic theory and real-world experience they need to become a confident, knowledgeable, forward-thinking graduate with exceptional career prospects.

This course explores the role of accountancy in businesses of all sizes and forms, opening students' eyes to the diverse range of potential career paths ahead. It’s a multi-disciplinary, globally focused accounting degree, developed with innovative industry-leading companies and respected professional bodies.

At Hertfordshire, students will be taught by their team of leading academics, who together have created a supportive, inspiring learning environment. They’re joined by visiting industry specialists who bring with them years of real-world accounting experience and a real enthusiasm for sharing their insights with students.

This degree course offers exemptions from a range of accounting bodies including the Chartered Institute of Management Accountants (CIMA), Association of Chartered Certified Accountants (ACCA) and Institute of Chartered Accountants in England and Wales (ICAEW).

Between the second and third year, students can do an entire year abroad at one of the partner universities based in America, Europe, Asia, Africa and Australia. That means plenty of places for learners to go and explore. Students can also do a year’s work placement abroad or in the UK.

Bachelor of Arts (Honours) – Accounting and Finance

3 years – 4 years Next intake: Sep 2027 Apply by 1 Aug 2027

For those who are curious about financial matters within companies or want to kick-start their career with professional accreditations before they've even left university, then BA (Hons) Accounting and Finance is the course for them.

This degree course offers exemptions from a range of accounting bodies including the Chartered Institute of Management Accountants (CIMA), Association of Chartered Certified Accountants (ACCA) and Institute of Chartered Accountants in England and Wales (ICAEW).

In the  first year, students will learn the principles of accounting and the basic mix of knowledge and skills they would require for a successful career in accounting. They'll explore financial information within companies and corporations and learn to analyze it. There is a dedicated module focusing on professional skills, such as communication and IT-literacy. Learners will investigate the different sides of business and where their role as an accountant sits within these.

In the second year, students' accounting knowledge and skill will be developed and so will their employability. They’ll focus on financial reporting, management accounting and financial management. They’ll also learn methods of enquiry and problem solving to ensure they are ready to go out into the real world. This will give them an advantage when they start working.

In the final year, students will build on the foundation they have established in their first and second years but will also challenge the knowledge they have gained in those previous years so that they can influence change in their future career. Students will take modules on advanced financial reporting and management accounting. They’ll also have the chance to pick from options such as research methods, taxation, Islamic finance and banking or information systems.

Between the second and third year, students can do an entire year abroad at one of the partner universities based in America, Europe, Asia, Africa and Australia. That means plenty of places for learners to go and explore. Students can also do a year’s work placement abroad or in the UK.

3 years – 4 years Next intake: Sep 2027 Apply by 1 Aug 2027

In today’s fast-paced media world, people with the ability to write, design and harness new technologies are in high demand.

Studying the BA Media and Communications degree will equip students with a portfolio of practical skills as well as a theoretical and critical understanding of Media. This industry-focused approach, combining theory and practice, will help build their confidence, give them the communication and technological skills required for the industry and help them plan their career.

Students will learn how to design their own app proposal, build and edit brilliant websites and create a hard-hitting ad campaign, including PR. They’ll also learn what makes a good news story or feature, and how to write one. Using industry standard software and equipment, professionally-experienced academics and researchers will help students develop skills across divergent platforms and for different audiences.

These hands-on skills will be underpinned by an understanding of the global media industry, and the legal and ethical contexts of students' work. In the first year students will explore Global Media and Society, looking at who owns the news, at the influence of the BBC and commercial entities such as Sky and Murdoch’s News Corporation, and consider issues such as fake news.

Other core modules (courses) include introductions to Media Communications and Visual Communications – how to use text and graphics to reach key audiences – while students will gain technical skills through Photoshop or animation projects.

In the second year students will learn key research methods and skills including how to conduct interviews and run focus groups, and how to pitch to different client groups. Students will explore radio journalism and social media, while a module on App Design will help them understand the commercial potential for their ideas.

For students that are into music, film, food or travel, they'll have opportunities to create magazine layouts and pdfs for online publications on whatever subject interests them. A video option will give them the chance to create a brand for a product or service.

In the final year students will develop and refine their ideas and skills further. Whether they're keen to work in web design, social media, customer relations, PR or advertising, the skills they’ve gained will prepare them to be tomorrow’s creative entrepreneurs, confident in using different platforms to get ahead in their chosen career.

MBA · Business & Management

Master of Business Administration (Extended Professional Practice)

2 years Next intake: Sep 2027 Apply by 1 Aug 2027

The Master of Business Administration with Extended Professional Practice is designed to provide students with a critical understanding of core business and management principles, including people management, marketing, and financial proficiency. The programme supports the development of strategic leadership capabilities and the ability to evaluate organisational positioning, making it suitable for individuals interested in leadership roles or entrepreneurial pathways, regardless of prior experience.

The course delivers a comprehensive and immersive learning experience, combining academic study with practical application. In addition to theoretical knowledge, students engage with real-world business contexts, supported by a 30-week postgraduate professional placement. Students who meet the academic requirements are responsible for securing their own placement, with structured guidance and support provided by the Future Success and Employability Team.

The two-year structure is divided into academic and practical components. The first year focuses on core academic modules, while the second year emphasises the application of knowledge through a professional placement, alongside the development of a reflective portfolio. The final semester also includes modules in Strategic Management Research and Financial Principles and Practice.

Students enrolled on the programme have access to a range of university resources, including the virtual learning environment, StudyNet, which offers access to course materials, peer collaboration, and an extensive collection of e-books and e-journals.

The programme also offers participation in the Young Enterprise Start-Up Programme, delivered in partnership with a leading UK enterprise education charity. Through this initiative, students collaborate in teams to establish and manage real businesses, taking on director-level responsibilities and participating in national and European competitions. This experience is designed to enhance practical skills in innovation, leadership, and entrepreneurship.

Teaching is delivered by research-active academic staff with extensive professional experience. Many are engaged in consultancy and advisory roles with major organisations, including FTSE-listed companies, global health institutions, and innovation-focused enterprises, ensuring that teaching is informed by current industry practice.

Master of Business Administration (MBA)

1 year Next intake: Jan 2027 Apply by 1 Dec 2026

Studying an MBA can transform the way students think and the way they work, change the way others see them and open up new leadership and management opportunities. This full-time MBA enables students to develop the critical and reflective strategic leadership skills they need to succeed in an uncertain world, challenging their assumptions, cultivating flexible thinking, effective decision making and responsible management.

Hertfordshire MBA students will be supported by first-class facilities, including the virtual learning environment, StudyNet, which provides access to other students, lecture notes and over 100,000 e-books and e-journals. While this supports their learning, they’ll be encouraged to develop and explore their business ideas and apply it directly to their career aspirations.

With an MBA, students can take a life-changing professional journey and expand their mind, skills, career potential and professional network. They can challenge their assumptions and gain a critical, reflexive approach to strategic leadership and management.
