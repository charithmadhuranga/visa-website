About Kaplan International College London: City, University of London

At Kaplan International College (KIC) London - City, University of London students can build their future by studying in the UK, with a pathway course. Situated in the world’s top academic city (QS Best Student Cities 2019), the College will prepare students for a university degree, and for a successful career. Students are able to study at one of the most respected international colleges in London, and progress to their chosen degree at City, University of London.

City is a member institution of the University of London, one of the oldest and largest universities in the UK. Dedicated to providing an outstanding academic experience and award-winning support, City equips students with the tools they need to build a thriving and sustainable career. Located in the heart of London’s financial district, City is home to five specialist ‘Schools’ including The Business School (formerly Cass) – one of Europe’s leading business schools with a world-class reputation. Join an international community, with students coming from more than 160 different countries to study at City. From the diverse faith team to the Student Counselling and Mental Health service, there are a range of support facilities on offer to help all members of the City community.

Why Kaplan International College London: City, University of London

  • Dedicated Support: From extending visas to dealing with homesickness, College staff will be happy to help with any issues that students may face. Student’s will also benefit from frequent events arranged by the College so they can meet new friends and make the most of their time in London
  • Convenient Accommodation: Students are able to stay in comfortable, modern accommodation that is close to classes. Students will enjoy shared social spaces and quiet study areas, as well as their own private bedroom.
  • City Living: Just a short walk away from London Bridge station, KIC London is ideally located for exploring the city and all its attractions, from Tower Bridge, Big Ben and the Houses of Parliament to London’s many shops, theatres, and cafes and restaurants.
  • Academic Excellence: For 125 years, the University has combined exceptional education with leading research and enterprise. The top-ranked Business School (formerly Cass), a triple-accredited institution positioned among the global elite of Business schools’ Central London location.

Bachelor of Law (Honours) - Law

This LLB Law degree gives students a foundation in the essential skills and knowledge needed to be successful in law and related fields.

It gives students a range of transferable skills which will help if they want to pursue a career path both within and outside law. Students will leave confident in their abilities and equipped with the skills demanded by employers. Students will be trained to deal with challenging situations in the law or law related workplace and demonstrate a high degree of professionalism in their work environment.

The knowledge and intellectual competencies students gain will enable them to develop as a mature, professional individual. This degree is also highly internationalised in its content, delivery and assessment strategies. City aims to produce highly qualified individuals with a global perspective on the law.

Student life & support

Work while studying

International students can work part-time alongside their studies (subject to visa conditions).

Co-op & placements

Co-op options are limited — ask us about placement-friendly alternatives.

On-campus housing

Students typically arrange off-campus or homestay accommodation.

Accommodation options

Students need to plan their accommodations well in advance of their arrival as all accommodation options fill up quickly, well before the start of the semester.

On-Campus Residence Accommodations

On-Campus residence accommodations are provided per discretion by the school.

Off-Campus Accommodations

The local area has a variety of off-campus rental housing options including single homes, duplexes, apartments and rooms for rent. Students wishing to live off-campus need to research availability on their own and should arrive well before the start of term to do so.

Homestay

There are a wide variety of homestay options available, and our partners do their best to match students and hosts according to their interests and preferences. Homestay hosts include single people, young couples with children and pets, and older couples. All homestay accommodations have been inspected, and all adults in the home have completed a required criminal reference check.
