About Richmond American University London

Established in 1972 and situated in Chiswick Park, Richmond American University London offers its students the chance to graduate with not only a UK accredited degree but also a US degree. Students who attend Richmond become part of a vibrant and diverse international student community, where students are encouraged and supported to flourish in a mix of diverse cultures, backgrounds and perspectives.

Richmond American University London is a leading liberal arts university committed to developing student's passion for learning. Students are fully supported throughout their programme so they engage, work with and participate in a vibrant academic community. A liberal arts education at Richmond provides more diversity, choice and freedom as well as fostering skills like personal and social development, providing students with self-confidence, self-understanding and a sense of social responsibility.

Why Richmond American University London?

  • Dual Degree: Dually-accredited UK and US Undergraduate and Master’s Degrees. Richmond teaches an American style Liberal Arts curriculum and all undergraduate degree programmes take four years.
  • Brand New Home: Starting January 2023, all students will be based in a new campus building within the innovative and award-winning Chiswick Park in west London. The state of the art building, set in a beautiful parkland location, has been designed with students in mind.
  • Academically-Accredited Work Placements: Richmond offers both undergraduate and postgraduate internships opportunities to provide students with exposure to the demands of specific jobs and allows students to learn from professionals in their chosen field.
  • Unity in Diversity: Richmond American University London is committed to living by the motto of “Unity in Diversity”. Richmond aspires to understand, accept and celebrate differences.

Bachelor's · Law & Criminology

Bachelor of Arts (Honours) – Criminology

4 years Next intake: Jan 2027 Apply by 1 Jan 2027

Bachelor of Arts (Honours) in Criminology blends the disciplines of criminology, psychology, international law, and political science and international relations with expertise in research methods, preparing students for a wide range of careers in criminal justice, police and law enforcement, border protection, security, and community safety. Other careers include employment in international organizations, business, finance, government institutions, agencies, consultancies, advocacy groups, and the media.

Students specialize in five areas in the study of Criminology and its connected fields (plus Combined Studies):

  • Core theories and practices of Criminology: theories of crime, criminality and victimhood (and critical responses to these); finding and interpreting data concerning crime; institutions of the criminal justice system (including law enforcement agencies, the judicial system, corrections and rehabilitation, and alternative approaches); types of crime; crime prevention strategies.
  • Key contemporary issues in Criminology: social structure and crime; deviant behaviour and social norms; criminology and the changing role of the media; organized crime; international criminal activities; terrorism/extremism and state responses to these; criminology in popular culture.
  • Psychology: Clinical psychology is the broad field of psychology to which forensic psychology belongs. Students study psychology throughout the programme, from Introduction to Psychology at level 4, to Human Science Research at level 5, to Clinical Psychology at level 6. This provides real substance to Criminology at Richmond as a genuinely interdisciplinary programme.
  • Public Policy: Ensuring students understand the global legal and political contexts for crime, as well as the issues and problems policy-makers and political processes face in addressing the dynamics of crime and criminal behaviour which may be transnational in character. Includes International Law and Human Rights at level 5 and Policy-Making in a Globalized World at level 6. Students engage in interdisciplinary cognate fields to Criminology (International Relations and Political Science) while addressing cutting edge issues on the discipline such as human rights violations.
  • Research Methods: Quantitative and Qualitative approaches and research ethics. There is strong emphasis on research methods in the programme. All students take probability and statistics as a core maths requirements, and study qualitative research methods both in the core Research Methods in Criminology course and as embedded in the other Criminology courses, as well as taking Human Science Research as part of the Psychology stream. Research ethics are addressed at various points in the programme, including in the Service-Learning course (Global Citizenship and Migration) and the final Senior Project in Criminology.
  • Combined Studies: students take a range of courses as part of the US ‘Liberal Arts’ component of the programme, including from arts and humanities, the sciences, and a service-learning course, as well as a further course in either International Relations or Psychology. There is also space in the degree structure for students to take a Minor in a range of other subject areas.

Bachelor's · Business & Management

Bachelor of Arts (Honours) – Marketing

3 years Next intake: Jan 2027 Apply by 1 Jan 2027

The BA (Hons) Marketing programme at Richmond American University London prepares students to identify market opportunities, anticipate trends, and develop digital marketing strategies that engage audiences and support business success. The programme equips students with the knowledge and skills required to influence audiences across multiple channels and make a meaningful impact in the marketplace.

In the first year, students build a strong foundation in business and marketing fundamentals while developing critical thinking skills and examining the impact of significant events such as Brexit and the Covid-19 pandemic. In subsequent years, they deepen their understanding of digital marketing by exploring customer journey stages, engagement strategies, website traffic generation, search engine optimisation, paid advertising, social media, and email marketing.

During the third and fourth years, students can tailor their studies through elective subjects such as data science, advanced computer applications in business, coding, and web design. The flexible Liberal Arts structure also allows them to select complementary and career-enhancing courses in areas including public relations, media and advertising, international business, finance, investment, accounting, economics, communication, and computer programming.

The programme develops a comprehensive understanding of business, placing marketing and digital technologies within their wider organisational context. Students learn to use digital marketing tools to design and deliver effective strategies throughout the complete campaign lifecycle.

Students examine successful global marketing practices and explore how they contribute to competitive advantage and organisational success. They also learn how to position and promote products and services, from initial concept through to launch, by analysing markets and engaging effectively with target audiences.

Contemporary debates surrounding marketing ethics are explored alongside a range of ethical approaches to digital marketing. This enables students to consider the wider social and professional responsibilities associated with marketing practice.

Practical learning opportunities include working on live marketing briefs, allowing students to apply their knowledge to genuine business challenges. Guest speakers from industry provide additional insights into the digital marketing sector and the skills required for professional success.

Studying in London provides access to a global centre for marketing, advertising, and digital communication, as well as potential internship opportunities. Following graduation, students may be eligible to apply for four exemptions from the Chartered Institute of Management Accountants and gain accreditation from the Chartered Institute of Marketing.

Master's · Science & Environment

Master of Science – Project Management for Sustainability

1.5 years Next intake: Sep 2027 Apply by 1 Sep 2027

MSc Project Management for Sustainability is a pioneering programme designed for the new era of sustainable development in businesses, charities and NGOs (non-governmental organisations). This master’s programme seamlessly combines the principles of sustainability with the rigour of project management, preparing graduates to lead with a unique blend of environmental awareness and practical management skills.

Student will also be offered the opportunity to gain an internationally recognised industry qualification, PRINCE2.7® Foundation and PRINCE2.7® Practitioner.

PRINCE2 is a process-based method for effective project management, and will give students the fundamental skills they need to become a successful project manager. It stands for PRojects IN Controlled Environments, and is used and recognised all over the world.

On this MSc programme student will explore the principles and practice of project management and discover the influence of successful project management on any organisation, with a particular emphasis on improving organisational sustainability.

With the growing recognition of sustainability as a core organisation practice, there’s no better time to study a master’s project management degree with sustainability at its heart.

Student life & support

Work while studying

International students can work part-time alongside their studies (subject to visa conditions).

Co-op & placements

Offers co-op or work-placement options in selected programs.

On-campus housing

Students typically arrange off-campus or homestay accommodation.

Accommodation options

Students need to plan their accommodations well in advance of their arrival as all accommodation options fill up quickly, well before the start of the semester.

On-Campus Residence Accommodations

On-Campus residence accommodations are provided per discretion by the school.

Off-Campus Accommodations

The local area has a variety of off-campus rental housing options including single homes, duplexes, apartments and rooms for rent. Students wishing to live off-campus need to research availability on their own and should arrive well before the start of term to do so.

Homestay

There are a wide variety of homestay options available, and our partners do their best to match students and hosts according to their interests and preferences. Homestay hosts include single people, young couples with children and pets, and older couples. All homestay accommodations have been inspected, and all adults in the home have completed a required criminal reference check.
