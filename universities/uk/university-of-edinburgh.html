Overview

About University of Edinburgh

Founded by the city of Edinburgh in 1583, the University of Edinburgh was the first true civic university in the UK and one of the four ancient universities of Scotland. Join some of the best students from around the globe at one of the world's top universities. Students will invest in an education that will help them build a successful future.

Join the University of Edinburgh's 13,000-strong international student community. The University of Edinburgh's flexible programmes, world-class facilities and award-winning support services combine to ensure students' university experience is exceptional and unique. The University works to support students at all stages offering welfare support, practical advice and events.

Why The University of Edinburgh

  • Location: The University is at the heart of the city of Edinburgh. Scotland’s inspiring capital is one of Europe’s great cultural hubs and a stimulating place to study. Edinburgh's dynamic cultural scene boasts the largest collection of historic buildings, museums, art galleries and theaters of any city in Scotland.
  • Clubs and Societies: The clubs and societies will help students develop their interests, meet like-minded people, indulge a hobby or simply socialize. The University of Edinburgh's host an extensive range of clubs and societies here at Edinburgh. With more than 290 to choose from, students are bound to find one that’s right for them.
  • Facilities and Services: At Edinburgh, students will get generous, flexible access to a state-of-the-art library, IT resources and study spaces, helping them fit study into their busy life. Students will have access to personal support services for all of their concerns, academic or otherwise.
  • Sport and Exercise: Edinburgh is one of the UK’s leading sports universities. Whether students are recreational gym-users or performance athletes going for gold, Edinburgh's world-class sports facilities, service and support can cater for students. The University of Edinburgh has been consistently ranked in the top 5 for sport in British Universities and Colleges Sport (BUCS) table and offer a wide range of world-class sport, fitness and performance facilities.

Master's · Computing & IT

Master of Science – Data Science

1 year Next intake: Sep 2027 Apply by 31 Mar 2027

Data science is the study of the computational principles, methods, and systems for extracting and structuring knowledge from data; and the application and use of those principles. Large data sets are now generated by almost every activity in science, society, and commerce - ranging from molecular biology to social media, from sustainable energy to health care.

As an Master of Science Data Science student students will explore how to efficiently find patterns in these vast streams of data. Many research areas have tackled parts of this problem. Machine learning focuses on finding patterns and making predictions from data; ideas from algorithms and databases are required to build systems that scale to big data streams; and separate research areas have grown around different types of unstructured data such as text, images, sensor data, video, and speech.

MBA · Business & Management

Master of Business Administration (MBA)

1 year Next intake: Sep 2028 Apply by 1 Sep 2028

The full-time MBA is an intensive program designed for students with a minimum of three years' managerial experience who wish to progress to more senior and leadership roles in business. The program immerses students in a world of academic thought, current business practice and applied projects.

Studied over 12 months, this is a taught program delivered by world class faculty complemented by guest business practitioners.

Successful businesses in the future will be those that can confidently and capably steer a path through a world characterized by intense competition, rapid technological development, economic turbulence and increasing resource insecurity.

Businesses need leaders who can think and act strategically in this volatile environment, managing the risk and challenges involved, but also spotting and seizing opportunities for efficiency, innovation and new business models. Businesses also need leaders who will act responsibly in delivering value to all stakeholders.

The University of Edinburgh program enhances students career potential by giving them a clear focus on the role of the highly sought strategic leader and helps them to progress with confidence. In order to achieve this university's MBA is built on three pillars:

Student life & support

Work while studying

International students can work part-time alongside their studies (subject to visa conditions).

Co-op & placements

Offers co-op or work-placement options in selected programs.

On-campus housing

Students typically arrange off-campus or homestay accommodation.

Accommodation options

Students need to plan their accommodations well in advance of their arrival as all accommodation options fill up quickly, well before the start of the semester.

On-Campus Residence Accommodations

On-Campus residence accommodations are provided per discretion by the school.

Off-Campus Accommodations

The local area has a variety of off-campus rental housing options including single homes, duplexes, apartments and rooms for rent. Students wishing to live off-campus need to research availability on their own and should arrive well before the start of term to do so.

Homestay

There are a wide variety of homestay options available, and our partners do their best to match students and hosts according to their interests and preferences. Homestay hosts include single people, young couples with children and pets, and older couples. All homestay accommodations have been inspected, and all adults in the home have completed a required criminal reference check.
