About Bath Spa University

Bath Spa University is a UK-based institution renowned for its emphasis on creativity, culture, and enterprise. It offers a diverse range of programs across arts, sciences, education, social sciences, and business, aiming to equip students with the skills and support needed to pursue their passions .

Established in 1852 as the Bath School of Art, the university has a rich heritage in the arts. Notably, artists such as Walter Sickert and Howard Hodgkin have been associated with the institution. In the post-war era, it relocated to Corsham Court and became a significant player in the English Modernist movement .

Why Bath Spa University

  • International Engagement: The university is committed to fostering global connections through its International Strategy, which includes initiatives like GALA, Go Global, and European Projects. These programs aim to enhance teaching and research by embedding international perspectives across all activities .
  • Student Support and Wellbeing: Bath Spa University prioritizes student wellbeing, offering services that address mental health, disability support, and financial guidance. The Student Wellbeing Services provide resources to help students navigate university life successfully .
  • Sustainability and Values: The university embraces a strong environmental ethos, with students and staff actively contributing to high environmental standards. Their commitment to sustainability is evident through various initiatives and a dedicated climate pledge .

Accommodation options

Bath Spa University offers a range of accommodation options to suit different student needs, including on-campus residences at Newton Park and city-based halls like Charlton Court, Waterside Court, and Twerton Mill. These accommodations feature en-suite rooms, studios, and shared living spaces, with easy access to university campuses and local amenities. The university also provides tailored support for students with specific requirements through its Priority Halls scheme, ensuring accessible and inclusive living environments. For those preferring private housing, Bath Spa offers guidance and tools like the Student Pad platform and community groups to help students find suitable off-campus accommodation.

For more details kindly refer to the website :- https://www.bathspa.ac.uk/student-life/accommodation/
