Overview

About Brunel University London Pathway College

Brunel University London Pathway College is designed to meet the needs of students by offering world-class pathway programs leading to undergraduate and postgraduate degrees at Brunel University London. At undergraduate level, this pathway college offers University Foundation and First year University Studies for candidates who do not meet the direct entry criteria or hold high school qualifications that are not recognized. At postgraduate level, Brunel University London Pathway College provides the highly acclaimed Pre-Master's program leading to an excellent range of Brunel University London Master programs in business and management, computing, engineering & design and arts.

Brunel University London Pathway College is located inside Brunel University London Campus, so students will experience university student life from day one. and they will have access to all of university facilities. Brunel University London Pathway College is there to support students for their future academic and professional careers. Brunel is a single site university where students can take part in a diverse range of clubs and societies for an action-packed social life. Everything is within a 10-minute walk - whether students are going to a class or for a coffee, whether they want a place to eat or a space to meet, it's all close by!

Why Brunel University London Pathway College?

  • Facilities and Safety: Located inside Brunel University London. Students are offered a safe, friendly and positive environment where they benefit from high-quality teaching, recreation and sporting facilities.
  • Location: Campus is well-connected. Just a short walk away, the town of Uxbridge offers students everything they need to shop, entertain and socialize, with central London just a tube ride away.
  • Employability: Students can develop a wide range of employability skills and improve their career prospects, with the help of the University's Professional Development service. Brunel’s careers service is rated the 5th best careers service in the country (Student Hut Survey, 2019).
  • Pathways: All pathway programs offer smaller class sizes for greater individual attention, of up to 30 students per class.
  • Placements: Over 95% of Brunel University London undergraduate courses offer a placement, giving students the opportunity to gain invaluable work experience.

At the postgraduate level, LBIC provides the highly acclaimed Pre-Master's programme leading to an excellent range of Brunel University London Master programmes in business and management, computing, engineering & design and arts. Accounting data and analysis lies at the very heart of good management. It provides vital information for investors and other external parties, and brings clarity to major decision-making in business management. If students have a business-related degree and want to develop their understanding of accounting and management in a business context, the Accounting and Business Management MSc is for them.

This integrated programme will develop theoretical knowledge and practical skills, giving students a robust foundation of knowledge to build upon. Students will acquire technical accounting skills, understand the ins and outs of financial reporting, and learn about quantitative and qualitative research methods.

Understanding the role of stakeholders and the influence of regulatory frameworks on accounting and management, both domestically and internationally will give students the edge in the global market.

The Accounting and Business Management MSc is accredited by ACCA, CIMA, ICAEW and AIA providing students with a range of exemptions that stop them from having to study the same topics that they have already been taught.

International Business MSc

International business is an exciting and rapidly evolving space as politics and economics shift, and societies and cultures evolve. In this course, students will gain a comprehensive understanding of the processes of globalisation in the 21st century and the implications that these ongoing processes have for today’s business women and men – and indeed, for tomorrow’s leaders.

The International Business MSc is designed for those seeking to operate within an international business setting or within companies and jobs where an understanding of international business is an asset. The course will help students to develop a sound understanding of international business contexts and to make integrative links across various disciplines.

At Brunel University London, students will explore the impact of internationalisation on factors such as the management of resources and operations, the financing of business enterprise, and the management and development of people. Aspects of the course will focus on emerging markets. It will also focus on the development of appropriate business policies and strategies to help put learning into practice.

Students will receive training in research methods with particular application to the empirical study of international management situations, issues and process.

Master's · Arts, Media & Design

Pre-Masters in Environmental Sciences – Continue to Master of Science – Sustainability, Entrepreneurship and Design (with Professional Development)

16 months – 20 months Next intake: May 2027 Apply by 1 May 2027

At the postgraduate level, LBIC provides the highly acclaimed Pre-Master's programme leading to an excellent range of Brunel University London Master programmes in business and management, computing, engineering & design and arts. After successfully completing the programme students will advance to their chosen progression degree.

The results of unsustainable human development in the 21st century are evident as climate change, species extinction, pollution, poverty and inequality become inescapable global realities. The next big challenge for society is to transform our economy to incorporate the principles of sustainability. This presents an exciting opportunity to rethink, redesign and rebuild a positive future for business practice.

The global market for environmental products and services is projected to double from USD 1,370 billion per year to USD 2,740 billion by 2020. Half of this market is in energy efficiency and the balance in sustainable transport, water supply, sanitation and waste management. 2.3 million people have in recent years found new jobs in the renewable energy sector alone. Employment in alternative energies may rise to 2.1 million in wind and 6.3 million in solar power by 2030.

This course will provide students with the skills across the disciplines of science, engineering design and business to be a part of this market and to become leaders and innovators in the context of environmental awareness.

The Sustainability, Entrepreneurship and Design MSc (with Professional Development) is a 21 month programme – 12 months of study at Brunel, followed by a short break while students await their dissertation grade, to allow them to finalise their placement. Once students have passed their dissertation, they'll complete a six month professional development phase. There is an option to complete the Sustainability, Entrepreneurship and Design MSc in 12 months without the professional development phase but Brunel encourages students to consider the professional development option to achieve the maximum from the programme and to enhance their future career prospects.

Business Administration MBA

The General Management MBA specialisation can help students become a more accomplished manager all-round. From finance to international strategy, harnessing technologies and marketing, this course will help students with senior management decision-making and solving business issues. Brunel University London supports their students in identifying their strengths and weaknesses and working with them to help them progress.

The Career Development Programme is an integral part of professional development while students are studying their MBA. Landing a top job after completing their MBA is, of course, a key objective, and the Career Development Programme is designed to provide the tools to help students achieve their ambitions. The programme will help students define their career goals and create a personalised career action plan.

Students will have access to one-to-one careers coaching to help students understand what makes them stand, out whether it’s their particular management experience and skills or specialist knowledge. Brunel University London will also help students with their CV, LinkedIn profile, and a professional portfolio as well as providing support in building a career plan, job-hunting, writing applications that get noticed, and brushing up on interview techniques.

As part of the MBA course, Brunel provides an innovative series of Leadership Masterclasses. Sessions will help students with effective ways to present their ideas, the best methods for managing change, negotiating better and resolving conflict, as well as giving students deeper insight into cross cultural issues and corporate responsibility.

The Brunel Business School has links to many leading organisations such as Barclays, British Airways, Goldman Sachs, HSBC, IBM, Iraq Energy Institute, Jaguar, Johnson and Johnson, Microsoft, and Xerox. MBA students undertake regular visits to company head offices and representatives from these companies often visit campus as part of the MBA Guest Speaker Series, Let’s Talk Business.

The Brunel MBA is one of a distinguished group of MBA programmes worldwide to have achieved accreditation by both the Association of Masters of Business Administration (AMBA) and the Association to Advance Collegiate Schools of Business (AACSB).

Student life & support

Work while studying

International students can work part-time alongside their studies (subject to visa conditions).

Co-op & placements

Offers co-op or work-placement options in selected programs.

On-campus housing

Students typically arrange off-campus or homestay accommodation.

Accommodation options

Students need to plan their accommodations well in advance of their arrival as all accommodation options fill up quickly, well before the start of the semester.

On-Campus Residence Accommodations

On-Campus residence accommodations are provided per discretion by the school.

Off-Campus Accommodations

The local area has a variety of off-campus rental housing options including single homes, duplexes, apartments and rooms for rent. Students wishing to live off-campus need to research availability on their own and should arrive well before the start of term to do so.

Homestay

There are a wide variety of homestay options available, and our partners do their best to match students and hosts according to their interests and preferences. Homestay hosts include single people, young couples with children and pets, and older couples. All homestay accommodations have been inspected, and all adults in the home have completed a required criminal reference check.
