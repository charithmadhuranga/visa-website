About University of Lincoln

Set against the backdrop of a beautiful and historic city, the University of Lincoln sits at the heart of it all on the picturesque Brayford Pool waterfront, with almost everything students will need either on campus or just a short walk away.

Established in 1996, the university has flourished into a diverse, internationally recognised university which welcomes thousands of students each year from around the world. In November 2023, the University of Lincoln was awarded the Queen's Anniversary Prize, the UK's highest award for universities, to recognise its work supporting the agri-food sector.

Throughout this exciting period of development, the university has been guided by a consistent set of principles which remain at the heart of its approach today: excellence in teaching, learning, and quality of student experience; responsiveness to the needs of communities and organisations; and working in partnership to make great things happen.

Why University of Lincoln - Brayford Pool

  • Future Ready Degree Courses: University of Lincoln's degree courses are packed with features to help students develop the skills and expertise that employers are looking for. Many of their courses are accredited by recognised and respected organisations, such as the Chartered Management Institute, and include employability, professional practice, or work-based learning modules that can help students apply theory to practice.
  • Industry Insights: The university's Futures Programme provides opportunities to hear from inspiring guest speakers, who are invited to talk about their careers and to discuss emerging industry trends. Students can also access Lincoln Connect, which is the University of Lincoln’s online mentoring platform, where they can develop their networking skills and build a professional community.
  • Teaching of the Highest Quality: The University of Lincoln was awarded Gold in the national Teaching Excellence Framework 2023 and a five-star score overall in the QS Stars rating 2023
  • Award Winning Student Support: Ranked in the top 10 in the UK for postgraduate support in the Postgraduate Taught Experience Survey 2024
  • A Great Location: Lincoln is regarded as one of England’s great historic cities, and was named the UK’s most affordable student city in the NatWest Student Living Index 2025.

Accommodation options

University Managed Accommodation

The University of Lincoln offers a great range of accommodation options within the city-centre, the majority of which are located on the Brayford Pool campus or a short walk away. With five residences to choose from, the University offers a variety of options to suit different needs and budgets.

Staying in university managed accommodation includes a range of benefits such as 24/7 security, on-site maintenance, contents insurance, access to classes and workout space at the University’s on-campus Sports Centre and more.

In 2025, the University of Lincoln was listed in the top 5 in the Halls and Student Accommodation category at the Whatuni Student Choice Awards 2025. Lincoln is also ranked 5th out of 100 UK institutions in the Uni Compare Top Universities for Accommodation 2026.

Privately Rented Accommodation

The city of Lincoln and the surrounding area is home to a wide range of privately rented accommodation options, such as shared housing and studio apartments. If students are considering living in privately rented accommodation away from campus, UoL recommends visiting the University’s website to find out more about what to look for and how to get started.
