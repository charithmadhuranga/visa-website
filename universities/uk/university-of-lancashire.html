Overview

About University of Lancashire

University of Lancashire - Preston Campus is right at the heart of the city. You'll find us minutes away from a vibrant shopping centre, attractions and nightlife. It’s home to a thriving international community of over 42,000 students and staff.

Why University of Lancashire - Preston

  • Mission: University is proud of their mission and they take it seriously. Since being founded as the Institution for the Diffusion of Knowledge in 1828, their educational ethos has brought life-changing learning to local people, this remains at the heart of their mission. University institution motto ‘Ex solo ad solem’ translates to ‘From the Earth to the Sun’. It means helping talented people from all walks of life to make the most of their potential. They create positive change in their students, staff, business partners and wider communities, enabling them to develop their full potential by providing excellent higher education, innovation and research.They are a proud civic anchor too, committed to adding social value and playing their role in the economic prosperity of the places in which they are based; sourcing products and services locally, boosting jobs and economic growth. There pride themselves on universally supporting their people, being actively liberating, inherently innovative and proudly spirited to face whatever life throws at us. These values are woven into the fabric of who they are and will always be.
  • Values and initiatives: University of Lancashire - Preston values reflect who they are as a university and what they stand for. They define the things that are most important to us and nurture University's sense of shared purpose. Supporting their values and initiatives are a number of networks, creating positive change throughout their university, for their students, colleagues and the wider communities.
  • Sustainability: The University is a Gold Tier University in the Green Universities Report 2023 (USwitch), highlighting University's commitment to sustainability. They have retained a 2:1 classification in the People & Planet University Green League 2023/24, where they also ranked first place in the North West for water reduction. The University is also one of just 13 universities worldwide to achieve a Bronze Carbon Literate Organisation status.

Master's · Engineering & Construction

Master in Architecture – Architecture

1 year Next intake: Sep 2027 Apply by 1 Sep 2027

The MArch Architecture course focuses on design innovation and current theories of architecture and urbanism to challenge student thinking and prepare them for practice.

This programme's studios, workshops and CAD suites are among the best in the UK and they'll receive regular one-to-one tutorial support from a team who are leading thinkers and practitioners in their field.

This MArch course provides progression for Part 1 Architecture graduates to a Part 2 course, which is a requirement for professional membership. Building on the knowledge students acquired on their undergraduate course, they'll get to advance their design work and critical thinking skills.

The course content is informed by contemporary regional issues, specifically in areas such as urban and rural contextual issues, food production, regeneration, post-industrial landscapes, heritage protection, and sustainable urbanism. Students undertake a series of guided complex urban and rural design projects, exploring technological strategies and coming up with creative solutions to the challenges of sustainability in architecture and urbanism.

Master's · Business & Management

Master of Research – Management

1 year Next intake: Jan 2027 Apply by 30 Nov 2026

The MRes in Management is a flexible programme allowing students to focus on business and management research. This course provides an opportunity for students to understand the research process within their chosen subject area, providing a pathway to gaining a postgraduate degree that develops essential, critical and analytical skills for workplace, or doctoral studies.

The programme has been designed to be flexible, putting students and their interests at the centre of the course. Students will undertake two taught modules, at the start of the course, these will help them build a critical understanding of the research process.

The taught elements will be delivered in a block session to provide students with an in-depth learning experience that can fit around other commitments. Students will then move to a research study supported by a supervisor who will provide one to one tuition.

Students may undertake a supervised research project, either focused on a business or industry setting or solely within an academic context. Here students will apply both the theory and practical implications of research.

Students will be able to develop skills to use on future projects, both academically and within the workplace.

This course is suitable for students who would like to have a flexible approach to study and have a specific interest in business or management. The course would also suit people currently in work and those who would like to become expert in their chosen area of study.

MBA · Business & Management

Master of Business Administration – Business Administration (Professional Placement)

2 years Next intake: Jan 2027 Apply by 30 Nov 2026

The Master of Business Administration with Professional Placement is a highly respected business management qualification.

It’s an intensive two-year programme focusing on leadership and strategic management and this distinguishes it from other master’s programmes.

This programme is specifically designed for those wishing to either establish or sustain a successful managerial or professional career in business.

Prior management experience is not an essential requirement for this course, as a professional placement is incorporated into the second year.

If a student isn't able to undertake a placement as part of Year 2, they'll be required to take a set of alternative modules equating to the necessary credits. This alternative Year 2 would mean the degree title awarded would change to MBA with Work-Based Learning, instead of the MBA with Professional Placement award.

On-campus housing

On-campus residence is available.

Accommodation options

Students need to plan their accommodations well in advance of their arrival as all accommodation options fill up quickly, well before the start of the semester.

On-Campus Residence Accommodations

At Preston we guarantee all first year, full-time students a place in University accommodation on campus. Rent varies depending on the room type and facilities available. Starting at only £85 per week, our on campus accommodation is almost half the national average (CUG, 2024).

Off-Campus Accommodations

All students must find suitable accommodation prior to arrival which is within an hour’s commute to campus.
