Overview

About Edinburgh Napier University International College

Edinburgh Napier University International College (ENUIC) is an embedded oncampus college for international students to start their studies at Edinburgh Napier University. It is located in the historic, city centre campus of Merchiston, with easy access to student accommodation. Pathway programmes at ENUIC prepare students for their chosen future degree with academic and English language support. ENUIC offers students the option between International Stage One, International Stage Two, or an International Incorporated Masters.

Why Edinburg Napier University International College

  • Track: Edinburgh Napier develop highly valued, career-ready graduates. How? By combining professional know-how with an academic approach to deliver effective work-related learning.
  • Student support: Supporting you all the way. How? Through student-focused support services and caring staff, our commitment to ensuring you thrive is our chief concern – Edinburgh Napier has even won an award for it.93.7% of undergraduate students and 95.1% of postgraduate students are in work or further study 15 months after graduation from Edinburgh Napier.
  • Seamless Transition: Students can expect a seamless transition directly onto their ENU course upon successful completion of their studies at ENUIC.

Bachelor's · Science & Environment

International Stage One in Biosciences – Transfer to Bachelor of Science (Honours) – Applied Microbiology

4 years Next intake: Jan 2027 Apply by 8 Dec 2026

The International Stage One (IS1) represents the first year of a four-year undergraduate degree which caters specifically for the needs of international students. It provides an opportunity for students to take part in a bespoke programme which maps against the standard first year offered at Edinburgh Napier University (ENU). They will be able to study in the supportive environment of an international college situated on an ENU campus and follow a Learning, Teaching and Assessment Approach designed to meet their learning needs while still being immersed in a university environment, accessing University facilities and resources and following university undergraduate regulations. Successful completion of the IS1 enables them to join the second year of their undergraduate studies at Edinburgh Napier University.

During students' studies on International Stage One (IS1) they will enhance their English language capabilities, develop academic study skills appropriate to the United Kingdom Higher Education environment and acclimate to living and studying in another country and cultural context very different from their own. Alongside language and study skills tuition, those working on the International Stage One are also able to study content modules within a chosen field of study and which are closely related to modules delivered in Edinburgh Napier University (ENU)’s standard year one. They are therefore able to join their second year studies at Scottish Credit and Qualifications Framework (SCQF) Level 8 alongside continuing students with increased confidence in both their language and study skills and subject knowledge.

The Bachelor of Science (Honours) Applied Microbiology course provides students with specialist skills in food, medical and environmental microbiology and biotechnology while also giving them a broad understanding of the biological sciences.
