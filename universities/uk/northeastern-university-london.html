About Northeastern University London

Part of Northeastern University’s Global Campus Network and overlooking the River Thames and Tower Bridge, Northeastern University London (NU London) offers academically challenging educational programmes designed to inspire innovative thinking, encourage interdisciplinary study, and provide global experiences, while equipping students with the advanced skills and experiences that are highly sought by employers and society.

Why Northeastern University London

  • Accessible Campus: The entire campus is extremely welcoming to all, with excellent accessibility and support, including lifts, wide entry points, adaptable furniture, and hearing loops. The ventilation and air-filtration system is similar to those used in hospital settings providing enhanced protection for the community.
  • Location : Devon House is situated at the heart of historic St Katharine Docks, just a few minutes’ walk from the Tower of London and Tower Bridge and enjoys stunning river views with terraces and open areas surrounded by dozens of restaurants, cafes, bars, and a leisure marina, all part of a dynamic waterside setting. The location is also right on the doorstep of the City, London’s Central Business District, a worldwide centre for commerce. Home to the Stock Exchange and the Bank of England, as well as finance and commerce sectors, the law profession, cultural organisations, creative industries, tech companies and start-ups. The City offers a wide range of opportunities for work experience, internships, and part-time student employment.The University is also just minutes from East London Tech City, a cluster of high-tech companies and web businesses.Devon House is the focal point for our stimulating and diverse international community, with Northeastern University London and Northeastern University students sharing facilities.

Bachelor's · Business & Management

Bachelor of Science (Honours) – Business

3 years Next intake: Sep 2027 Apply by 1 Sep 2027

The BSc (Hons) Business examines all aspects of business: management approaches, business structures, marketing, supply chains and operations management, and finance. Courses use case studies and examples drawn from a wide range of business types (from entrepreneurs to global corporations), to understand the varying ways businesses can operate and be managed.

The curriculum includes courses in marketing, management, operations, finance, supply chain, innovation, fintech, statistics, global responsibility and sustainability.

This degree programme combines a rigorous business syllabus with a personalised elective pathway. Students can choose to deepen their focus on business through their elective courses, or they can further contextualise and broaden their studies with courses that match other interests and goals.

What makes this course different:

  • Rigorous, in-depth core subject study
  • Interdisciplinary degrees, built around real-world problems
  • Flexibility to create your own academic pathway with elective courses
  • Gain core competencies and develop practical and transferable skills for your future career
  • Embedded experiential opportunities to drive real-world change
  • Explore the big issues that need agile thinking crossing subject boundaries

Master's · Computing & IT

Master of Arts – Philosophy and Artificial Intelligence

1 year Next intake: Sep 2027 Apply by 1 Sep 2027

The MA Philosophy and Artificial Intelligence combines exploration of a range of philosophical issues, including ethical issues related to computer data, with opportunities to develop the skills and techniques of data science. Poised at the intersection of the humanities and technology, this innovative masters degree can equip you with the combination of thinking, communication, and technical skills currently sought by employers in sectors ranging from financial and professional services, to the creative industries, government and policy-making.

Presupposing no background in either philosophy or programming, it ensures students are equipped with the skills and knowledge needed for graduate study in philosophy, and offers opportunities to acquire relevant coding abilities, and knowledge of the techniques of data science that underpin recent advances in machine learning and artificial intelligence, should students so desire.

At the same time, the programme allows for a progressive exploration of ethical and other philosophical issues arising in this area, with courses directly addressing them, and a dissertation devoted to a detailed investigation of an approved topic of the student’s choosing.

Master's · Business & Management

Master of Science – International Business

1 year Next intake: Sep 2027 Apply by 1 Sep 2027

MSc International Business: Leading in an Evolving Global Workplace

The Master of Science in International Business is designed to prepare graduates to navigate and lead within today’s rapidly changing global environment. Emphasising contemporary management practices and people-centric innovation, the programme cultivates advanced knowledge in organisational strategy, human resource management, marketing, and strategic decision-making.

In response to evolving workplace dynamics—driven by artificial intelligence, remote work, and shifting workforce expectations—the programme provides participants with the critical skills required to align organisational performance with effective people management. A range of specialised modules are available, including negotiation, innovation, and leadership with a focus on sustainability.

Furthermore, students have the opportunity to consolidate their learning through an optional experiential project conducted in a real-world business context.

Whether seeking to advance an existing career or transition into a leadership role, graduates of the MSc in International Business are well-equipped to succeed in the complex and competitive landscape of international commerce.

This Master of Science in International Business provides graduates with a comprehensive understanding of international management and people-focused leadership, equipping them to navigate the complexities of today’s global business environment.

Master key business functions – The programme fosters expertise in areas such as business growth, marketing, ethics, and finance, examined through a variety of lenses including those of customers, shareholders, society, governance, and the environment.

Address real-world business challenges – Students acquire practical tools and methodologies to formulate strategic, evidence-based solutions to complex international business issues.

Lead with confidence – By integrating core business knowledge with advanced people management capabilities, the programme prepares graduates to drive innovation and effect meaningful change across global organisations.

Develop self-awareness and professional impact – Participants are encouraged to reflect on their professional identity and develop the competencies required to become high-value contributors within diverse organisational settings.

Overall, the programme is designed to cultivate critical thinking, strategic decision-making, and the capacity to make a lasting, positive impact within the field of international business.
