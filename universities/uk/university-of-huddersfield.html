Overview

About University of Huddersfield

The University of Huddersfield - London campus is specifically dedicated to preparing international students with the skills and knowledge needed to successfully complete a postgraduate degree in business. Choose a Master’s degree that offers the reputation of the University of Huddersfield's teaching quality and employability success as well as all the opportunities that London has to offer! This campus offers not only a top-rate business education, but also support students as they adapt to life in London and take the next step in their careers. The University of Huddersfield is among the UK's top providers of work placement courses and knows exactly how to prepare students for successful careers and a great future.

Why University of Huddersfield - London

  • Facilities: Students get to learn in lecture theatres, small classrooms, and have one-to-one sessions with tutors. They also have access to a library during their studies.
  • Location: Ranked the world’s number one student city (QS Best Student Cities Index 2019), London is also a world-renowned city of thriving commerce where students can develop their interest in business. Close proximity to some of the world’s leading organizations gives students the opportunity to build links and networks that could help progress their careers.
  • Career-Focused Studies: Postgraduate degrees are designed with students' employability in mind. During their studies, students will have the opportunity to gain valuable experience by working for up to 20 hours per week - this will give them a chance to build a network, gain experience in their chosen field and earn some extra spending money.
  • Job Search Support: The university's employability advisers support students to find the right work experience for them by reviewing CVs and cover letters, helping with application forms, interview preparation and more.
  • Education Excellence: This university is gold-rated by the Teaching Excellence Framework (TEF), 2017 and received the Global Teaching Excellence Award from the Higher Education Academy in the same year.
  • Accommodation: International students can live with each other a residence hall in McMillan Student Village, close to their classes. The residence has on-site staff who are there to help students from around the world adjust to life in London.

Students typically arrange off-campus or homestay accommodation.

Accommodation options

Students need to plan their accommodations well in advance of their arrival as all accommodation options fill up quickly, well before the start of the semester.

On-Campus Residence Accommodations

On-Campus residence accommodations are provided per discretion by the school.

Off-Campus Accommodations

The local area has a variety of off-campus rental housing options including single homes, duplexes, apartments and rooms for rent. Students wishing to live off-campus need to research availability on their own and should arrive well before the start of term to do so.
