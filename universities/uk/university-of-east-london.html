Overview

About University of East London (UEL)

The University of East London was founded in 1898 with the aim of serving both its local community and a wider audience. The University believes that education is a potent tool in decreasing inequality and promoting a brighter future for everyone. UEL is reshaping education for the benefit of all, giving students from any background the skills and opportunities to thrive in the modern world.

The University of East London equips students for a rewarding career with work placements, networking opportunities, and tools to navigate the unexpected. Their graduates - whether artists, architects, or accountants - are making a real impact in their communities.

Why University of East London (UEL)

  • Location: UEL is one of the only London-based universities to have a self-contained campus. They are part of the local community and well-connected to the rest of London, and the world - City Airport is at their doorstep. Full of culture, creativity and career opportunities, East London is one of the youngest, most multicultural neighbourhoods in the UK, so students will fit in no matter where they’re from.
  • Careers-Led University: UEL is a careers-first university, offering education and industry access to people from all walks of life. With ambition and hard work, students can turn their goals into reality through UEL's contacts, expertise, and support. Their academics, often active in their industries, guide students through courses designed to address real-world challenges. The University collaborates with industry to constantly update its courses, ensuring that education aligns with evolving employer needs, preparing students for a rewarding career.
  • Stand Out From the Crowd: Why do University of East London graduates stand out? It's because they offer employers more than just academic expertise. UEL has designed their degree programmes to include essential skills to help students adapt, embrace change and show potential employers that they are fit for the careers of the future. UEL's Professional Fitness and Mental Wealth programme develops students' emotional, cultural and cognitive intelligence alongside digital skills, communication, resilience and entrepreneurship.
  • Diverse and Supportive Community: At the University of East London, students will benefit from learning alongside people from all over the world. UEL is diverse, inclusive, and supportive. Students will feel welcomed and supported throughout their university journey and beyond - from application and beyond into career.

Master's · Business & Management

Master of Science – International Business Management

1 year Next intake: Jan 2027 Apply by 28 Nov 2026

UEL's MSc in International Business Management prepares students for a huge variety of business roles in a global context, whether they are interested in entrepreneurship, global innovation, corporate strategy, consultancy, or multinational management. UEL's staff and students are drawn from all over the world with widely varied expertise, making this MSc a truly international programme.

The course looks toward future trends in business, from the changing role of business in society to the impact that rapidly evolving technology has on business in a global context. The university trains students to be mobile across different markets in high-tech, knowledge-intensive industries; remain flexible in the face of technological change, and maintain an international outlook as the world evolves.

Students will learn about the way businesses can adapt to new challenges, from the importance of sustainability in the face of climate change to the rise of robotics and the Internet of Things.

Based across Docklands and USS campuses, students will have easy access to London City Airport, City Of London Square Mile, Canary Wharf financial and digital district, International Excel Centre and the O2 Arena.

The course is designed to resonate with people from a variety of backgrounds, so students won't need to have specific academic or work experience. Throughout the degree, students will develop crucial analytical, research, and communication skills as they work on projects with their fellow students and undertake their own original research. UEL's personal tutoring scheme also gives students access to bespoke academic advice and exciting industry opportunities.

This course is accredited by the Association for Project Management (APM).
