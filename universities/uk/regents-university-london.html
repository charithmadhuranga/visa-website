Overview

About Regent's University London

Based in Royal Regent's Park and surrounded by 11 acres of beautiful private parkland, Regent's University London offers a tranquil and secure setting for those wishing to study in the heart of London. The school offers small class sizes and a personal experience, which is an excellent fit for international students choosing to study overseas for the first time. Core program offerings span a wide variety of disciplines including Business and Management, Fashion and Design, Liberal Arts and Humanities, Film, Media and Performance, and Psychology.

Regent's has one of the most diverse student bodies across the entirety of the UK, attracting 80 percent of its students from over 137 different countries outside of the UK. With the ethos of developing tomorrow's global leaders, Regent's offers exclusive networking opportunities for students seeking an international education. Other cultural and cross-cultural elective modules are available to all students, providing the opportunity to develop language skills, cultural agility and internationalism, whilst enhancing and personalising their professional profiles.

Why Regent's University London

  • Location: Located in the heart of London, students will find themselves immersed with plenty of opportunities outside of the classroom. London has a world-class arts and culture, fashion, and entertainment scene. As well, Regent's University London is located in the heart of Regent's Park, one of the Royal Parks of London, offering a tranquil and secure setting for students.
  • Small Class Sizes: With an average class size of 14 students and a 13:1 student to staff ratio, Regent's faculty staff understand and guide each students on their individual academic journeys.
  • Emphasis on Internationalization: Regent's University London is a truly global institution whose student body is 80 percent international, coming from over 137 different countries outside of the UK. With the ethos of developing tomorrow's global leaders, Regent's offers exclusive networking opportunities for students who are seeking an international education.
  • Facilities: Nearly one-third of Regent's graduates start their own businesses within six months of graduation. The school's award-winning entrepreneurial space, The Hive, provides a support system with excellent guidance, ranging from how to take a unique idea and create a business plan to how to expand a business into new markets. As well as providing workshops on CV preparation and interview skills, The Hive provides a hub for internship and placement opportunities.

NOTE: This school may use UCAS as an external portal for application for some undergraduate programs. A £20 (single application) or £26 (up to 5 applications) application fee may apply.

Bachelor's · Business & Management

Bachelor of Arts (Honours) – Business and Entrepreneurship

3 years Next intake: Jan 2027 Apply by 10 Dec 2026

Many businesses rely on enterprise and innovation to manage uncertainty and ensure sustained growth. This course teaches students to become an effective entrepreneur and an innovative leader – with the insight to identify opportunities, implement new processes, harness new technology and transform global businesses into market leaders.

Students will discover how to identify and develop a potential market opportunity, and drive sustained business growth – undertaking market research, making strategic decisions, managing uncertainty, exploring new technology, generating ideas and overcoming challenges.

Working with classmates, students will construct a business plan for a new profitable offering and work together to refine it – for instance, analysing the cost implications of adding or subtracting certain features. By doing this, students will gain a solid grounding in business principles and a deep understanding of how enterprises thrive: learning core competencies like finance, law, regulations, operations and organisational behaviour, while developing specialist skills, creative thinking, strategic decision-making and ability to innovate, globally.

From global organisations to new start-ups, studying in London offers opportunities to engage with all aspects of the business arena – gaining valuable work experience and meeting influential industry contacts.

Students will join bootcamps, simulations and creative labs to learn to think like a leader and an entrepreneur, and work on live briefs commissioned by industry experts that will expose them to real-world business issues and give them the strategic skills to combat them.

Students will graduate with a practical, future-facing business education that's entrepreneurial, network-driven and tech engaged.

Program Description

This degree with integrated foundation aims to prepare students for a professional and creative career as an interior designer.

The integrated foundation has been specially designed to give students the introductory knowledge and skills needed to confidently progress to degree-level study.

In the foundation year, students will develop a broad understanding of the creative industries, touching on aspects of fashion design, fashion marketing, illustration, interior design and graphic design.

Once students have successfully completed the foundation year, they will progress to the first year of BA (Hons) Interior Design

Master's · Arts, Media & Design

Master of Arts – Media and Digital Communications

1 year Next intake: Sep 2027 Apply by 1 Sep 2027

Program Description

This course combines traditional media studies with new practices emerging in the 21st century – teaching students how to innovate, anticipate, analyse and utilise communications across a variety of media platforms.

Students will cover a range of traditional and modern communicative practices across the arts, sciences, humanities, business and industry – exploring how the field is shaped by digital advances. This interdisciplinary approach will give students a thorough grounding in media and communications: as an academic field, a cultural landscape and a complex network of industries.

Examining theories, students will discover the powerful influence media and communications can have socially, culturally and politically – gaining a critical understanding of media and transmedia creation and consumption. Looking at current global issues, students will develop the skills to monitor and analyse audience perceptions and interactions, and implement creative strategies to overcome challenges – using emerging tools and methods like data scraping and data visualisation.

Students will choose from a variety of electives to tailor their studies to topics that interest them – from transmedia storytelling to media regulations, social media marketing, creative economies and even human connection – exploring how dating apps use algorithms to shape matches.

In the final term, students will complete a dissertation, creative project or industry placement – consolidating their learning and gaining insights into one of the most progressive and exciting industries in the world.

Master's · Business & Management

Master of Science – Finance and Financial Technology

1 year – 16 months Next intake: Jan 2027 Apply by 10 Dec 2026

The world of finance was one of the first to adopt today's advances in technology. On this course, students will master the skills needed to work with complex and dynamic financial markets – utilising integrated financial technology and responding to change at pace.

Students will learn to analyse the financial technology (fintech) and innovation sector, explore the digital investment space (including crowd sourcing and crowd funding) and examine new technological disruptions that aim to make financial services more accessible and efficient, personal and secure – including AI, cryptocurrency, cognitive computing, big data and blockchain.

Students will become skilled at analysing new data and connectivity, evaluating technological intelligence and managing risks and uncertainty.

Classes are designed to simulate a real-life experience of the financial industry. Students will benefit from hands-on training in economics software (with access to Thomson Reuters Eikon Terminals, Bloomberg L.P Terminals, Datastream databases and trading rooms on campus), as well as the chance to collaborate with students from all over the world to gain a global insight.

Studying in London, students will have access to financial networks, banking organisations and high-net-worth clients, as well as workshops and industry speakers – exposing them to new insights.

Students will become a financial player with the skills to innovate, analyse new technology and evaluate risks – making financial services more efficient and secure. Students will understand the wider financial industry (from corporate finance to investments) and work across international markets.

This course is also accredited by the Chartered Management Institute (CMI) and, upon completion, students will receive a Level 7 Diploma in Strategic Management and Leadership Practice alongside their degree.

Student life & support

Work while studying

International students can work part-time alongside their studies (subject to visa conditions).

Co-op & placements

Co-op options are limited — ask us about placement-friendly alternatives.

On-campus housing

Students typically arrange off-campus or homestay accommodation.

Accommodation options

Students need to plan their accommodations well in advance of their arrival as all accommodation options fill up quickly, well before the start of the semester.

On-Campus Residence Accommodations

On-Campus residence accommodations are provided per discretion by the school.

Off-Campus Accommodations

The local area has a variety of off-campus rental housing options including single homes, duplexes, apartments and rooms for rent. Students wishing to live off-campus need to research availability on their own and should arrive well before the start of term to do so.

Homestay

There are a wide variety of homestay options available, and our partners do their best to match students and hosts according to their interests and preferences. Homestay hosts include single people, young couples with children and pets, and older couples. All homestay accommodations have been inspected, and all adults in the home have completed a required criminal reference check.
