About Heriot-Watt University

Heriot-Watt University is a product of the Scottish Enlightenment. A pioneer in bringing education to working people in 1821 and founder of a global movement through the creation of the world's first Mechanics Institute. Heriot-Watt is a university with global presence and a visionary international strategy. Since the early 1990s, they have built sustainable academic partnerships with institutions across the globe from the US to the Ukraine and from China to Trinidad and Tobago. They continue to push new frontiers in the fields of science, technology, engineering, business, and design.

Why Heriot-Watt University- Edinburgh?

  • Location: Edinburgh is a really lively city with plenty to see and do. It's a great city for cultural events with something happening every day of the week. Whether it's music, film, theatre, comedy or the arts, students are bound to find plenty going on of interest.
  • Facilities: At Heriot-Watt University, they offer a wide range of services to help students deal with all aspects of their life, whether these be academic, personal, technical, financial or just plain fun. The Student Services are on hand to support students' learning through a wide range of provision including accommodation, computing, healthcare, sport, welfare and careers. University support complements the services and facilities offered through the Students' Association.
  • Globally Connected: The university, one of Scotland's most international universities having around a third of their students from outside, have 5 campuses and 150 learning partners around the world. Many of their students get the chance to transfer their study to campuses in Dubai and Malaysia through their Go Global inter-campus exchange programme.

Master's · Data & Analytics

Master of Science – Actuarial Management

1 year Next intake: Sep 2027 Apply by 1 Sep 2027

The MSc in Actuarial Management is fully accredited with the Institute and Faculty of Actuaries in the UK for subjects: CP1, CP2, CP3, SP2, SP4, SP5, SP6 and SP9. In addition, studying subject SP9 leads to the internationally recognised Certified Enterprise Risk Actuary (CERA) qualification. It is also possible to "catch up" with some of the earlier subjects that students may have missed – such as CB1, CB2 or CM2.

These subjects cover a variety of areas including life insurance, risk management, investment, derivatives, pensions, communication and modelling. Students choose the best combination for themselves. Completing this degree gets students close to qualification as an actuary, with only one or two exams left to complete after leaving university. It typically speeds up their qualification by one or two years.

Heriot-Watt University is the only Centre of Actuarial Excellence (as recognised by the society of actuaries (SOA) – North America) in Europe. Heriot-Watt has a very active Students' Actuarial Society which won several awards at a recent Heriot-Watt 'Oscars' ceremony. This body is completely managed, enthusiastically and professionally, by its students.

Master's · Engineering & Construction

Master of Science – Construction Project Management

1 year Next intake: Jan 2027 Apply by 1 Jan 2027

Construction Project Management at Heriot-Watt University is one of the longest-running courses of its kind in the UK, equipping graduates with the theoretical knowledge and practical and technical skills to manage people and projects within the construction industry.

A range of core modules covering management, procurement and technology-related subjects will give students a foundation in management principles while providing practical skills relevant to professional practice in construction.

Students will also choose an optional module from a range of subjects drawn from the built environment courses, providing them with a broader understanding of built environment issues and an opportunity to tailor their studies to fit their interests and professional needs.

The University is also a Corporate Member of the Association for Project Management. This course is delivered by the Centre of Excellence in Sustainable Building Design, a leading body recognised by the Royal Academy of Engineering. This is one of four such centres established at UK universities that jointly form a national network to demonstrate and exchange best practice in teaching and research for the sustainable built environment. Students tend to come from the construction industry, bringing with them practical experience in a variety of built environment roles, or from other professional backgrounds but with an interest in converting to a career in the construction industry. This course offers a number of guest lectures each year specific to the modules where experienced professionals from industry visit to share their knowledge and experience with students.

Master's · Computing & IT

Master of Science – Data Science

1 year Next intake: Jan 2027 Apply by 1 Jan 2027

This Data Science masters teaches the theory and skills for managing and analysing large, complex data sets – sometimes called Big Data. Students will learn how to model, store and process these data sets using the latest algorithms and techniques. Modern methods and tools for visually exploring the data will also be covered. The MSc course offers the key skill set of a data scientist. Optional courses provide an opportunity to explore closely-related areas based on the internationally leading research work of Heriot-Watt's academics.

The MSc is accredited by the British Computer Society. The skills that students learn through the Data Science masters will be applicable to industrial applications, as well as to scientific data exploration. In some cases, the MSc project can also be carried out in collaboration with an outside industrial or academic organisation.

Master's · Business & Management

Master of Science – International Accounting and Finance

1 year Next intake: Jan 2027 Apply by 1 Jan 2027

With a focus on the ever-changing global financial markets, this MSc in Accounting and Finance aims to equip graduates with highly employable expertise and a thorough understanding of current issues in global financial markets – both academically and professionally.

On this MSc students will take six main courses on the subject of international accounting and be offered a choice of one specialist per semester. These courses will give them an international perspective on accounting and help them understand things like national differences, harmonisation and the challenges and problems facing multinational companies.

Students will have the opportunity to apply their newly acquired knowledge and expertise gained from courses in international banking, financial markets and accountancy to a subject of interest to them in their dissertation research project which is undertaken during the summer months.

Heriot-Watt University is a named Bloomberg Experiential Learning Partner (ELP), a programme designed by Bloomberg to recognise universities that are leaders in experiential learning.

Master's · Business & Management

Master of Science – International Business Management

1 year Next intake: Jan 2027 Apply by 1 Jan 2027

This one-year postgraduate programme, MSc International Business Management, allows students to study specialist subjects from across the business sphere, including:

  • International Business Context
  • Competitive Strategy
  • Research Philosophy and Practice
  • Business Economics.

These four courses provide insight into relevant social, political, legal, and macroeconomic conditions affecting international business and develop skills in decision-making, people management, leadership and team working.

In addition, students will select two optional courses in Semester 1 and two optional courses in Semester 2. Shaping their MSc study in this way allows them to develop knowledge and skills that reflect their unique career aspirations.

Ultimately, students will learn how to critically evaluate and implement theories, concepts and techniques of business and apply this knowledge to practical contexts through industry-focused, case-study-led projects.

MSc International Business Management is a conversion degree programme led by academics from Edinburgh Business School. It introduces the study of business and management to graduates and professionals from non-management backgrounds who wish to enhance their careers.
