About University of the West of England Bristol International College

The University of the West of England (UWE) Bristol’s International College offers pathway courses in partnership with UWE Bristol, and is integrated into the University campus. The College is fully equipped to help students prepare for their university degree. With dedicated and experienced staff and outstanding facilities, students will be supported at every step as they start their path to university. Students at UWE Bristol’s International College can choose from a number of courses, with progression to undergraduate or postgraduate degrees.

A pathway course at UWE Bristol's International College (UWEBIC) will provide students with the skills they need to study a degree course at UWE Bristol. As well as English language study, students will learn academic and subject-based skills to help them prepare for university. The College has three pathway options available to students: Foundation Year, International Year One, and Pre-Masters. If students successfully complete their pathway course at the required level, they are guaranteed entry to their chosen undergraduate or postgraduate degrees at UWE Bristol.

Why University of the West of England (UWE) Bristol International College

  • Location: Bristol is a beautiful city with a lively harbourside area, where students will find lots of cafés and restaurants overlooking the water. Students will find lots of street art here, as well as plenty of museums, art galleries, shops and parks.
  • Fantastic Facilities: The College is equipped with everything students need for their studies, and from the first day students will have access to UWE Bristol's advanced facilities like a production studio, and the UK's largest robotics lab, as well as the amenities of the Students' Union.
  • Comprehensive Support: The College's friendly staff will be ready to help students with any problems they may have, whether it's studying for exams or coping with homesickness. They'll also organize fun events for international students, so they can see more of the city and make friends.

Student life & support

Work while studying

International students can work part-time alongside their studies (subject to visa conditions).

Co-op & placements

Offers co-op or work-placement options in selected programs.

On-campus housing

Students typically arrange off-campus or homestay accommodation.

Accommodation options

Students need to plan their accommodations well in advance of their arrival as all accommodation options fill up quickly, well before the start of the semester.

On-Campus Residence Accommodations

On-Campus residence accommodations are provided per discretion by the school.

Off-Campus Accommodations

The local area has a variety of off-campus rental housing options including single homes, duplexes, apartments and rooms for rent. Students wishing to live off-campus need to research availability on their own and should arrive well before the start of term to do so.

Homestay

There are a wide variety of homestay options available, and our partners do their best to match students and hosts according to their interests and preferences. Homestay hosts include single people, young couples with children and pets, and older couples. All homestay accommodations have been inspected, and all adults in the home have completed a required criminal reference check.
