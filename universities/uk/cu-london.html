Overview

About CU London

Study for a degree from one of the UK’s leading universities, right in the country's capital! CU London, part of the Coventry University Group, offers a style of education that is flexible and designed to fit around students' lives. Students can choose from a wide range of vocational, career-focused courses and study at a pace that suits them. CU London students benefit from a modern, forward-thinking higher education institution, reinforced by the quality and reputation of Coventry University. All courses are awarded and accredited by Coventry University, which means that students receive a Coventry University qualification upon graduation.

Dagenham campus is located in East London, at the former Civic Centre. The grade II listed building has undergone a £4.5 million renovation that is purpose built for the needs of students. It offers everything students need under one roof!

Why CU London - Dagenham

  • Flexible Start Dates and Small Class Sizes: CU London aims to makes higher education accessible for international students by offering competitively-priced courses with numerous intakes throughout the year, small class sizes for higher levels of support, and ongoing English language support.
  • Highly-ranked University: Shortlisted for University of the Year - The Times and Sunday Times Good University Guide 2021.
  • Teaching Excellence: Awarded Gold for Teaching and Learning Excellence by the Teaching Excellence Framework (TEF) 2017.
  • Focus on Employability: CU London students are taught by lecturers who are experts in their field, making them career-ready with the skills to succeed upon graduation. 97% are employed or enrolled in further study within 6 months of graduation (DLHE survey, UG UK, 2016/17).
  • Facilities: Classrooms and IT suites are fully-equipped for interactive learning and there are modern, open breakout spaces. There is a student diner and lounge, as well as a Customer Service Desk with staff on hand and ready to help students at any time.
  • Location: Ranked the world’s number one student city (QS Best Student Cities Index 2018), there is something for everyone in London! The UK's capital offers the perfect combination of history and culture in a modern setting.

Bachelor's · Health & Nursing

4 years Next intake: May 2027 Apply by 1 Apr 2027

This course aims to help students become a knowledgeable, enthusiastic and skilled individual ready to support the delivery of health and social care within the NHS, local authority services, private health provision or third sector services. It provides the opportunity for the development of the knowledge and skills required of healthcare support workers, community health care workers, those with roles in health and social care, and offers a Continuing Professional Development opportunity. The learning outcomes of modules, assignments and projects will be clearly stated. Students' work will be marked according to how well they achieve these learning outcomes and their final feedback will refer to each outcome, as well as providing an overall percentage grade.

On successful completion of the course, students will have the knowledge and skills to be able to succeed in the health and social care sector. They will develop communication skills and the ability to work as part of a team. Through teaching and assessment, students will have developed a range of digital skills that are required for work and be able to demonstrate a range of competencies. Students will be prepared for roles in a variety of health and social care settings, such as working with those experiencing mental health issues or domestic violence, tackling environmental health issues or in public health teams. Students will also be prepared for postgraduate study in areas such as nursing, social work or postgraduate teacher training.
