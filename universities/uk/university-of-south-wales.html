Overview

About University of South Wales

At the University of South Wales, it’s all about being bold, independent and fulfilling potential. It is one of the largest universities in the UK, offering students excellent opportunities and prospects. South Wales is an exceptional part of the UK, with city life, countryside and coast. With campuses located in Cardiff, Newport, Pontypridd and Dubai, 95% of graduates work or study within six months of graduation (DLHE 2016/17). All of these campuses are easily reached by road, rail and air, so wherever students come from, they’re never far from home.

A modern, ambitious university, the University of South Wales makes a real difference to the lives of students, staff and the communities which surround it. With campuses in Cardiff, Newport, Pontypridd and Dubai, the University is renowned for its partnerships with major employers. Their industry connections and emphasis on equipping students with real world experiences make their graduates among the most employable in the UK. And throughout everything they do, they strive to stay true to their core values of being Professional, Responsive, Inspiring and Creative.

Why The University of South Wales

  • Work Placements: Several courses offered at South Wales University guarantee work placements, while the Business and Management, Engineering, Computing and Science and Creative Industries faculties offer the opportunity to work in industry for a year.
  • Study Abroad: ERASMUS+ is the European Union programme for training, youth and sport offering exciting opportunities for participants to study, work, volunteer, teach and train abroad.
  • Employability: The University works hard to ensure that students are ready to start a successful career as soon as they graduate. They have even made employability part of the curriculum, with teaching informed by what happens in the real world and skills students will need for the workplace.
  • High Quality Education: Many courses at the University have been accredited by professional bodies. This means they are of the highest standard and can grant students exemptions from professional exams as an added extra.

Bachelor's · Business & Management

Bachelor of Arts (Honours) – Accounting and Finance

3 years – 4 years Next intake: Sep 2027 Apply by 1 Sep 2027

USW's multi-accredited BA (Hons) Accounting and Finance degree oﬀers learners the chance to develop and practice their technical knowledge, skills and expertise, while gaining professional recognition from the world’s leading accountancy bodies. Topics covered include financial reporting, management accounting, corporate finance, taxation, audit, and computerized accounting systems.

During the first year, students will covers subject like financial accounting, management accounting, an introduction to finance, quantitative techniques and data analysis, the business environment, and business and corporate law. They will have the chance to gain a CIMA-accredited certificate in Sage, which is the UK’s biggest accounting software provider.

The second year studies include financial reporting, performance measurement and control, audit and assurance, corporate finance, digital accounting and a specialist employability module where learners can develop their employability skills and work on live projects with real world businesses.

The final year explores advanced aspects of accounting and has specialist optional modules, including taxation, forensic accounting and financial trading and investment, where students can use USW's simulation trading room on campus. Upon choosing the cases in audit module, students will have the opportunity to work with a number of third sector organizations and carry out assurance engagements with actual clients from this sector.

Students can also apply for a one year work placement after their second year of study or short-term placement (up to three months) between their second and third year. Longer work placements are offered with a number of organizations, and in recent years this has included Atradius – one of the world’s largest credit insurers, Alcatel-Lucent – a global communications industry leader, and the Welsh Government.

The Association of Chartered Certified Accountants (ACCA) has fully accredited this degree course, so students can gain up to the maximum exemptions from a professional qualification when they graduate. USW is a recognised Institute of Chartered Accountants in England and Wales (ICAEW) 'Partner in Learning' and, depending on its module choices, learners can gain exemptions from ICAEW, with further exemptions offered towards the Chartered Institute of Management Accountants (CIMA) and Chartered Institute of Public Finance Accountants (CIPFA) examinations.

Bachelor of Laws (Honours) – Law

3 years Next intake: Sep 2027 Apply by 1 Sep 2027

The LLB (Hons) Law degree is a stimulating and challenging course that focuses on justice, technology and employability. It combines a balanced and stimulating legal education with practical skills and work-based learning. Students will gain knowledge of the law and its application in the real world, learn how to analyze and solve problems, and be able to present logical, reasoned arguments. This emphasis on skills means learners will be ready for the challenges of any career.

USW is a Top 50 Law School, according to Guardian League Tables, and prides itself on its excellent teaching and student support. Its LLB degree is recognised as a qualifying law degree (QLD) for professional purposes in the UK. It satisfies the academic stage of training set by the Solicitors Regulation Authority and the Bar Standards Board for progression to the vocational stage; the Legal Practice Course (LPC) to become a solicitor, or the Bar Professional Training Course (BPTC) to become a barrister.

Students will benefit from USW's award-wining Legal Advice Clinic, which provides opportunities for every law student to gain vital skills that they can take with them into the workplace. In addition to this, the University has strong connections with industry and host a range of guest speakers who are experts in their field, to provide context and real-life examples relating to the topics they study.

Teaching methods vary from traditional lectures to more clinical legal education and online simulations. Outside of the classroom, learners will have the opportunity to join the thriving USW Student Law Society and get involved in a range of sporting and networking activities. They could also visits the Houses of Parliament and the Supreme Court, to mooting classes and social activities.

USW works with the legal services sector to offer valuable work placements, where students can practice their new legal skills and understand the realities of the workplace. This placement will also contribute towards their legal degree. Firms involved in the scheme include Hugh James, Eversheds, Capital Law and many more. Students are also provided with many volunteering opportunities with a Regional Employment Judge, ELIPS (Employment Tribunal Litigant in Person Scheme), Shelter Cymru Volunteer Pathways Programme, Age Cymru Independent Volunteer Advocates and Cardiff Lawyers Care Homelessness Clinic.

Graduates progress into a variety of careers which range from Legal Technologists, Social Work, Police, Third Sector organizations, Business, Finance and HR to name a few possibilities.

Master of Business Administration – Global – Entrepreneurship

2 years Next intake: Feb 2027 Apply by 10 Dec 2026

The University of South Wales's MBA Global programme is a two-year MBA that offers students the opportunity to gain up to eight months work experience in the UK. The MBA Global programme builds on a strong ethos of helping managers and leaders solve complex business problems while developing professionals who will make a strategic difference within the context of their organizations.

It will develop leadership and strategic thinking skills required to build successful teams and navigate growth within an uncertain, resource-constrained and competitive business environment. Students will gain insights into how to prepare, plan and react efficiently to a rapidly changing business world.

The Entrepreneur in a Global Environment module is intended to develop students’ critical awareness of the key contexts in which entrepreneurs and their organizations operate in a global environment and of the strategies they utilize when analyzing and reacting to complex and often unpredictable circumstances.

Depending on the length of the internship or work-placement, this block allows the student time and scope to develop a professional project report based on their work-placement or consultancy project internship to highlight and report their impact on the respective client organizations. Contact time and independent study hours will vary across blocks.

USW’s Global Choices programme of talks, workshops and masterclasses will expose students to guest lectures and seminars conducted by industry and academic leaders. The University is host to a vibrant, multicultural environment that provides excellent opportunities for students to gain global perspectives on business and management.

As a USW MBA Global graduate, students will become part of a global alumni network. In addition to an MBA Global qualification, graduates will also be eligible to apply for the Chartered Management Institute (CMI) Level 7 Diploma in Strategic Management and Leadership.
