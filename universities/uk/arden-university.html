Overview

About Arden University

Arden University provides access to the skills, knowledge, and opportunities learners need to further their careers and achieve the success they deserve.

As an innovative and applied university, Arden’s cutting-edge digital learning platforms contain all the support students need to succeed. The University was recognised in the latest National Student Survey in 2025 as No 5 in the UK for student satisfaction and has more than 40,000 ambitious students learning with it. The university built its courses to reflect the needs of today’s students and employers, with many of its courses being developed hand in hand with modern business organisations.

Arden's industry accreditation and partnerships with institutions like the NHS, CMI, and CIPD provide students with the resources, insight, and opportunities to transform their careers and make a positive contribution to their communities.

Why Arden University - Manchester

  • Location: Located in one of the UK’s most exciting and diverse cities, Arden University’s Manchester campus offers everything students need to succeed in their studies and shape their future career. International students will find a warm welcome, modern learning spaces, and dedicated support to help them settle into the city. Manchester itself is one of the country’s most vibrant student cities, known for its diversity, culture, and friendliness, making it the perfect place to learn, grow, and feel at home.
  • Student Satisfaction: Arden University's MBA is ranked the Best MBA in the UK by the CEO Magazine, 2024 and 2025. The University is also ranked Top 5 in the UK for Student Experience by NSS 2025. Over 90% of their students agree that staff make their subject engaging, say their course is developing the skills they need for the future, and believe that the library resources fully support their learning.
  • Scholarships: Arden University believes that everyone, everywhere has the right to higher education. They understand that studying abroad can bring additional financial challenges. That’s why they offer a range of scholarships and regional funding options designed specifically for international students.
  • Learning Success Hub: Academic Skills Tutors at the Manchester campus are there to help students develop key academic skills for success. On-campus academic support is available every day, including face-to-face, online workshops, 1:1 sessions, dissertation support (pilot programme), and "On Track” drop-ins at key points in the academic year. Arden also offers collaboration with Peer Mentors to provide additional support when and where students need it.
  • Career Support: From the very first day, Arden University helps students plan and progress their careers. Whether students are working towards a promotion, building their CV, or exploring a new field, Arden's Careers Team provides expert guidance every step of the way. On the Manchester campus, they offer 1:1 careers appointments, weekly careers drop-ins, helping finding part-time jobs and work experience, employer events, access to specialist careers tools, and career and professional development workshops.

Master of Public Health

Arden’s Master of Public Health course has been developed to meet the increasing demand for graduates with a passion for managing and leading preventive health services in communicable and non-communicable diseases. The course places emphasis on providing students with the most current skills in healthcare and digital literacy in health services management, giving them the ability to support healthcare delivery and better health outcomes at population, community, and national level.

The course is designed to be flexible and contemporary in its structure and delivery, and has been developed in close collaboration with leading public health employers and industry advisory groups. This means students will be exposed to a variety of teaching approaches and assessment methods to support the development of knowledge and abilities in public health, helping students graduate with the specialised skills necessary for a successful career in the public health sector.

Master of Science – Cyber Security

Arden University's MSc Cyber Security programme is aligned with the NCSC academic framework and equips students with the skills necessary to thrive in highly lucrative cyber security roles. In recent studies, 57% of businesses said they currently have unfilled cyber security positions* , and 1.8 million jobs in the field went unfilled in 2022 alone**. With such huge skills gaps and a burgeoning demand for cyber experts across the globe, this course will arm students with the knowledge they need to excel as an expert in cyber security across almost limitless industries.

Arden's programme goes deep into cyber security, covering everything from understanding core concepts and standards to identifying vulnerabilities and risks within modern networks and infrastructures. Students will gain the expertise to design secure systems, making them resilient against potential attacks. Arden will also teach students the technical skills needed to become an ethical hacker and proactive security tester, as well as how to perform digital forensics and incident management.

Additionally, students will explore cloud, web, and mobile security concepts, becoming adept at recognising critical threats. Arden University emphasises hands-on learning, allowing students to apply essential security techniques to test and protect against real-world attacks and recommend improvements.

MBA · Business & Management

Master of Business Administration – Executive (MBA)

1 year Next intake: Feb 2027 Apply by 25 Jan 2027

The driving force behind Arden University’s Executive MBA is to help experienced managers disrupt conventional ways of thinking to lead business transformations. The programme equips students with the key skills needed for modern global business management, helping them gain a competitive edge in the industry. Rooted in a learning-by-doing methodology, the degree emphasises directly applying what students learn to their day-to-day role, allowing them to demonstrate immediate impact in their organisation. This, combined with Arden’s flexible learning, means this degree will complement students' careers, not get in the way of it.

With industries transforming faster than ever, organisations will require jobholders to be agile and tech-savvy. That’s why Arden has ensured this Executive MBA takes a digital-first, global, and contemporary approach to learning, ensuring students gain the modern strategic, financial, and people skills they need to meet the current and future demands of a constantly evolving sector. Arden's esteemed lecturers bring a wealth of experience from senior roles in both the private and public sectors, along with significant academic expertise. They have designed the course in consultation with leading UK employers and Arden's international alumni, so students can be confident their studies will provide them with the advanced skill set to become a future leader of tomorrow.

This course has dual accreditation from the Chartered Management Institute (CMI) and the Institute of Leadership and Management (ILM), providing students with the prestigious CMI Level 7 Diploma in Strategic Leadership and Management and ILM Level 7 Diploma in Leadership and Management. That means students will graduate with professional recognition and a qualification that’s recognised across the globe. And with the CMI Level 7 Diploma, students will be eligible for the fast-track route to Chartered Manager status.

The course is ideal for experienced managers who are looking to take their careers to the next level. Arden also offers MBA pathways focused on finance and artificial intelligence, as well as a specialised MBA to help those without work experience break into the world of business management.
