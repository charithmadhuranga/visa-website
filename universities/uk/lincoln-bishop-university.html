Overview

About Lincoln Bishop University (LBU)

Lincoln Bishop University, formerly Bishop Grosseteste University, has been educating students since 1862. It was first established as an Anglican teacher training college and continues to provide first-class education to high-calibre graduates who go on to positively change the world.

Lincoln Bishop provides a welcoming and inclusive environment where students can thrive academically, socially, and personally. It is one of the best universities in the country for student experience and teaching quality.

The University is in Lincoln, a city known for its beauty and historic charm. Its self-contained, green and leafy campus is the perfect base for a university life, with everything students need within convenient reach.

Why Lincoln Bishop University (LBU)

  • Location: Situated in the East Midlands, Lincoln boasts a large student population with lots on offer, from its picturesque streets to the vibrant nightlife – there’s something for everyone. Regarded as one of the most affordable cities for students in the UK, it is a financially attractive study destination. The city’s central location provides good transport links to other major UK cities and airports. Students will find direct trains to Leicester, Nottingham, Leeds, Sheffield, London, and more. Lincoln has a diverse culinary landscape, with independent pubs and restaurants, coffee shops, and tea rooms at students' disposal. Popular nearby attractions include Lincoln Cathedral, Lincoln Castle, and the Brayford Waterfront.
  • Facilities: Beautiful and green campus, filled with large open spaces, providing a natural and relaxing environment in which to live and work. On-campus cinema and performance space. Students can expect to enjoy movie screenings, public lectures, and live performances in the 230-seat venue. Quirky Alice-in-Wonderland-themed coffee shop 'Curiositea' where students can relax and enjoy a cuppa. Fitness centre, free for students and fully equipped with a spacious sports hall and gym, and expansive playing fields.
  • Employability: Employability rates of Lincoln Bishop graduates are consistently high. The Careers Readiness team is all about extending students' experience beyond their degree, ensuring their future is in safe hands. Lincoln Bishop helps students develop their career prospects through workshops and events, job fairs, and access to a network of employers, all of which aim to support students to build their skills and experience so they can stand out from the crowd.
  • Scholarships: Lincoln Bishop offers a range of financial support to international students, including government bursaries for selected courses.
  • Student Wellbeing: At Lincoln Bishop University, student well-being is the number one priority. The University provides comprehensive support, both personally and academically, from before students start right through to after graduation. The campus features dedicated quiet spaces, a secluded peace garden, meditation, prayer, and wellbeing spaces.

Bachelor's · Business & Management

Bachelor of Science (Honours) – Business Management

3 years Next intake: Jan 2027 Apply by 5 Dec 2026

The BSc (Hons) Business Management provides a broad and contemporary business education designed around the organisations and challenges of the future.

Students develop knowledge across management, leadership, people, strategy, enterprise and organisational performance, while building the practical and digital capabilities needed to succeed in a changing economy.

The degree takes a people-centred approach to management. It explores how effective leaders motivate and develop employees, build organisational cultures and respond to complex challenges.

Digital business, artificial intelligence, analytics and innovation are also central to understanding modern organisations. Students consider how technology is changing business models, decision-making, customer relationships and the workplace.

The degree is designed to maintain broad career options while giving students a strong business identity. Whether graduates pursue careers in management, consultancy, operations, entrepreneurship or other commercial fields, they will develop transferable skills that can be applied across a range of sectors.

Business involves more than understanding how individual functions operate. It also requires an understanding of how people, ideas, resources and decisions come together to create organisational performance. The degree provides a broad view of business while allowing students to explore contemporary issues shaping organisations.

Students examine leadership, organisational behaviour, strategy, people management, enterprise, digital business and responsible management. Artificial intelligence and analytics help them understand how technology is influencing decision-making and transforming business models.

Particular emphasis is placed on leadership and people. Successful organisations require managers who can communicate effectively, motivate employees, develop talent and create environments in which individuals and teams can perform successfully.

Responsible leadership is central to effective modern management. Throughout the degree, students explore ethics, responsible decision-making, sustainability and the wider impact of organisational activity.

Students consider how businesses can balance commercial performance with their responsibilities towards employees, customers, communities and the environment. Themes connected to the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals help them understand the contribution businesses can make to wider sustainable development.

The course combines a strong Lincolnshire identity with a global outlook. Students explore business challenges, markets and opportunities from local, national and international perspectives.

Lincolnshire’s diverse economy provides opportunities to understand organisations operating across agriculture and food, manufacturing, tourism, retail and professional services. Students also consider global supply chains, international competition, technological change and emerging markets.

The programme develops students’ ability to analyse complex business problems and turn ideas into practical recommendations. It therefore combines academic understanding with applied learning and professional development.

MBA · Business & Management

Master of Business Administration

1 year Next intake: Jan 2027 Apply by 5 Dec 2026

The Master of Business Administration at Lincoln Bishop University is a postgraduate programme designed to develop the strategic, analytical, and leadership capabilities required to manage organisations in a complex and rapidly changing global business environment. It is suitable for professionals, aspiring managers, entrepreneurs, and graduates seeking to advance into leadership or senior management roles.

The programme provides an integrated understanding of key business areas, which may include strategic management, leadership, marketing, finance, operations, human resource management, entrepreneurship, innovation, and responsible business practices. Through case studies, practical projects, group activities, presentations, and independent research, students learn to assess business challenges, make evidence-based decisions, and develop effective organisational strategies.

Students are expected to strengthen their abilities in critical thinking, problem-solving, communication, teamwork, and change management. The programme also explores contemporary issues such as digital transformation, sustainability, ethical leadership, globalisation, and the impact of technology on organisations.

A significant component of the MBA may involve an independent research project, dissertation, or consultancy-based project. This allows students to investigate a relevant business issue, apply appropriate research methods, and recommend practical solutions.
