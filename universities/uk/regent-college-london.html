About Regent College London (in partnership with University of Greater Manchester)

Regent College London was formed in 2010 as part of  Regent Group, an education, training, and investment management business founded by the Pankaj family. They share a rich and successful history in education, characterised by compact campuses, small, focused classes, and unparalleled support. The college initially opened in Wembley, delivering BTEC Higher National Diplomas and providing students with alternative progression routes into Higher Education. In 2013, they opened admissions to the public to reflect their commitment to widening access and participation. Since then, they have been delivering a range of HND business programmes validated by Pearson. They have also expanded their Higher Education provision, working with a collection of university partners to deliver Foundation Year Routes, Foundation Degrees, Bachelor’s Degrees, Top-up Degrees, and Master’s Degrees.

As a college, they have achieved many goals, such as a positive outcome for QAA Higher Education Review, excellent results in the National Student Survey, opening five campus locations across London, and of course, helping its students achieve their lifelong goals and aspirations.

Why Regent College London (in partnership with University of Greater Manchester)?

  • Partner University: Regent College London (RCL) is the London academic centre for the University of Greater Manchester. International students studying at RCL will graduate with a University of Greater Manchester degree. The University of Greater Manchester prides itself on being a teaching-intensive, research-informed institution and was recently awarded an overall Silver rating in the Teaching Excellence Framework 2023, with a Gold rating in Student Experience. The University of Greater Manchester is a modern university with a distinguished history and has a long and proud heritage that goes back to 1825.
  • Campus Locations: Regent College London have multiple campus locations across London which are home to experienced teachers, thriving communities and inspiring learning resources. Each study location is close to transport links and boasts modern facilities with intelligent and cutting-edge technology, providing everything students need throughout their student journey with them.
  • Careers and Employability Support: Whether students are looking to career transition, progress in their chosen sector or start a business, the Regent Careers team are there to support them every step of the way. The College offer career support throughout students' studies and even after they graduate where they can continue to access workshops, resources and individualised support through their lifelong Alumni Association.
  • Wellbeing and Support: Regent College London is committed to supporting students' wellbeing and providing supportive services alongside their studies. The Student Support team is here to provide confidential and personalised assistance tailored to students' individual needs. Whether they need help with academic challenges, mental health support, or guidance on personal matters, the team is there to support students at every step of the way. Collaboration with external organisations, such as Student Finance England, to provide guidance on support services like Student Disability Allowance( DSA) and Child Care Allowance as well.

Bachelor's · Health & Nursing

Bachelor of Science (Honours) – Health and Social Care

3 years Next intake: Jan 2027 Apply by 1 Jan 2027

The three-year BSc (Hons) Health and Social Care degree is designed to prepare students to make a positive impact on the daily lives of vulnerable people.

Delivered in excellent teaching facilities, the undergraduate programme provides opportunities for students to develop the values, practical skills and knowledge required for a successful career in the global health and social care sector.

Led by a highly experienced and dedicated teaching team, the programme explores key principles including professional values, ethical care practices, the promotion of physical and mental health, safeguarding, and the advocacy of diversity and inclusion. Its focus is on developing knowledgeable, compassionate and person-centred care professionals.

The BSc (Hons) Health and Social Care provides a strong academic pathway for students preparing to enter the health and social care workplace or progress to further study in a related academic or professional area.

The second year includes a placement module, giving students the opportunity to gain practical experience in a health and social care setting. The module focuses on the development of generic competencies, while enabling students to gain an understanding of workplace functions and structures. During the placement, students are required to maintain a written reflective log and are supported by a tutor who helps them demonstrate the learning gained through their practical experience.

The placement module is linked to an employability module, which focuses on transferable skills and career planning. This enables students to consider their personal and professional development throughout the programme and to prepare for future employment opportunities.

In the final year, students can expand their knowledge in specific areas of health and social care that are of particular interest to them. Available modules include children and families, mental health and safeguarding. These options provide insight into potential areas of specialisation and support the development of deeper knowledge and understanding.

The programme also includes a module examining vulnerability in relation to people who use health and social care services. Compassionate, person-centred care is considered throughout the degree, alongside the principles and practices required to support individuals effectively.

Students will also study research methods and approaches in health and social care. This includes the development of a research proposal, which will provide the foundation for the final research project or dissertation. This work enables students to develop knowledge and understanding in an area of personal interest, while preparing them for postgraduate study and demonstrating academic skills that can be applied in the workplace.

Learning through case studies, workshops and role play is embedded throughout the programme. These activities enable students to explore how theoretical and academic learning connects with practice in the health and social care sector.

Master's · Law & Criminology

Master of Laws (LLM) – International Business Law

1 year Next intake: Jan 2027 Apply by 1 Jan 2027

The programme is designed to enable students to specialise in International Business Law, equipping them with a comprehensive and critical understanding of the legal processes governing international business transactions. It covers key areas such as trade law, corporate governance, employment law, and international alternative dispute resolution, while also deepening knowledge of law as an academic discipline. Students develop an understanding of legal methods, concepts, and the broader social, political, and theoretical contexts in which legal systems operate.

The course enhances professional development by broadening students’ perspectives and strengthening their ability to analyse complex legal, economic, cultural, ethical, and political issues in a transnational context. It fosters advanced research and writing skills applicable across a wide range of sectors, including legal practice, policymaking, corporate environments, government, and academia. Students are trained to apply their knowledge creatively and systematically to address challenges in global trade and corporate governance, while building high-level transferable skills, critical self-awareness, and effective problem-solving and decision-making abilities.

A distinctive feature of the programme is its international focus, allowing students to develop advanced expertise in International Business Law alongside strategies for personal and professional growth. Teaching is delivered by experienced academic and industry professionals with strong links to public, private, and enterprise sectors. Students have the opportunity to undertake a traditional dissertation to further develop subject expertise, as well as attend guest lectures from leading specialists, providing valuable insight into contemporary professional practice. Employability skills are embedded throughout the curriculum to align with current industry demands.

Upon completion, graduates demonstrate a systematic understanding of key principles, practices, and emerging issues in International Business Law, as well as the employability skills required for career progression. They gain knowledge of the historical and ongoing influence of English law, the UN Convention on Contracts for the International Sale of Goods (CISG), and the regulatory framework of the World Trade Organization (WTO). Students also develop an understanding of the effectiveness and impact of legal mechanisms governing international trade, commercial transactions, and dispute resolution, alongside awareness of the ethical, social, and cultural contexts in which law operates.

The programme also develops advanced cognitive and intellectual skills, enabling students to critically analyse complex legal issues and apply sound judgement in evaluating contractual and trade mechanisms. Graduates are able to assess competing legal arguments, identify potential disputes, and interpret decisions from courts, arbitral bodies, and international organisations. They demonstrate critical awareness of the relationships between international, regional, and domestic legal frameworks, as well as the ability to conduct independent research with originality and authority. The programme culminates in the development of sophisticated analytical, evaluative, and innovative capabilities at Master’s level.
