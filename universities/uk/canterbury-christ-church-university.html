About Canterbury Christ Church University

Canterbury Christ Church University (CCCU) started out as a teacher training college in the 1960s in response to a national shortage of teachers. Today they continue to shape their courses and research around critical social issues both nationally and globally. In 2022, they celebrated their Diamond Jubilee, marking 60 years of making a positive difference to people’s lives, through education, research and innovation. From teacher training in the early 1960s, adding professional health courses in the 80s, and now new creative arts, science, engineering, technology and medicine for future generations, their story is one of relevance, impact and progress.

InUni, a part of the Global University Systems (GUS) and one of the leading global education partners to help institutions attract diverse and quality students, across the globe. They aim to democratise the world through higher education, and partner with universities to offer customised, quality-focused and end-to-end solutions to meet their specific needs.

Why Canterbury Christ Church University?

  • Location: Less than an hour from London, Canterbury is a lively student city with historic streets, ancient city walls and a vibrant atmosphere. The Medway Campus is in an area known as Chatham Maritime, a student-friendly quarter.
  • Facilities: A multi-million pound investment is transforming the University, including a centre for science, technology, health, and engineering. CCCU also home to the Kent and Medway Medical School and a stunning creative arts facility.
  • Student Support: Canterbury Christ Church University want to give students the best possible experience, from registration to graduation. The services are available to support students at whatever point they are in their university journey. Study support, money management, wellbeing support and more, CCCU is committed to working with students to ensure they reach their full potential.

Bachelor's · Law & Criminology

Bachelor of Laws – Law

3 years Next intake: Sep 2027 Apply by 1 Sep 2027

Study Law at Canterbury Christ Church and join a challenging and vibrant community, led by an experienced team of academics and practitioners, including solicitors, barristers and international lawyers.

This Law degree will enable students to progress to the vocational stage of lawyer’s training, to qualify as either a solicitor or barrister. With a combination of theory and practice, this original and innovative degree in Law will effectively challenge students and give them the opportunity to develop vital and transferable skills needed for a future career in Law and beyond.

Canterbury Christ Church is one of only a few universities across the country which offers a dual qualification to those who choose to take this path: an LLB and Membership of the National Association of Licensed Paralegals. This means that students will graduate with a GradNALP in addition to their LLB, which will enable students to be an official paralegal.

The teaching staff are specialists in a wide range of areas. From human rights and artificial intelligence to corporate and medical, students will learn alongside experts, many of whom are still practising.

Students will also have the option to get involved with practical justice, including opportunities to shadow a local judge and experience live mediations from the in-house Mediation Clinic. This practice will certainly highlight their professional experience and understanding, which will enhance their overall skillset. Through the strong links with the local community, Citizens Advice and the local courts, students will have the opportunity to act as a Community Legal Companion for unrepresented parties at court. This is an excellent opportunity for students to gain hands-on work experience in a legal setting, which will set them up for the world of work post-graduation.

Master's · Social Sciences & Psychology

Master of Arts – Social Work

2 years Next intake: Sep 2027 Apply by 1 Sep 2027

Individuals can advance their careers and make a meaningful impact on society with the MA Social Work program. This two-year, full-time course equips students with the knowledge, skills, and professional experience needed to support and protect vulnerable individuals at all stages of life. Fully accredited by Social Work England (SWE), the course integrates academic study, hands-on skills training, and extensive practice placements to ensure that students develop the expertise required for effective and ethical social work practice.

Through engaging lectures, seminars, and practical training, students will learn from academic tutors, experienced practitioners, and individuals with lived experience of social services. The state-of-the-art facilities, including the Hydra simulated skills suite and courtroom, provide invaluable opportunities for students to refine their abilities in case analysis, report writing, and court proceedings.

In addition to 30 skills development days, students will undertake two significant practice placements—70 days in the first year and 100 days in the second—working in diverse settings to gain hands-on experience with children, families, vulnerable adults, and individuals with mental health needs.

At the heart of this course is a commitment to social justice and inclusion. Students will explore the impact of structural inequalities, social policies, and intersecting factors that affect individuals’ well-being. Through research and reflective practice, they will challenge preconceptions in a supportive environment, developing the listening and advocacy skills essential for empowering those in need.

Upon successful completion, students will be eligible to apply for registration with Social Work England (SWE), opening the door to a fulfilling and impactful career. They are encouraged to join the program and take the next step in shaping a fairer, more inclusive society.
