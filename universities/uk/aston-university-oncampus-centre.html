About Aston University ONCAMPUS Centre

ONCAMPUS Aston is based on the campus of Aston University located in the city of Birmingham. As a top 30 UK university, Aston was the pioneer of the placement year and is renowned for its focus on employability and entrepreneurship.

Aston has been named as University of the Year 2020 (The Guardian) and awarded Outstanding Entrepreneurial University 2020 (Times Higher Education). Aston Business School has also been ranked in the top 100 European Business Schools by the Financial Times in 2020.

Students who study at ONCAMPUS Aston are guaranteed progression to Aston University following the successful completion of a Foundation Programme.

Why Aston University - ONCAMPUS

  • Internationally Recognized: Aston University was awarded University of the Year by The Guardian (2020), Outstanding Entrepreneurial University by Times Higher Education (2020), and awarded the Teaching Excellence Framework (TEF) Gold for Teaching Excellence.
  • Location: Aston University is located in Birmingham, a diverse, multi-cultural hub for business and industry that has been recognized as the most diverse entrepreneurial region by Start-up Britain. Birmingham is also located less than 90 minutes from London and Manchester and has great links to Europe with the airport located just a 10 minute train ride from the city.
  • Facilities and Campus Life: Aston offers a wide range of sports and fitness facilities, including a 25m swimming pool, a state of the art gym, sports halls, grass pitches, and all-weather pitches. The school also offers over 130 student-led groups, meaning there is something to appeal to any and all interests!
  • Guaranteed Progression: Students who study at ONCAMPUS Aston are guaranteed progression to Aston University upon successful completion of a Foundation Programme.

Student life & support

Work while studying

International students can work part-time alongside their studies (subject to visa conditions).

Co-op & placements

Co-op options are limited — ask us about placement-friendly alternatives.

On-campus housing

Students typically arrange off-campus or homestay accommodation.

Accommodation options

Students need to plan their accommodations well in advance of their arrival as all accommodation options fill up quickly, well before the start of the semester.

On-Campus Residence Accommodations

On-Campus residence accommodations are provided per discretion by the school.

Off-Campus Accommodations

The local area has a variety of off-campus rental housing options including single homes, duplexes, apartments and rooms for rent. Students wishing to live off-campus need to research availability on their own and should arrive well before the start of term to do so.

Homestay

There are a wide variety of homestay options available, and our partners do their best to match students and hosts according to their interests and preferences. Homestay hosts include single people, young couples with children and pets, and older couples. All homestay accommodations have been inspected, and all adults in the home have completed a required criminal reference check.
