Overview

About University of Liverpool International College

The University of Liverpool International College (UoLIC) offers pathway courses in partnership with the University of Liverpool, and is situated on the University campus. The College is fully equipped to help students follow their path to the prestigious University of Liverpool, with dedicated and experienced staff ready to support them every step of the way. Students can now study in a brand new, state-of-the-art College building with integrated accommodation, which opened in January 2020.

Based on University of Liverpool's campus in Liverpool, the College is fully integrated with the University and UoLIC students are given full access to university facilities while they prepare for their degree. 51 nationalities were represented by new students at the College in 2020. UoLIC offers courses to prepare students for both undergraduate and postgraduate degrees. The Foundation Certificate at University of Liverpool International College is designed to prepare students for entry to an undergraduate degree in a number of subject areas. UoLIC also offer a pathway for a Pre-Master's course to prepare international students for studying at postgraduate level at the University of Liverpool.

Why University of Liverpool International College

  • Rewarding Experience: Staff at the College make sure students get the most out of their experience studying abroad. They'll arrange trips where students can make new friends and see more of the city, and they'll be there to support students at every stage of their path to university.
  • Cultural Hub: Liverpool is a major cultural hub for the north of England. The University has adopted the same international and cultural diversity as the city itself. Liverpool thrives off its multicultural community. Students will be able to enjoy fantastic art galleries and museums like Tate Liverpool and the Beatles Story Museum, as well as many independent shops and cafes. It's a great place to be an international student.
  • Accommodation: When students study at the University of Liverpool International College, they can stay at the new Kaplan Living residence in the same building. Students will have their own room with a study space and bathroom, plus shared social spaces. Students couldn't be closer to their classes.

MBA · Business & Management

2-Term Pathway – Pre-Master's for Business, Economics and Finance – Continue to Master of Business Administration – Football Industries

1.5 years – 22 months Next intake: Jan 2027 Apply by 1 Jan 2027

Program Description

The University of Liverpool Management School is one of an elite group of institutions worldwide to hold the gold standard 'triple-crown' accreditation from AACSB, AMBA and EQUIS. The MBA programme is ranked amongst the top 100 MBA programmes in the world featuring in The Economist 'Which MBA?' top 100 2019 global ranking, and achieved Tier 1 Status in CEO Magazine’s 2020 Global MBA Rankings. The Football Industries MBA is ranked at number 18 in the SportBusiness Postgraduate Course Rankings for 2020.

The MBA Football Industries (FIMBA) programme is the original, and still the only, Football industries MBA. As an MBA programme, it has the real business focus that management positions in modern sport demand. Focusing on the professionalism and business of the beautiful game, if students want to work in the global football industry— with clubs, governing bodies or related marketing, media and sponsorship companies— this is the MBA programme for them. The core elements of the programme—leadership, strategy, organizations, innovation, international business and the global environment, entrepreneurship and managing financial resources—are delivered in concentrated blocks to encourage a deeper understanding, with a strong emphasis on interactive teaching and learning. The specialist football modules apply business concepts to modern football. Students will gain a critical awareness of issues in football management, finance and law.

Student life & support

Work while studying

International students can work part-time alongside their studies (subject to visa conditions).

Co-op & placements

Co-op options are limited — ask us about placement-friendly alternatives.

On-campus housing

Students typically arrange off-campus or homestay accommodation.

Accommodation options

Students need to plan their accommodations well in advance of their arrival as all accommodation options fill up quickly, well before the start of the semester.

On-Campus Residence Accommodations

On-Campus residence accommodations are provided per discretion by the school.

Off-Campus Accommodations

The local area has a variety of off-campus rental housing options including single homes, duplexes, apartments and rooms for rent. Students wishing to live off-campus need to research availability on their own and should arrive well before the start of term to do so.

Homestay

There are a wide variety of homestay options available, and our partners do their best to match students and hosts according to their interests and preferences. Homestay hosts include single people, young couples with children and pets, and older couples. All homestay accommodations have been inspected, and all adults in the home have completed a required criminal reference check.
