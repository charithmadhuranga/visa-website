Overview

About Bournemouth University International College

Bournemouth University International College offers pathway courses and Pre-Sessional English programmes in partnership with Bournemouth University, and is located on the University campus. The College is the ideal setting for degree preparation. Students will benefit from state-of-the-art facilities and expert teaching, as well as comfortable accommodation attached to the College building as students start their path degree success. Bournemouth University is creative, enterprising and international. Located on England’s beautiful south coast, it has state-of-the-art facilities that help students get the most from their studies. 39 nationalities were represented by new students at the College in 2020.

Bournemouth University International College has 3 main types of pathway course: Foundation Certificate, International Year One and Pre-Master’s. The College also offers Pre-Sessional English courses if students already meet BU’s academic entry requirements.

Why Bournemouth University International College (Kaplan International Pathways)

  • Location: Bournemouth is a seaside town with 11km of sandy beaches and beautiful surrounding countryside. The College's convenient location is ideal for shopping and socializing with friends, playing outdoor sports, and visiting local attractions.
  • Staff Support: Bournemouth's friendly staff help students with every aspect of student life, both inside and outside the classroom, and organize many different activities, events and trips for students to enjoy.
  • Outstanding Accommodation: The College building gives students the ideal study abroad experience. As well as excellent learning and social facilities, the College has its own student residence on the upper floors, with stylish, modern apartments offering privacy and comfort.

Student life & support

Work while studying

International students can work part-time alongside their studies (subject to visa conditions).

Co-op & placements

Offers co-op or work-placement options in selected programs.

On-campus housing

Students typically arrange off-campus or homestay accommodation.

Accommodation options

Students need to plan their accommodations well in advance of their arrival as all accommodation options fill up quickly, well before the start of the semester.

On-Campus Residence Accommodations

On-Campus residence accommodations are provided per discretion by the school.

Off-Campus Accommodations

The local area has a variety of off-campus rental housing options including single homes, duplexes, apartments and rooms for rent. Students wishing to live off-campus need to research availability on their own and should arrive well before the start of term to do so.

Homestay

There are a wide variety of homestay options available, and our partners do their best to match students and hosts according to their interests and preferences. Homestay hosts include single people, young couples with children and pets, and older couples. All homestay accommodations have been inspected, and all adults in the home have completed a required criminal reference check.
