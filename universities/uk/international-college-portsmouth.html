About International College Portsmouth (ICP)

Studying at International College of Portsmouth and then the University of Portsmouth is going to be one of the most exciting times of a student's life. A degree from the University of Portsmouth will offer students the best learning experience and industry exposure. Students can choose from a range of pathway programs designed to prepare them for the next step in their studies.

ICP is an Associate College of the University of Portsmouth founded in 2009. ICP is a member of the global Navitas Group, a respected leader in global higher education partnered with more than 120 colleges and campuses worldwide. ICP provides multiple pathways to a broad range of degrees, in an environment that encourages students to achieve their best in all aspects of the university journey.

Why International College Portsmouth (ICP)

  • Location: Only 90 minutes from central London by train, Portsmouth is one of the sunniest cities in the country. ICP is in the heart of Portsmouth so students are never too far away from the city centre, or even the beautiful beaches along the south coast of England. It is a campus based college part of the University of Portsmouth city centre campus, just a five-minute walk from the main railway station (Portsmouth & Southsea) and within easy reach of the beach.
  • Excellence: The University of Portsmouth is a winner of the prestigious 'Gold' rating in the Teaching Excellence Framework (TEF) – one of only four Gold-rated universities in the south east.
  • Success: Over 4,000 ICP students have progressed to the University of Portsmouth since 2009.

Student life & support

Work while studying

International students can work part-time alongside their studies (subject to visa conditions).

Co-op & placements

Offers co-op or work-placement options in selected programs.

On-campus housing

Students typically arrange off-campus or homestay accommodation.

Accommodation options

Students need to plan their accommodations well in advance of their arrival as all accommodation options fill up quickly, well before the start of the semester.

On-Campus Residence Accommodations

On-Campus residence accommodations are provided per discretion by the school.

Off-Campus Accommodations

The local area has a variety of off-campus rental housing options including single homes, duplexes, apartments and rooms for rent. Students wishing to live off-campus need to research availability on their own and should arrive well before the start of term to do so.

Homestay

There are a wide variety of homestay options available, and our partners do their best to match students and hosts according to their interests and preferences. Homestay hosts include single people, young couples with children and pets, and older couples. All homestay accommodations have been inspected, and all adults in the home have completed a required criminal reference check.
