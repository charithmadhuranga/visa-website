About Bournemouth University

Bournemouth University is an innovative and vibrant public university located in Bournemouth, Dorset, a coastal town famous for its seven-mile stretch of sandy beaches. The town of Bournemouth has a population of roughly 500,000 and is a thriving hub for several industries including the creative, financial, and tourism sectors. Bournemouth University boasts a strong student community with over 19,000 students and more than 2,600 international students. Bournemouth University offers students many innovative and industry-focused programs, with particular strengths in media and communication and technology. Students will benefit from close ties to industry and many of BU's programs offer industry accreditation or placements with over 1,500 industry partners.

Talbot Campus is the main campus for Bournemouth University and hosts the main University buildings including the student union and the main library. It has had many recent updates (2017-2018) and is a self-contained campus located on the boundary of Bournemouth and Poole.

Why Bournemouth University - Talbot

  • Campus: Talbot Campus is the main campus for Bournemouth University, and has many of the flagship facilities including the Sir Michael Cobham Library, and the Student Centre.
  • Economic Region: Bournemouth is home to UK's fastest growing digital economy, and one of the UK's largest financial centres outside of London.
  • Facilities: Talbot Campus houses the main library and student union, alongside SportBU - a fully equipped sport centre and gym.
  • Culture: Bournemouth is a cosmopolitan, student-friendly town with a relaxed, beach-side lifestyle. Bournemouth is home to AFC Bournemouth, one of England's newest Premiership football teams and BU's official sport partner.

Note: Document verification is mandatory for all applicants applying to Bournemouth University.

Bachelor's · Hospitality & Tourism

Bachelor of Arts (Honours) – International Tourism and Hospitality Management

3 years Next intake: Sep 2028 Apply by 1 Sep 2028

Program Description

Underpinned by long-standing and internationally recognized research in tourism and hospitality, this Bournemouth University degree is suitable for those with career aspirations related to global hospitality and the wider travel and tourism industry. It will provide students with an understanding of the origins, development and organization of the tourism and hospitality industries, and the opportunities and challenges facing organizations operating in and across the various sectors.

The accommodation sector continues to grow rapidly. Year after year, global revenues and occupancy rates have continued to increase, as have international passenger arrivals. Helping to sustain this growth means that many new jobs – as well as small and medium-sized enterprises – have been created in the accommodation, food and drink, transport, visitor attractions and travel distribution sectors.

Social and technological changes have created new opportunities in these markets, many of which are threats to the existing organizations. In order to succeed, businesses need managers that understand how to compete and collaborate with others in a dynamic and diverse group of industries.

Students will graduate with the skills those businesses are looking for through studying a combination of tourism and hospitality with a specific focus on international issues, students will learn what is needed not just to survive in such an environment, but to thrive in it.

Bachelor's · Social Sciences & Psychology

Bachelor of Arts (Honours) – Social Work

3 years Next intake: Sep 2028 Apply by 1 Sep 2028

Program Description

This course is approved by Social Work England which means students will be eligible to apply for registration as a social worker on successful completion of the course, explaining why 95% of BU graduates are employed within six months of graduating.

Social workers practice in areas including child protection, fostering and adoption, adult safeguarding, mental health, substance use, hospital discharge and disability.

This practical degree, which is one of the top ten of its kind according to the 2018 Guardian League tables, blends a powerful combination of academic study with essential practical experience.

As a result, graduates will leave BU equipped with specialist knowledge and practical skills needed to deal with a broad range of issues such as social exclusion and discrimination, law and social policy, psycho-social development, models and methods of intervention and research methods.

Students will gain lots of relevant experience during their placements, operating in real-world settings. What’s more, they can even seize the opportunity to travel and engage in international activities.

Master's · Arts, Media & Design

Master of Arts – Producing Film and Television

1 year Next intake: Sep 2027 Apply by 1 Aug 2027

Program Description

This Masters is for students who want to complement their ideas and aspirations with the practical skills needed to make film and television programs. Whether students plan to join the production team of a film crew, shape a career as a researcher, associate producer or development executive, or even run their own independent production company, this course will give students the practical training and theoretical grounding to help them achieve their ambitions in the dynamic world of media production.

As a creative producer, graduates will learn how to develop their ideas as viable projects and sell them in a highly competitive marketplace. Thanks to expert tuition from professionals with experience from across industry, graduates will also become accomplished in the art of production management.

Working collaboratively across the year with other students in related subjects (directing, editing, sound design, screen writing and cinematography), students will plan and produce their own projects and learn how to work collaboratively within a creative environment. The final production project will serve as evidence of their abilities to develop, plan and manage a project of their choice, be that a short film, a business plan for a production company or a development plan for a TV series – the choice is up to the student. There is also an option to develop an alternative research-focused project if students prefer.

Students may have an undergraduate qualification in a related subject or may be able to show their suitability for this program of study through associated work-experience or evidence of and outputs from other related activities.

Students will have the chance to take advantage of opportunities with RedBalloon, an award winning professional production unit based in the Faculty of Media and Communication.

Master's · Law & Criminology

Master of Law – International Tax Law

1 year Next intake: Sep 2028 Apply by 1 Sep 2028

Program Description

The LLM International Tax Law is aimed at those considering a career in international tax consultancy or within in-house tax departments of multinational companies. It is also highly suitable for staff of foreign government finance ministries and tax authorities who wish to learn more about tax policy.

The Stage 2 taught units can be used as preparation for an important qualification offered by the Chartered Institute of Tax, the Advanced Diploma in International Tax (Paper I, Principles), whilst the dissertation can be structured so as to be suitable for submission as a thesis in Paper II or Paper III of the same internationally recognized qualification.

The course draws on Bournemouth University's academic excellence in international taxation and public law. It includes a wide range of tax and law modules that are focused on comparative, rather than on UK law, so applications from international students are encouraged.

Tax issues are relevant in tax and non-tax areas alike, such as competition law and family law, within its remit of marriage, divorce and estates. International business transactions and the management of state entities are both subject to the ramifications of UK and international tax.

Why study a Master of Business Administration at Bournemouth University?

The MBA at Bournemouth University is a carefully designed course that prepares students to tackle the realities of the modern business world, incorporating emerging technologies and aligning with the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (UN SDGs).

Students have the opportunity to advance their leadership and managerial skills through consultancy projects and industry engagements, allowing them to apply their knowledge in real-world business contexts.

The programme builds confidence in addressing complex business challenges through innovative and creative assessments.

BU fosters a community of lifelong learning, with students gaining associate membership to respected organisations such as AACSB, BGA, CMI, and IDM.

With academic expertise drawn from across Bournemouth University’s Faculties, the MBA programme is continuously evolving to remain at the forefront of change and advancement across all sectors. The course serves as a strong foundation for students aiming to build and progress in a managerial career.

  • Director
  • Lead Project Manager
  • Head of Economic Development Services
  • Head of Information and Systems Development

  • Marketing

Student life & support

Work while studying

International students can work part-time alongside their studies (subject to visa conditions).

On-campus housing

Students typically arrange off-campus or homestay accommodation.

Accommodation options

Students need to plan their accommodations well in advance of their arrival as all accommodation options fill up quickly, well before the start of the semester.

On-Campus Residence Accommodations

On-Campus residence accommodations are provided per discretion by the school.

Off-Campus Accommodations

The local area has a variety of off-campus rental housing options including single homes, duplexes, apartments and rooms for rent. Students wishing to live off-campus need to research availability on their own and should arrive well before the start of term to do so.
