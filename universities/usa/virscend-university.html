About Virscend University

Virscend University is part of Virscend Education Company Limited schools. Virscend Education Co Ltd. currently has a total of 29 educational institutions, with a total student population of 58,000. Virscend Education Co. Ltd. is the largest private educational organization in southern China with nationwide recognition for its innovative and high-performing graduates. Many schools of Virscend Education Co. Ltd. have placed students in renowned universities in China and all over the world, which include Beijing University, Tsinghua University, Harvard University, MIT, UC-Berkeley, UC-Irvine, CSU-Long Beach, CSUF, UCLA, and many others.

As part of Virscend Education Co. Ltd., Virscend University aspires to fulfill its parent company’s commitment to quality education. Virscend University's mission is to inspire students with innovative ideas and business intelligence to prepare them to excel in a dynamic global business environment. The university aspires to attain its mission by offering small-size classes taught by experienced professors and leading industry professionals.

Why Virscend University?

  • Location: Virscend University is located in Irvine, California. Irvine is one of the nation's largest planned urban communities and encompasses more than 65 square miles. Irvine — centrally located in the heart of Orange County — is adjacent to one of America's most passenger-friendly international airports with dozens of direct flights daily. While students are here, they get to experience award-winning, globally inspired cuisine, eclectic entertainment venues, diverse shopping options, beautiful nature experiences and a variety of great golf courses.
  • Experienced Faculty: Virscend University's experienced, well-educated, and diverse faculty is one of the central pillars of the institution. All of their faculty teach at well-known, accredited, and respected universities from the Southern California area such as Pepperdine, Chapman, Brandman, CSUs and UC schools. More than 90 percent hold Ph.Ds. in their field, actively publish research, and speak at conferences around the world. While the majority of their faculty are Asian, they are proud of the diversity of their faculty as most nationalities are represented.
  • Scholarships: Virscend University offers five types of scholarships for qualified students in order to make quality education accessible for everyone. These include Presidential, Academic, Professional, Merit and International scholarships with different eligibilities and benefits.

MBA · Business & Management

Master of Business Administration (MBA)

2 years Next intake: Jan 2027 Apply by 1 Dec 2026

The MBA program provides students with a strong foundation in content and competencies that will support their development as effective managers in many different types of settings. Students develop and utilize analytical tools to assist in decision making, to acquire organizational skills, to assist in their effective implementation of operational policies, to acquire a body of knowledge in the functional areas of business so that they develop leadership and supervisory skills, communication skills, and an understanding of the role of technology as an aid to effective management.

This program is STEM designated and designed for the working professional. There are 10 courses total ranging from digital marketing to data analytics and management strategy. This is a hybrid program that can be completed in 2 years as a full-time student. In-person classes will be held on campus in Irvine, CA. The University offer convenient nighttime classes to accommodate students who maintain full-time jobs.

Program Learning Outcomes

  • Critical Thinking and Research Analytics - Students will identify potential business opportunities, analyze competitive advantages, and apply fundamental strategy frameworks by critical thinking and applying proper problem-solving skills
  • Business Ethics and Corporate Governance - Students will develop awareness and knowledge of the ethical decision making in all aspects of corporate governance
  • Interpersonal &Team Skills - Students will develop their interpersonal skills by working collaboratively to integrate diverse viewpoints
  • Business Functions - Students will apply integrative business knowledge to solve problems
  • Quantitative & Technical Skills - Students will utilize quantitative skills and analytical reasoning to analyze and interpret data to improve business performance
  • Domestic & Global Environment - Students will learn to evaluate the domestic and globally competitive market and apply the relevant concepts to facilitate efficient globalized operation
