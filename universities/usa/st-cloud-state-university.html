Overview

About St. Cloud State University

Since its establishment in 1869, St. Cloud State University has revolutionized what it means to be a state university through constant innovation and providing a rich learning experience to students. St. Cloud State University offers an impressive selection of programs to suit each student’s individual goals, in addition to specialty schools in business, science and engineering, liberal arts, and more.

St. Cloud State University’s mission is centered around encouraging active discovery, applied knowledge, and creative interaction. Upon graduation, students are prepared for life, work, and beyond, and have a set of transferable skills to positively transform their communities.

Why St. Cloud State University

  • Location: St. Cloud University is located on a beautiful 100-acre campus in Minnesota’s city of St. Cloud, which is home to several gardens, parks, a history museum, and more. The university is close to the Mississippi river in addition to a variety of off-campus and on-campus buildings, and is just an hour away from the Twin Cities.
  • Student Support: St. Cloud University is known for providing an outstanding student experience. Students can meet new people and get involved in nearly 250 clubs and organizations at St. Cloud University. Many community service opportunities and events are available as well, so students can make a positive impact beyond the university campus.
  • Facilities: From the Kiehle Visual Arts Center, to the James W. Miller Learning Resources Center, to the Integrated Science and Engineering Laboratory Facility, students have access to a variety of spaces to learn about their interests. Students can also access the Atwood Memorial Center, which is home to a variety of student resources and study spaces. Finally, St. Cloud State University has an impressive set of sports and recreation facilities for students to enjoy.
  • Accommodation: All students, with a few exceptions, live on campus in their first year, and carry a meal plan for at least two semesters. A variety of on-campus housing options are available, and it gives students a greater connection to classes, student resources, and a supportive community.
  • Academic Excellence: In addition to offering programs in a variety of subjects, St. Cloud State University’s small class sizes and knowledgeable professors set students up for success in their careers and beyond. St. Cloud State University is also recognized by various Colleges of Distinction for its programs in Business, Nursing, Education, and Engineering.

Bachelor of Science – Computer Science

4 years Next intake: Aug 2027 Apply by 1 Jul 2027

The Bachelor of Science - Computer Science program provides students with a broad competence to take on any role in the IT sector. The program core lays down all the essentials for a strong foundation in Computer Science. Electives provide them with breadth in applications and advanced topics, and the concentrations enable them to go in depth into high-demand specializations. Areas of employment include software development as well as other jobs as a computing professional.

Students who major in computer science will find themselves with the skills that drive advances in tomorrow's business, industry and homes. They will be taught by a diverse faculty whose global research and reputations emphasize focus on the latest computer science advancements. They will start with theoretical and practical application courses such as programming, data structures, operating systems, problem solving, digital logic, data algorithms and ethics. Later, they will choose specialization courses such as artificial intelligence, distributed systems, parallel computing, networks, computer security, database systems, programming languages, advanced computer architecture and software engineering.

The program also offers:

  • Well-equipped, updated laboratories.
  • Laboratory projects that are considered graduate-level work.
  • Industry ties for internships and jobs.
  • Student-faculty collaboration on research with immediate applications.

Bachelor of Science – General Business

4 years Next intake: Aug 2027 Apply by 1 Jul 2027

In Bachelor of Science - General Business, students are entering a global field where information, economics and people collide. They will be prepared to enter and lead in a variety of industries. Bachelor's · Computing & IT

Bachelor of Science in Engineering – Software Engineering

4 years Next intake: Aug 2027 Apply by 1 Jul 2027

The Bachelor of Science in Engineering - Software Engineering program prepares individuals to apply scientific and technological principles to analyze, design, implement, verify and validate, deliver, and maintain software systems for communications, business, medical, industrial, military, aerospace, scientific, and general computing applications.

The Software Engineering curriculum has been developed in collaboration with industry and evolves best technological practices. It utilizes a project-driven curriculum, establishing a new model for software engineering education. Upon graduation, students will be able to:

  • demonstrate an understanding of and apply current theories, models, and techniques that provide a basis for the software lifecycle;
  • apply the software engineering lifecycle by demonstrating competence in communication, planning, analysis, design, construction, and deployment;
  • demonstrate an ability to use the techniques and tools necessary for engineering practice.

Accommodation options

A variety of on-campus housing options are available to students, including traditional residence halls, renovated residence halls, and both unfurnished and furnished apartments. Living on campus provides convenience, easy access to student resources and support systems, and a unique and fun opportunity. Additionally, with many different housing options available, each student will be able to find something that suits their unique lifestyle.

All incoming first year students live on campus and carry a residential meal plan for two semesters, with some exceptions for students who are:

  • living with a legal guardian within 35 miles of campus
  • guardians of a minor child or dependent
  • married, or
  • younger than 16 or older than 21
