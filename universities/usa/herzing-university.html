About Herzing University (HU)

Established in 1965, Herzing University is a private, non-profit American university offering degrees worldwide at campuses throughout the United States and through its online programs. Herzing University offers experienced faculty, contemporary course content, and a global educational perspective.

The United States is recognized as having the most distinguished university degree system in the world. As part of that system, Herzing University offers degree programs that help students:

  • Grow intellectually
  • Develop personally
  • Think analytically
  • Communicate effectively
  • Achieve career goals and personal goals

Herzing University offers a wide variety of convenient, flexible, career-focused undergraduate and graduate degree programs. Herzing delivers its innovative programs on-campus and online in a caring, supportive environment that fosters both academic and professional success.

Herzing University's international students are valued members of the Herzing University family, and Herzing is committed to assist its students every step of the way as they pursue their U.S. degree and their career goals.

Top disciplines at Herzing university include Business, Management, Data Analytics, Business Analytics, Health Care, Project Management, and IT.

Herzing University was ranked #60 in Social Mobility by US News & World Report 2024.

Why Herzing University

  • Campus: Located just north of Atlanta, Herzing University’s Atlanta campus is a vibrant hub for international students from all corners of the globe. Herzing University prides itself on the swift processing of applications and a warm, supportive approach upon arrival of its students, ensuring they feel comfortable and welcomed in the dynamic and culturally rich city of Atlanta, Georgia.
  • Scholarships: Students who meet Herzing's academic and English language requirements for direct admission are eligible for undergraduate scholarships of $40,000 and graduate scholarships of $20,000.
  • Work Opportunities: OPT is 1 year and a STEM extension of 2 years is available for STEM programs, so a total 3 years of work opportunities are available to students. CPT is also available.
  • Academic Excellence in a Supportive Environment: Herzing University's on-campus programs are designed to nurture both academic and professional growth. The campus features modern, bright classrooms, enhanced study areas, and state-of-the-industry IT/Networking labs, providing an optimal setting for both lectures and hands-on learning. The University's dedicated faculty and staff are committed to fostering a caring and supportive environment, ensuring they have the resources and encouragement needed to succeed.
  • Flexible, Career-Focused Programs: The University's career-focused approach prepares students for success in the professional world, making Herzing University the ideal choice for international students seeking a high-quality education in a supportive setting.
  • Diverse Community: Become part of a diverse and inclusive community at Herzing University’s Atlanta Campus. Here, students will find not just an educational institution, but a place where they can thrive academically, professionally, and personally.
  • Timely Application Processing: Average time for I-20 acceptance is 5-7 days and average time to process an offer letter is 3-5 days. Herzing also does not require a deposit for the I-20.

Bachelor's · Computing & IT

Bachelor of Science – Information Technology (STEM-Designated Program)

4 years Next intake: May 2027 Apply by 23 Mar 2027

This program prepares students for a broad range of positions in the field of information technology. Foundational skills taught include end-user support, client/server operating systems, network infrastructure administration, security, scripting, database fundamentals, cloud computing, virtualization, data storage and technical communication. Advanced skills include database design, project management, information technology management and business systems analysis. Coursework in this program is designed to provide a foundation for select industry certifications.

Students will have an opportunity to complete their studies through a selection of elective coursework that deepens their understanding of general information technology concepts.

Upon completion of this program, the student should be able to:

  • Use information systems for the collection, organization, and delivery of data.
  • Express relevant information to technical and nontechnical audiences.
  • Design secure network infrastructures for physical and virtual environments.
  • Create information technology solutions based on business needs or requirements.
  • Demonstrate the principles of information technology security.
  • Apply industry standards to the implementation and support of network systems and computer devices.
