Overview

About University of Central Arkansas

The University of Central Arkansas, a leader in 21st-century higher education, is committed to excellence through the delivery of outstanding undergraduate and graduate education that remains current and responsive to the diverse needs of those it serves. The university’s faculty and staff promote the intellectual, professional, social, and personal development of its students through innovations in learning, scholarship, and creative endeavors. Students, faculty, and staff partner to create strong engagement with the local, national, and global communities. The University of Central Arkansas dedicates itself to academic vitality, integrity, and diversity. The University of Central Arkansas aspires to be a premier learner-focused public comprehensive university, a nationally recognized leader for its continuous record of excellence in undergraduate and graduate education, scholarly and creative endeavors, and engagement with local, national, and global communities.

Why University of Central Arkansas

  • Community: The University of Central Arkansas values and respects the people who make up its community—students, faculty, and staff—as well as the people connected to the school through ties to the local community and region, the state of Arkansas, our nation, and the world.
  • Intellectual Excellence: The school believes in lifelong intellectual development of students, faculty, and staff. The University of Central Arkansas is committed to the free pursuit of knowledge and continuous growth in learning and teaching.
  • Educated Citizens: The University of Central Arkansas believes in student success and in preparing students to engage complex issues and express informed opinion through critical thinking, writing, and speech. Given this institution’s historical roots in teacher education, this foundation inspires all of the colleges to work together to ensure our faculty and students promote instructional excellence and lifelong learning.
  • Diversity: The school is dedicated to attracting and supporting a diverse student, faculty and staff population and enhanced multicultural learning opportunities. They value the opportunity to work, learn, and develop in a community that embraces the diversity of individuals and ideas, including race, ethnicity, religion, spiritual beliefs, national origin, age, gender, marital status, socioeconomic background, sexual orientation, physical ability, political affiliation, and intellectual perspective.
  • Integrity: The University of Central Arkansas is committed to ethical and responsible behavior in actions and to developing the same commitment in students, thus fostering individuals who will have the skills, knowledge, and ability to engage positively with a diverse and changing world. The school's commitment extends to all levels of the campus to foster a climate of ethical conduct, respect, responsibility, and trust.
  • Low Cost of Attendance with a High Quality of Education: The University of Central Arkansas offers a high-value educational experience, with affordable tuition rates and living expenses.
  • Housing Scholarship & Residential Life: More than 3,800 students live in UCA's 18 residence halls and university apartments. All international students are also automatically offered a full out-of-state tuition scholarship after they are fully admitted and choose to live in university housing.

Bachelor's · Health & Nursing

Bachelor of Science – Communication Sciences and Disorders

4 years Next intake: Aug 2027 Apply by 15 Jul 2027

Program Description

Speech-Language Pathology is the health-care profession involved in preventing, evaluating, and treating speech, language, and swallowing disorders in individuals of all ages. Speech-language pathologists hold either a master’s or doctoral degree and have earned the Certificate of Clinical Competence (CCC) from the American Speech-Language-Hearing Association (ASHA), the national professional, scientific, and accrediting organization. Speech-language pathologists work in a wide variety of settings such as public and private schools, hospitals, clinics, health departments, rehabilitation centers, and private practice.

The undergraduate program in Communication Sciences and Disorders provides the academic background necessary for professional education in speech-language pathology or audiology at the graduate level. This program provides basic content courses relating to the processes of speech, language, and hearing, with introductory courses in the disorders of human communication.

Student life & support

Work while studying

International students can work part-time alongside their studies (subject to visa conditions).

Co-op & placements

Offers co-op or work-placement options in selected programs.

On-campus housing

Students typically arrange off-campus or homestay accommodation.

Accommodation options

Students need to plan their accommodations well in advance of their arrival as all accommodation options fill up quickly, well before the start of the semester.

On-Campus Residence Accommodations

On-Campus residence accommodations are provided per discretion by the school.

Off-Campus Accommodations

The local area has a variety of off-campus rental housing options including single homes, duplexes, apartments and rooms for rent. Students wishing to live off-campus need to research availability on their own and should arrive well before the start of term to do so.

Homestay

There are a wide variety of homestay options available, and our partners do their best to match students and hosts according to their interests and preferences. Homestay hosts include single people, young couples with children and pets, and older couples. All homestay accommodations have been inspected, and all adults in the home have completed a required criminal reference check.
