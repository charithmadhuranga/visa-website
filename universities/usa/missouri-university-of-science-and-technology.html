About Missouri University of Science and Technology

Founded in 1870, Missouri University of Science and Technology is one of the oldest public universities in the US, known for outstanding degree programs in science and technology. Ranked #185 in Best National Universities by U.S. News & World Report, Missouri S&T invites international students to check out its specialized degree programs, opportunities for industry research projects, and experiential work-study programs.

With more than 30 undergraduate and 25 graduate degree programs for international students to choose from, Missouri University of Science and Technology provides outstanding academics and career development in engineering and physical sciences, as well as the liberal arts and humanities.

Why Missouri University of Science and Technology

  • Campus Life: Campus life features a variety of student organizations, intramural sports, and sports clubs – and international students are encouraged to participate.
  • Location: Rolla is a safe, clean, attractive community surrounded by both the timeless beauty of the Ozarks and the hustle and bustle of a small, vibrant city. The city is also home to a friendly, close-knit community, making international students feel welcome from day one. Lively local fairs, cultural celebrations, and music festivals are regularly scheduled throughout the year, including the Fall Nature Festival, Celebration of Nations, and golf tournaments, among other fun events. Local restaurants, pubs, coffee shops, and pizzerias provide plenty of off-campus dining options for students to relax and spend time with friends.
  • Accolades: According to U.S. News & World Report, Missouri S&T ranks #75 in the best undergraduate engineering programs and #82 in graduate engineering, making it a top university in the US to pursue STEM specializations. Missouri S&T was also ranked #3 Best Public University for Career Placement (Princeton Review, 2022) and #1 Public University in Missouri for Return on Investment (Georgetown's Center on Education and Workforce, 2022).
  • Affordability: Missouri S&T is committed to providing an affordable and accessible education to students in every field. The university ranks #8 on the New York Times list of schools that offer great value for the cost and is the #1 public university in Missouri for return on investment, according to Georgetown’s Center on Education and the Workforce (2022).
  • Experiential Learning: Missouri S&T students can expect career readiness after graduation: With several opportunities for flexible work-study programs, internships, and co-ops at leading companies, Missouri S&T ranks #3 on Princeton Review’s list of best public universities for career placement. The school has also been ranked #18 for graduate economic success by DegreeChoices, as a majority of its students graduate into high-demand fields such as business, engineering, science, and technology.

Bachelor's · Computing & IT

Bachelor of Science – Computer Engineering (Optional Co-op)

4 years Next intake: Jan 2027 Apply by 2 Dec 2026

The Bachelor of Science - Computer Engineering program is designed to prepare an engineer to work with software and hardware of computers. In the software world, high level languages and complex programs are often the solution to a problem. In the hardware world, designs also include many aspects of the physical world, like temperature or noise, and often must include compromises between many opposing factors. The ability of a computer engineer to work in both worlds is what distinguishes them from a computer scientist or from an electrical engineer who specializes in computers. Computer scientists typically have little training with hardware. Electrical engineers typically have little training with software. The students are trained to work with both, since many computer systems cannot be built well without a clear understanding of both.

Computer engineers can be found just about anywhere there are computers. They might build the integrated circuits (ICs) that go into their home video game or their cell phone. They might develop the microprocessor that goes into their home computer, deciding what instructions it executes and how it interfaces with memory. Computer engineers also build computer systems that use these integrated circuits – for example, they might put together the ICs to build the motherboard for their home computer or the video card that goes into that computer. Computer engineers also help computers work together, for example developing computer networks or working with parallel processing.

Master's · Computing & IT

Master of Science – Computer Science

2 years Next intake: Jan 2027 Apply by 2 Dec 2026

The Computer Science department offers comprehensive Master of Science and Doctorate of Philosophy programs that focus on computer network security, software engineering, web databases, wireless systems, intelligent systems, data mining, parallel and distributed processing pervasive computing, computer networks, scientific visualization, and algorithms. These research activities support the department’s two major areas of excellence: software engineering and critical infrastructure protection.

The computer science department at Missouri Science and Technology makes use of both its own computer learning center (CLC) as well as university CLCs. The CLC contains a mix of Linux and Windows computing platform. Class sizes are kept small to facilitate student and faculty interactions. Research laboratories provide support for both undergraduate and graduate students.

Graduate student will do research alongside expert computer science faculty in the areas of distributed embedded systems, machine learning, data mining, and software engineering. Or they can choose the interdisciplinary route, doing research beside faculty in various engineering disciplines. In fact, they often work in large group settings with faculty from departments across campus, examining advanced research problems in bio-informatics, homeland security, embedded systems, and virtual reality. Cooperation between students and faculty at this advanced level is a hallmark of the computer science research program.

The Missouri Science and Technology Master of Business Administration program combines core business knowledge with the latest information technology trends, all in an environment of team based project work, business plan development, live simulations, and employer networking.
