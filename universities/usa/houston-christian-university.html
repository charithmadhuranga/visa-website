About Houston Christian University

Houston Christian University offers an intimate college environment in the heart of one of the most vibrant and diverse cities in the United States. Coming to HCU as a student is about more than books, tests and lectures. It is about stepping into a community where each person is recognized as a distinct individual. HCU strives to not only develop each student academically, but spiritually, physically and relationally as well. With an average class size of 25, the more than 3,000 students of HCU get the personal attention they want and need.

The beautiful 100-acre campus is located just minutes from the city’s thriving downtown district, and the opportunities for jobs, internships and fun surround it. The University has a very diverse student body, with an ethnic composition similar to that of America’s fourth-largest city. Although HCU is a Christian institution, a number of other world religions are represented within the student body, adding to the institution’s diversity.

Why Houston Christian University

  • Mission: HCU's mission is to deliver a learning environment that instills in students a passion for excellence across academic, spiritual, and professional domains, rooted in the confession, "Jesus Christ is Lord".
  • Vision - The Ten Pillars 2030: The university's vision, articulated in "The Ten Pillars 2030," outlines core convictions that serve as a foundation and guide for HCU's future development.
  • Campus Facilities: HCU's 158-acre campus in southwest Houston is thoughtfully designed to foster meaningful connections and support a vibrant academic community. Key facilities include: Morris Cultural Arts Center: Dedicated in 2007, this complex offers exceptional venues for academic, family, and faith-based events, including the Belin Chapel and McNair Hall. University Academic Center: Opened in 2008, it houses departments such as Visual Arts, Cinema & New Media Arts, and Writing, featuring a Contemporary Art Gallery and a Fine Arts Museum. Sharp Gymnasium: A 1,000-seat multi-purpose arena serving as the home for HCU's basketball and volleyball teams. Lawrence Park: A serene area on the east side of campus, providing a tranquil environment for students and staff.

Accommodation options

​Houston Christian University (HCU) offers a variety of on-campus housing options designed to support students' academic and social experiences. The Residence Life Office is dedicated to creating a secure and supportive community environment.​

Housing Options

  1. Freshman Village: Specifically designed for incoming freshmen, this community fosters connections and engagement among first-year students.
  2. Hodo Residence College: Named after HCU's second president, Dr. Doug Hodo, this six-story residence houses sophomores and upperclassmen, with shared lobby areas on each floor.
  3. Husky Village: These on-campus apartments offer students independence with the convenience of proximity to campus facilities. Most units feature single occupancy rooms within shared apartments, complete with full-size kitchens and living areas. Efficiency units with double occupancy are also available.

Residency Requirements

HCU emphasizes the value of on-campus living as part of the holistic educational experience. All unmarried undergraduate students under the age of 21 (prior to September 1) who have completed fewer than 64 credit hours are required to live on campus unless they meet specific exceptions, such as living with parents or guardians within 50 miles of the university.

Application Process

After submitting a $200 enrollment deposit, students receive instructions to access HCU's housing system, Symplicity, where they can request housing assignments, select roommates, and choose meal plans.

Accommodations for Disabilities

The Office of Disability Services provides accommodations for students with disabilities, including meal plan waivers, specific room assignments, and approval for service or assistance animals. Students seeking such accommodations must provide appropriate documentation and follow the outlined procedures.

For more detailed information or specific inquiries, students are encouraged to contact the Residence Life Office directly. Accommodation website for reference:- https://hc.edu/student-affairs/reslife/
