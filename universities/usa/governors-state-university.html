About Governors State University (GovState)

GovState (Governors State University) is the only regional, public, comprehensive university in Chicago’s south suburban area, serving approximately 4,600 students annually in 85 different bachelor’s, master’s, doctoral, and certificate programs. GovState features a highly diverse faculty and staff of roughly 1,000 employees that reflect the students and community they serve. GovState provides accessible, high value undergraduate and graduate education to culturally and economically diverse students from across the region and around the world. From its mission statement, Governors State University is committed to offering an exceptional and accessible education that prepares students with the knowledge, skills, and confidence to succeed in a global society.

GovState is an expert at guiding freshman and transfer students to discover the right degree program to excite their interests. The university’s extensive list of graduate programs prepares students in a variety of fields for the next level of academic achievement. The GovState experience includes a comprehensive student life, both on and off campus, with on-campus housing; club sports and recreation; civic engagement; international student services; more than 70 student clubs and organizations; the world-renowned Nathan Manilow Sculpture Park, the Center for Performing Arts and much more.

Why Governors State University

  • Programs: In every college GovState has stellar programs and star professors, all focused on delivering academic excellence in a small-class setting. Not only will students find the programs and courses they are looking for, they'll also find more than 80 student clubs and honor societies to dig deeper into whatever interests them.
  • Schedule: Since its founding, one of GovState's big ideas came from understanding that daytime classes don't fit many students' schedules. That's why GovState offers classes at night, online, on television, and at locations around the Chicago area.
  • Location: Govstate is in the heart of Chicago's Southland near I-57, a major north-south route, and I-80, the region's major east-west route. For those who travel by train, the Metra station is close enough to walk – along the Alumni Path that leads from the station to campus – or by free shuttle bus. And, the PACE bus service makes stops on campus.
  • Safety: GovState ranks as the safest public university campus in Illinois.
