About Humphreys University

Humphreys University is a non-profit university accredited by the WASC Senior College and University Commission. The University offers flexible class formats for students wishing to pursue associate, bachelor, masters and juris doctor degree programs. For over a century, Humphreys University has been dedicated to helping students of all ages and backgrounds achieve their goals through its wide range of degree programs.

The staff and faculty of Humphreys University are committed to student academic success. Classes are small and the teaching is personalized and friendly. Humphreys University offers a wealth of support services to help students achieve their goals and ensure their higher education is within reach.

Why Humphreys University

  • Integral Curricular Practical Training (CPT): The practicum/CPT experience is an integral part of the MBA-IT and MBA-Organizational Leadership programs and participation is required. CPT is authorized from day 1 for the duration of the 27-month program with no gaps or breaks.
  • Flexible Learning: Humphreys University gives students the flexibility to choose courses that fit their personal and work commitments, giving students the opportunity to attend classes in their preferred format. HU's 11-week quarters help students save time by allowing them to complete degree requirements faster and more efficiently.
  • Personalized Attention: Humphreys is a private nonprofit school with a focus on its students. Humphreys is perfect for those do not want to be lost with crowded classes. Students can get the help and guidance they need without feeling intimidated and overwhelmed. If students have difficulty with a particular class, there is help. There is always help- for free.
  • Nonprofit: Humphreys University is proud to be nonprofit. Nonprofit universities are focused on improving the student’s experience and increasing student success, rather than focusing on making a profit.
  • Accreditation: Humphreys University is regionally accredited by the WASC Senior College and University Commission, or, WSCUC. Other schools with WSCUC accreditation include CSU schools, UC schools, and other private universities.

Optional Practical Training (OPT)

Optional Practical Training allows international F-1 visa students to work for 12 months in a U.S. job related to their field of study. Students must have completed one academic year and have been enrolled in a full course of study. Students must not be authorized for a total of 12 months of Curricular Practical Training (CPT) and not completed 12 months of OPT at the same degree level.
