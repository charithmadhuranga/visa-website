About Nova Southeastern University

Since its establishment in 1964, Nova Southeastern University has earned a global reputation as a leading research university by delivering innovative academic programs and upholding strong core values of integrity, academic excellence, community service, and leadership. Its beautiful Florida campus, strong student community, and influential faculty attract students from all over the world.

Nova Southeastern University strives to set students up for success and encourage positive change in the world through education. An impressive variety of programs are available to suit the unique goals of each student, and has specialty schools in healthcare, law, engineering, business, arts, and more.

  • Location: Located in beautiful Fort Lauderdale, Florida, students are only 15 minutes away from the beach and 3 hours away from Orlando’s major theme parks. Closer to campus, there are a variety of shopping malls, restaurants, and festivals. The thriving city also provides a variety of employment opportunities for students and graduates.
  • Student Support: Nova Southeastern University takes pride in its student experience, and aims to support all students in their academic journeys. Students can also connect with others in over 150 clubs, 16 NCAA athletic teams, and a many campus activities.
  • Facilities: Nova Southeastern University’s campus offers many spaces for students to enjoy, from the Medicinal Garden, to the Art Museum, to exercising at the RecPlex.
  • Accomodation: There are a variety of on-campus housing options available to students on a first-come, first-serve basis. On-campus living provides great opportunities to meet new people, enjoy campus amenities, and have the convenience of being close to classes, study spaces, restaurants, and more.
  • Academic Excellence: From the research opportunities at the Guy Harvey Research Institute, to the available support from faculty and students, Nova Southeastern University sets students up for success.

Bachelor of Science – Computer Science

4 years Next intake: Jan 2027 Apply by 25 Nov 2026

Nova Southeastern University’s Bachelors of Science in Computer Science program students will explore algorithms, software design and development and take on real-world challenges.

Nova Southeastern University prepares students for a range of potential career paths. They can go beyond coding to gain expertise on data structures, algorithms, architecture, database and information retrieval, human-computer interaction, numeral and symbolic computation, operating systems, programming languages and software methodology and engineering. They will also work on practical projects and be challenged to bridge the gap between hardware and software issues. Upon completion of the program, students will have the skills every business and organization needs from a computing professional.

The Bachelor of Science in Computer Science program is accredited by the Computing Accreditation Commission (CAC) of the Accreditation Board for Engineering and Technology (ABET).

Master of Science – Professional Practice Management

Accommodation options

On-Campus Residence Accommodations

There are a variety of on-campus housing options available to students on a first-come, first-serve basis, and students should plan well in advance to ensure accommodation arrangements. Nova Southeastern University requires all undergraduate students with less than 49 credit hours live on-campus unless:

  • They are 20 years of age or older
  • They are married or they reside with a parent or legal guardian within the area of Broward, Dade, and Palm Beach.

On-campus living provides great opportunities to meet new people, enjoy campus amenities, and have the convenience of being close to classes, study spaces, restaurants, and more. Living on NSU's beautiful campus sets students up for success and contributes to an outstanding student experience.
