About Worcester Polytechnic Institute

Founded in 1865, Worcester Polytechnic Institute (WPI) is the third-oldest private technological university in United States and is located in the tech hub of Greater Boston in Massachusetts. With a 150-year history of technological advancement, Worcester Polytechnic Institute (WPI), is a global leader in project-based learning with a distinctive approach to STEM education and research. The Business School at WPI bridges the worlds of business and technology to develop adaptive leaders who impact the world—all with a STEM focus. WPI Business school is the B-School for engineers, scientists, and change makers.

WPI combine business and STEM expertise to make an impact, earning a spot in the top 100 rankings by US News & World Report and accreditation from AACSB.

Why WPI Business School?

  • Academic Recognition: Top 100 Ranking, WPI is known for its high-quality education.
  • Bright Career Prospects: Career Development Center (CDC) hosts 2 career Fairs approx. 450 Companies. Graduates enjoy an average salary of $90,000.
  • Affordability and High ROI : With a total tuition fee of $34,650 for 2-year full time on campus.
  • Scholarship Opportunities: International students can benefit from Merit and Diversity Scholarships, emphasizing inclusivity.
  • Global Community: Join a diverse community of students. 40% of graduate students are international, and the faculty is equally diverse.
  • Practical Learning: WPI focuses on Project-Based Learning
  • Studying in Massachusetts offers unique benefits: Massachusetts, known as the "Innovation State," offers a thriving and vibrant environment for creativity, entrepreneurship, and research. The state is home to top tech companies, prestigious universities, and a rich cultural scene. The WPI Business School, located in the vibrant tech hub of Greater Boston, offers a unique blend of academic excellence and practical skills that set you up for success.

Master's · Data & Analytics

Master of Science – Business Analytics

2 years Next intake: Aug 2027 Apply by 15 Jun 2027

Informed business decisions rely on significant and current data to help executives make decisions. A master's in business analytics gives students the skills and training they need to make these choices. Because data is prolific and continual, business analytics professionals who know how to interpret and use that data are in high demand.

When data is accurately and efficiently understood with an eye for business needs and technology-driven platforms, organizational leaders can make strategic decisions based on up-to-the-minute trends and results. Without trained analysts and decision makers who know how to use analytics methods and interpret the results for business results, great opportunities are lost.

WPI’s STEM-designated Master's in Business Analytics provides a solid foundation in business analytics, with applications in a variety of business areas, plus a real-world, team-based project, which ensures that students are prepared to be successful in a data-driven, technological business environment.

There is a pressing need for engineers, scientists, and coders with business acumen and management skills. WPI is committed to making STEM education more attainable, and The Business School is doing its part by reducing its tuition for graduate programs. Today, a premier degree from WPI’s business school is more affordable than ever—meaning students can see a high return on investment from their degree even sooner.

MSIT students must complete two, three-course specialties, selected from the following four specialties:

  • Marketing Analytics
  • Operations Analytics
  • Advanced Business Analytics Methods
  • Artificial Intelligence in Business
