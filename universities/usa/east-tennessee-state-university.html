About East Tennessee State University (ETSU)

East Tennessee State University (ETSU) opened in 1911 as East Tennessee State Normal School, a teacher training institution which survives to this day as ETSU’s University School K-12 education program. The East Tennessee State Normal School took on its current name, East Tennessee State University, in 1963 after expanding its curriculum and gaining university status.

East Tennessee State University offers a broad range of academic subjects, with colleges of Arts and Sciences, Business and Technology, Clinical and Rehabilitative Health Sciences, Education, Medicine, Nursing, Pharmacy and Public Health. It also has an Honors College and schools of Continuing Studies and Graduate Studies. East Tennessee State University offers several unique programs. The Appalachian Studies minor explores the ongoing history and culture of the Appalachian region and its Indigenous Peoples. East Tennessee State University is one of just a handful of universities to offer a graduate-level course in Storytelling. East Tennessee State University is also the proud home of the first degree in Bluegrass, Old Time and Country Music Studies, making it a favourable choice for musicians.

East Tennessee State University provides a student-centered community of learning, reflecting high standards and promoting a balance of liberal arts and professional preparation, and continuous improvement. East Tennessee State University conducts a wide array of educational and research programs and clinical services including a comprehensive Academic Health Sciences Center. Education is the university’s highest priority, and East Tennessee State University is committed to increasing the level of educational attainment in the state and region based on core values where: people come first, are treated with dignity and respect, and are encouraged to achieve their full potential; relationships are built on honesty, integrity, and trust; diversity of people and thought is respected; excellence is achieved through teamwork, leadership, creativity, and a strong work ethic; efficiency is achieved through wise use of human and financial resources; and commitment to intellectual achievement is embraced.

East Tennessee State University’s main campus is located in Johnson City, Tennessee, in the northeastern tip of the state bordered by Virginia and North Carolina. As of 2020, the population of Johnson City was 71,046, making it the eighth largest city in the state of Tennessee. Johnson City has a humid subtropical climate, with warm summers and cool winters. Temperatures in Johnson City are partially moderated by its elevation and proximity to the Appalachian Mountains. Precipitation is abundant, with an annual average of 45.22 inches. Summer is typically the wettest part of the year, while early autumn is considerably drier. Snowfall is moderate and sporadic, with an annual average of 15.6 inches. Trails through the Appalachian Mountains begin just 30 minutes away from East Tennessee State University’s main campus in Johnson City. Further afield, the Cherokee National Forest and Great Smokey Mountains National Parks offer ample opportunities for camping, hiking and climbing in beautiful natural settings.

Why East Tennessee State University

  • Natural Tranquility: East Tennessee State University is located in the mountains of northeastern Tennessee, replete with fresh mountain air and a peaceful environment for studying.
  • Financial Support: East Tennessee State University provides scholarship opportunities to assist international students with tuition. For example, the International Merit Scholarship can contribute up to $12,500 per year for entering undergraduate students, and up to $11,500 per year for entering graduate students.
  • Get Involved: East Tennessee State University has 200+ on-campus clubs and organizations to help make new friends and find community, as well as network professionally.
  • Administrative Support: East Tennessee State University’s helpful staff members will help guide international students through the admissions and I-20 processes.
  • Musical Heritage: The East Tennessee State University is situated within a hub for bluegrass music and Americana. Johnson City’s annual Little Chicago Blues Festival pays homage to the region’s rich musical heritage, while international music is celebrated at the Blue Plum Festival.

On-Campus Residence Accommodations

On-Campus residence accommodations are provided per discretion by the school.

Off-Campus Accommodations

The local area has a variety of off-campus rental housing options including single homes, duplexes, apartments and rooms for rent. Students wishing to live off-campus need to research availability on their own and should arrive well before the start of term to do so.

Homestay

There are a wide variety of homestay options available, and our partners do their best to match students and hosts according to their interests and preferences. Homestay hosts include single people, young couples with children and pets, and older couples. All homestay accommodations have been inspected, and all adults in the home have completed a required criminal reference check.
