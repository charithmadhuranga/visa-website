About Pittsburg State University

Pittsburg State University is a fully accredited state university with more than 200 undergraduate and graduate programs. Pitt State's expansive campus is filled with stunning architecture, state-of-the-art technology and magnificent artwork. Students will feel the history surround them as they enter stately buildings such as McCray, Porter and Russ Halls. Student's will also experience the brilliance of modern design and engineering in Pitt State's Kansas Technology Center, Robert W. Plaster Center and Bicknell Family Center for the Arts.

Pittsburg State University and the Pittsburg community have a proud tradition of welcoming and supporting international students. It's part of their community's history which, to this day, is known as the "Little Balkans" for the number of nationalities - French, German, Italian, Slovenian, Swedish, eastern European and more - which helped found the city of Pittsburg.

Why Pittsburg State University

  • Campus: The historical buildings on Pitt State's campus are all connected by an easily accessible hike/bike trail surrounded by manicured landscaping, tranquil lake and open vistas.
  • Sports Facilities: The SRC has over 70 pieces of Strength/Fitness/Cardio equipment as well as 4 wood floor gyms that offer Volleyball, Basketball, Pickle Ball, Indoor Soccer and many other indoor games that they could be used for. 10 treadmills, 13 elliptical/cross trainers, 12 bikes, recumbent and upright as well as 4 spin bikes and 4 rowing machines. There is a dance studio and upstairs walking/jogging track.
  • Accommodation: Pittsburg State offers traditional, apartment and suite-style living options. Rooms are furnished with beds, desks and chairs, chests, and wardrobes. Amenities include parking pass, all utility costs, laundry facilities, kitchen access, high-speed wireless Internet, and secured access. Students feel the vibrancy of the university, have easy access to all university events, and great food from Gibson Dining Hall.
  • Student Center: The Overman Student Center provides students, faculty, staff, alumni and guests with facilities, programs and services to meet the needs of daily life on campus. Whether students are visiting the Student Center for a meeting, to relax, eat, shop, study or just talk with friends, the center welcomes them.

Bachelor's · Health & Nursing

Bachelor of Science – Nursing

4 years Next intake: Aug 2027 Apply by 1 Jun 2027

The Irene Ransom Bradley School of Nursing offers students a choice of two tracks in which they can earn a Bachelor of Science in Nursing (BSN, Pre-licensure) degree. If the students' career goal is to become a registered nurse or to build a bridge to become an advanced practice nurse, the BSN program at the Bradley School of Nursing is the perfect option for them.

In fact, as the population ages, a BSN may soon be a requirement for nurses. The “Future of Nursing Report,” issued by the Institute of Medicine and the Robert Wood Johnson Foundation, recommends that 80% of registered nurses have a Bachelor of Science in Nursing degree by 2020.

Pittsburg State University’s BSN program prepares graduates for success in today’s clinical setting. The experienced faculty, many of whom continue to practice in the field, provide students with the guidance and leadership they need to successfully earn licensure. In fact, the Bradley School of Nursing boasts a 2018 NCLEX Registered Nurse Board Pass Rate of 98.68% and a 2019 NCLEX Registered Nurse Board Pass Rate of 91.03%. (A phenomenal achievement when the national pass rate is just 89 percent.) This rate takes into account ALL students admitted into the program, not just those who sit for the NCLEX. It’s an astounding number, and a true reflection of the university faculty's expertise.

Student life & support

Work while studying

International students can work part-time alongside their studies (subject to visa conditions).

Co-op & placements

Offers co-op or work-placement options in selected programs.

On-campus housing

Students typically arrange off-campus or homestay accommodation.

Accommodation options

Students need to plan their accommodations well in advance of their arrival as all accommodation options fill up quickly, well before the start of the semester.

On-Campus Residence Accommodations

On-Campus residence accommodations are provided per discretion by the school.

Off-Campus Accommodations

The local area has a variety of off-campus rental housing options including single homes, duplexes, apartments and rooms for rent. Students wishing to live off-campus need to research availability on their own and should arrive well before the start of term to do so.

Homestay

There are a wide variety of homestay options available, and our partners do their best to match students and hosts according to their interests and preferences. Homestay hosts include single people, young couples with children and pets, and older couples. All homestay accommodations have been inspected, and all adults in the home have completed a required criminal reference check.
