About Louisiana State University

Founded in 1860, Louisiana State University (LSU) has a beautiful campus located in Baton Rouge, Louisiana, the state capital and a cultural crossroads between the Cajun country of Lafayette and the beautiful, internationally known city of New Orleans, the birthplace of jazz music and home of Mardi Gras. Just minutes from LSU, downtown Baton Rouge is a vibrant hot spot that’s bursting with an authentic, festive culture you can’t find anywhere else. Attend an exhibition at the LSU Museum of Art, watch the sun set over the mighty Mississippi River or visit a historic plantation. Dine on delicious Southern cuisine (ever tried fried alligator?), explore one of many international art and music festivals and check out the Baton Rouge Zoo. Whatever your interests may be, our city is the perfect place to spend a day - and four years!

Why Louisiana State University

As an LSU student, you’ll take a deep dive into courses that instill practical skills - like critical thinking, communication, problem-solving and entrepreneurship - that you’ll carry with you into the workforce and beyond. Here are some of the many reasons for which you want to study at LSU:

  • LSU is one of only 30 universities nationwide holding land-grant, sea-grant and space-grant status.
  • Graduates work for top companies like Gerber, Nestle, Ralph Lauren, DreamWorks Animation, Nike, and Shell.
  • LSU is among the elite one percent of research universities in the United States.
  • 235 academic fields of study in 10 colleges and schools
  • Top 100 Public Schools
  • Top 100 Engineering Schools
  • No. 1 Landscape Architecture program in the Nation
  • LSU provides great opportunities for students hoping to strengthen or expand family businesses, or to start, own, run and manage their own businesses after graduation.

Academic Accelerator – Bachelor of Science – Computer Science – Software Engineering

4 years Next intake: Jan 2027 Apply by 8 Dec 2026

The Academic Accelerator Program (AAP) is a balanced first-year program for international students with strong English-language skills. It supplements immersive classes taught in English with the appropriate level of support.

Students in the Academic Accelerator Program will receive:

  • Support tailored to their academic and English-speaking needs
  • A full year’s worth of college credits
  • Access to courses specifically for international students
  • Assistance with English-language vocabulary relevant to their classes
  • A mix of classes with both international and U.S. students

The Louisiana State University Computer Science undergraduate program is offered through the College of Engineering and provides students with a range of opportunities to take courses in core and applied areas of computer science, as well as engage in research, summer internships, and co-ops. Students can choose from four concentrations:

  • Software Engineering (SE) Concentration
  • Cloud Computing and Networking (CCN) Concentration
  • Data Science and Analytics (DSA) Concentration
  • Computer Science and Second Discipline Concentration

Upon graduation, students will demonstrate:

  • The ability to apply computing and mathematical knowledge effectively
  • The skills to analyze and solve computing problems
  • The capability to design, implement, and evaluate computer-based systems
  • Effective teamwork skills
  • Understanding of professional, ethical, legal, security, and social issues in computing
  • Strong communication skills
  • Awareness of the global impact of computing
  • Commitment to continuing professional development
  • Proficiency in current computing techniques and tools
  • Application of mathematical foundations and computer science theory in system design
  • Utilization of design and development principles in software construction

Software Engineering focuses on applying engineering principles to software design, development, and maintenance. Subdisciplines include requirements engineering, software design, testing, maintenance, and quality management.

Emerging opportunities in software engineering include app development, digital media, mobile devices, entertainment software design, and gaming.

Bachelor's · Computing & IT

International Direct – Bachelor of Science – Computer Science – Cloud Computing and Networking

4 years Next intake: Jan 2027 Apply by 8 Dec 2026

International direct admission to Louisiana State University (LSU) is available for international students with advanced English-language skills. The program focuses on academic, cultural, and professional growth to help students excel in school and prepare for a successful future.

As an international direct student at Louisiana State University, individuals will receive exclusive and rigorous academic coursework, priority access to in-demand courses, guidance from academic advisors and mentors, career development opportunities, and the chance to participate in intensive classes with domestic students.

The Louisiana State University Computer Science undergraduate program, offered through the College of Engineering, provides students with a wide range of opportunities to take courses in core and applied areas of computer science, as well as engage in research, summer internships, and co-ops. Students can choose from four concentrations: Software Engineering, Cloud Computing and Networking, Data Science and Analytics, or Computer Science and Second Discipline.

Upon graduation, students are expected to demonstrate the ability to apply knowledge of computing and mathematics, analyze and define computing requirements, design and evaluate computer-based systems, work effectively in teams, understand professional and ethical responsibilities, communicate effectively, analyze the impact of computing on society, engage in continuing professional development, use current techniques and tools, apply mathematical foundations and algorithmic principles, and apply design and development principles in software construction.

The Cloud Computing and Networking concentration focuses on the concept of cloud computing as a virtual server located remotely. This approach allows developers to access various components and create customizable applications by integrating multiple systems running on different platforms. Businesses are increasingly adopting cloud computing for its efficiency, cost-savings, scalability, flexibility, redundancy, reliability, and security benefits.

Student life & support

Work while studying

International students can work part-time alongside their studies (subject to visa conditions).

Co-op & placements

Co-op options are limited — ask us about placement-friendly alternatives.

On-campus housing

Students typically arrange off-campus or homestay accommodation.

Accommodation options

Students need to plan their accommodations well in advance of their arrival as all accommodation options fill up quickly, well before the start of the semester.

On-Campus Residence Accommodations

On-Campus residence accommodations are provided per discretion by the school.

Off-Campus Accommodations

The local area has a variety of off-campus rental housing options including single homes, duplexes, apartments and rooms for rent. Students wishing to live off-campus need to research availability on their own and should arrive well before the start of term to do so.

Homestay

There are a wide variety of homestay options available, and our partners do their best to match students and hosts according to their interests and preferences. Homestay hosts include single people, young couples with children and pets, and older couples. All homestay accommodations have been inspected, and all adults in the home have completed a required criminal reference check.
