About California State University, Monterey Bay

California State University, Monterey Bay empowers its students to become compassionate leaders who are ready to shape the future. The university offers a transformative, experience-rich education within a supportive, inclusive, and inspiring environment. Established in 1994, Cal State Monterey Bay is guided and inspired by its founding vision—to become a collaborative intellectual community that serves the diverse people of California through high-quality, innovative education. Its mission is to prepare students to contribute responsibly to California and the global community by providing transformative learning experiences in an inclusive environment. The university aspires to be recognized as a premier comprehensive institution that prepares reflective practitioners, innovative leaders, and thriving citizens dedicated to the public good. CSUMB values Student-Centeredness, Intellectual Curiosity, Creativity and Innovation, Community and Belonging, Service and Civic Engagement, Sustainability, Global Orientation, and Health and Wellness.

Why California State University, Monterey Bay

  • The Monterey Bay Area: California State University, Monterey Bay’s coastal location provides students with unique learning experiences and opportunities for adventure beyond the classroom. The region’s rich biodiversity offers abundant research potential, while the university’s proximity to San Francisco and San Jose expands possibilities for career connections.
  • Supportive Community: As members of the Raft, the Otter community, students quickly discover that they are stronger together. They are encouraged by their peers, mentored by dedicated faculty, and supported by committed staff. Cal State Monterey Bay offers a small-school atmosphere that provides ample opportunities for meaningful, face-to-face interactions with professors.
  • Transformative Outcomes: California State University, Monterey Bay’s programs are designed to be life-changing. The university serves a diverse student population from across California and around the world. Graduates leave the university prepared for successful careers and empowered to make a positive impact in their communities and beyond.

Bachelor of Science – Statistics and Data Science

4 years Next intake: Aug 2027 Apply by 30 Apr 2027

The Bachelor of Science in Statistics and Data Science program focuses on the study of data collection, analysis, interpretation, and presentation. Statisticians and data scientists use these skills to summarize and visualize data, test hypotheses, estimate unknown quantities, and predict outcomes based on available information.

The program prepares students to (i) summarize and visualize data effectively, (ii) analyze data to address research and practical objectives, (iii) communicate results clearly in both oral and written forms, (iv) understand the role of the profession in service to society, and (v) collaborate successfully in interdisciplinary environments.

Designed to support two primary career paths, the program equips students either to enter the workforce immediately after graduation—in fields such as business, pharmaceuticals, actuarial science, and government—or to pursue advanced degrees (M.S., M.A., or Ph.D.) in statistics, biostatistics, data science, or related disciplines that require advanced quantitative and analytical expertise.
