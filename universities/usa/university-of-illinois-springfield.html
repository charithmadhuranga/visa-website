About University of Illinois Springfield (UIS)

The University of Illinois - Springfield (UIS) is uniquely positioned in Illinois’ capital to provide all students a unique, student-centered learning experience. The University of Illinois Springfield (UIS) is one of the three universities in the world-class University of Illinois system and the #1 public regional university in Illinois. Located in the capital city of Illinois, Springfield, UIS offers international students the safety and hospitality that is the best of small-town America. Earn a world-renowned University of Illinois degree at UIS, named a best value school by US News and World Report.

Why the University of Illinois - Springfield (UIS):

  • Location: The University of Illinois - Springfield is situated in Illinois’ state capital, offering easy access to the major employers, cultural centers, and attractions in Chicago (three hours away) and St. Louis, Missouri (90 minutes away). Springfield is known for vibrant dining, nightlife, and live music, as well as outdoor recreation.
  • Small-classroom sizes: At UIS, classes are small, averaging 13:1, and are 100% faculty taught — no teaching assistants. Beyond the classroom, students are encouraged to collaborate with faculty experts on research projects and/or pursue additional experiential learning opportunities.
  • Quality Rankings: Excellence and value go hand in hand at the University of Illinois Springfield: U.S. News & World Report ranks UIS as the #1 regional public university in Illinois (three years running), and #4 in the Midwest.

Student life & support

Work while studying

International students can work part-time alongside their studies (subject to visa conditions).

Co-op & placements

Co-op options are limited — ask us about placement-friendly alternatives.

On-campus housing

Students typically arrange off-campus or homestay accommodation.

Accommodation options

Students need to plan their accommodations well in advance of their arrival as all accommodation options fill up quickly, well before the start of the semester.

On-Campus Residence Accommodations

On-Campus residence accommodations are provided per discretion by the school.

Off-Campus Accommodations

The local area has a variety of off-campus rental housing options including single homes, duplexes, apartments and rooms for rent. Students wishing to live off-campus need to research availability on their own and should arrive well before the start of term to do so.

Homestay

There are a wide variety of homestay options available, and our partners do their best to match students and hosts according to their interests and preferences. Homestay hosts include single people, young couples with children and pets, and older couples. All homestay accommodations have been inspected, and all adults in the home have completed a required criminal reference check.
