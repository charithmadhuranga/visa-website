About College of Saint Benedict and Saint John's University

The College of Saint Benedict and Saint John's University are two liberal arts colleges located four miles apart in Central Minnesota. Saint Benedict's is a college for women and Saint John's is a college for men. The students of these two colleges share in one common education, as well as coeducational social, cultural and spiritual programs. The colleges encourage students to come to terms with their own personal development in relation to their peers and to bring that enriched understanding into the lively coeducational life which characterizes the two campuses.

Founded in 1913, the College of Saint Benedict embraces the rich heritage of bold leadership and pioneering spirit of its Benedictine founders, the Sisters of Saint Benedict’s Monastery. The monastery traces its beginning to 1857, when four young nuns and two monastic candidates made the treacherous journey from Pennsylvania to the Minnesota frontier. Their mission: to establish a Benedictine community in central Minnesota and to serve the area’s German immigrant families.

The College of Saint Benedict designs programs to meet the needs and aspirations of young women, emphasizing women's leadership and a personal development profile that includes intellectual, spiritual, emotional and physical development. Their values-based and value-added residential liberal arts education provides the framework for fulfilling lives of disciplined inquiry and leadership in careers devoted to the professions, public life and service to others.

Why College of Saint Benedict and Saint John's University

  • Campus Life: CSB/SJU are proud NCAA Division III schools in the Minnesota Intercollegiate Athletic Conference. In addition to their 23 varsity athletic programs, they offer club sports and intramurals for students looking to get involved, try something new or continue a sport they love. Students can also enjoy an impressive variety of outdoor and campus recreation opportunities to stay active and adventurous.
  • Student Wellness: The idea of a balanced, fulfilling life is not new to CSB/SJU. Under the overarching Benedictine values of their founders, they’ve historically focused on providing transformative experiences that enable students to reach their full potential intellectually, spiritually, emotionally and physically. A wide variety of approaches to wellness exist for students—from counseling services and peer programs to medical referral services and health information and resources—inside and outside the classroom, students experience a holistic approach to promoting health in body, mind and spirit.
  • Career Development: Students will have access to all sorts of resources and a tight network of support as they prepare for their next steps after college. In addition to an academic advisor who knows their name and their goals, the CSB/SJU's Experience & Professional Development (XPD) department will help students with career readiness or preparations for graduate school. They're so committed to student success that they’re ranked the #1 and #2 colleges in Minnesota for getting a job.

Bachelor of Science – Nursing

Nursing is a rewarding profession focused on helping others. A career path in nursing offers a wide variety of choices in many different practice areas. Nursing provides opportunities to make a difference in the lives of individuals, families, and communities every day.

The nursing program at the College of Saint Benedict and Saint John's University prepares its students for a meaningful career in the healthcare industry. The university is well-known for its distinctive nursing program that strives for excellence in the areas of professionalism, quality, innovation, and leadership. Every nursing course integrates liberal learning concepts with Catholic and Benedictine traditions and values. As a senior nursing student, they will participate in a clinical and leadership intensive capstone that provides an excellent platform for entry to practice.

In addition to the formal curriculum, students have many opportunities to engage in leadership and service experiences. The Nursing Club provides them with opportunities such as volunteering, a mentorship program, and networking. The Nursing Department is also active in Sigma, a renowned nursing honor society emphasizing leadership and scholarship.

On-Campus Residence Accommodations

As a Four-Year Residential College, all students are required to live on campus. There are different types of housing options including dormitories, suites and apartments. Women live at CSB and men live at SJU but all classes are co-educational and there is regular, free and convenient transportation system between the campuses. New students will learn of their on-campus housing accommodations in July before their arrival in late August for the start of the Fall semester.
