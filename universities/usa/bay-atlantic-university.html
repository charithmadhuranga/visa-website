About Bay Atlantic University

Bay Atlantic University is a private, non-profit university in Washington, DC that offers higher education programs to domestic and international students at its centrally located campus, just a short walk from the White House. Bay Atlantic University is established as a New Generation University which is defined by six key aspects: Affordable, Focused, Global, Connected, Central and Dynamic; aspects that are measured within the strategic planning process.

BAU was recognized by Intelligent magazine in 2022 with the MBA program ranking 26 out of 66 for the Best for Entrepreneurship in the Best MBA Programs of 2022, and the Master of Science in Cybersecurity program ranking 22 out of 30 for the Best Master’s in Internet Security Degree Programs of 2022.

Why Bay Atlantic University

  • Affordable: Bay Atlantic University believes that high quality education doesn’t have to come at a high cost. BAU strives to make high quality education available for everyone.
  • Focused: BAU puts their students in the best position to succeed in their careers/lives by offering degree programs that are in high demand fields and rapidly growing. Additionally, being in the heart of DC, BAU offers degrees in Politics, Business, and Information Sciences: the three core industries that power DC and the world.
  • Global: BAU believes in education without borders! Bay Atlantic University encourages its students to be global citizens. The international environment on campus enables them to learn about other cultures and discuss global matters with different perspectives in the courses.
  • Connected: BAU is well-connected to the DC Community through its business partnerships, faculty networks, and community service.
  • Central: The whole city is BAU's campus with easy access to key U.S. agencies, embassies, and leading international institutions (World Bank, International Monetary Fund, Inter-American Development Bank, etc.). Students are not limited to just on-campus events; students can attend numerous cultural events and festivals throughout the year.
  • Dynamic: BAU prides itself on providing a curriculum that reflects the current market trends and most up-to-date teaching methodologies.

Accommodation options

BAU offers housing (Island Condos) to students located in the newly developing area of Washington, D.C., Brookland, which is less than 4 miles from campus. The housing facilities have a total of 158 beds in eight attached buildings configured as single (6), double (81), triple (39) and quad (32). The rooms are fully furnished with free wi-fi access. All common areas have built-in LCD TVs. Each building has a flat rooftop area and outdoor common patio. A professional cleaning staff clean all common areas, bathrooms, and kitchens every two weeks. There is security staff at night and security cameras around the premises. The student housing coordinator, located on site, is responsible for all aspects of the management such as student conduct, security, maintenance, and cleaning. In addition, the coordinator is responsible for checking students in and ensuring students have read, understand, and sign the standard operating procedures. There is also a shuttle bus service 3 times a day between Island Condos and BAU Campus during the week.

For more information, visit the Student Housing page.
