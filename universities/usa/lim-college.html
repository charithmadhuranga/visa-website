Overview

About LIM College (LC)

New York City is the epicenter of fashion and business, and LIM is right at the heart of it. Discover a warm, inviting campus community that feels like home, all within the most exciting city in the world.

Since 1939, LIM College has embraced a "learning by doing" philosophy to propel graduates forward into successful futures. LIM's commitment to hands-on experience and cross-disciplinary education in business, fashion, and lifestyle has led to a multitude of dream opportunities and careers.

Located in the heart of midtown Manhattan in New York City, LIM is the ideal launchpad for students who want to be on the cutting edge. LIM College strives to match drive, ignite creative energy, and push students to reach their full potential.

Recognized globally for educating leaders in the business of fashion since 1939, LIM is a college with no equal, in a city like no other.

LIM College in stats:

  • A top fashion school in the country. According to Vogue, LIM College is one of "The Best Fashion Schools for Design, Marketing and More"
  • 97% of LIM's 2023 Grads were working in the business of fashion and lifestyle or continuing their education within 6 months of graduation.
  • There are over 3,000 companies in LIM's internship and employment pipeline.
  • 98% of internship employers surveyed view LIM students as career-ready.
  • By the time they graduate, LIM students have completed an average of 5 internships.

Why LIM College

  • NYC boasts one of the world's best public transportation systems. Students can walk to their classes, internships, and some of the best-known attractions in the world.
  • NYC is home to an astounding number of diverse cultures and creative minds. This is where people from all over the world come to express their individuality and be who they were meant to be.
  • Location: Living in New York City means that opportunities are everywhere - students can work at fashion shows one week and explore showrooms the next.
  • Experiential learning: LIM takes the theoretical into the real world. Build practical skills through multiple internships, industry engagement activities, and networking opportunities. And students won’t just learn what’s driving the fashion and lifestyle trends of today—they'll be a part of what’s coming next.
  • Enviable Internships: From luxury brands to retail giants, PR and marketing firms to publishers, and entertainment conglomerates to savvy startups—LIM students gain workplace experience at industry-leading companies.
  • Career Development at LIM: From career fairs to mock interviews, LIM College provides comprehensive resources to prepare students to excel in the workplace. Students receive one-on-one career coaching and personalized support to help them stand out from the crowd.
  • International Student Services: As of Fall 2024, international students in all master's programs can apply to begin Curricular Practical Training (CPT) in their first semester. In addition, master's degree students in STEM-designated programs may be eligible for a 24-month STEM Optional Practical Training (OPT) extension after completing their post-completion OPT.

Accommodation options

Nothing compares to life in NYC. It's colorful, fast-paced, and never dull.

Students can choose to live in LIM's recently renovated FOUND Study - Midtown East residence hall, or strike out on their own to find a place that fits their individual needs.

About the FOUND Study - Midtown East Residence Hall:

  • LIM has its own dedicated floors at FOUND Study - Midtown East.
  • This modern high-rise student residence is in the heart of Manhattan at Lexington Ave. and 51st St.—a quick walk from both of our academic buildings, steps from multiple subway and bus lines, and right around the corner from an NYPD police precinct.
  • Renovated in 2022, FOUND Study features double rooms w/private bathrooms, a fitness center, 24hr security, a laundry room, bike storage, a communal kitchen and dining room, as well as numerous other co-living amenities.

For more housing information, including cost and application process, visit the Housing & Residential Life I LIM College webpage.
