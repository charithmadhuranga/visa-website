About University of Central Oklahoma

The University of Central Oklahoma is a campus community in motion—leading, innovating and creating transformative learning opportunities that foster success for their students. As Oklahoma’s largest metropolitan university, UCO provides high-quality, nationally recognized programs that are always evolving and adapting to the needs of its students and the community. This commitment is rooted in their founding in 1890 as the state’s teacher’s college, training those who would educate the next generation of leaders and innovators. We remain dedicated to offering an accessible, affordable education, earning national accolades for social mobility, veterans’ outreach and first-generation student programs.

Guided by the motto “ubi motus est” – which translates to “where movement is” – the university continues to evolve and grow, offering 123 undergraduate and 82 graduate programs, as well as one doctoral program, to its more than 12,500 students from the main campus in Edmond, Oklahoma, and facilities throughout the Oklahoma City metro. UCO's colors were the first adopted of any university in the state, and the Bronze and Blue symbolize their greatest strengths—the illumination of new ideas and the endless expanse of a clear sky above. At UCO, anything is possible!

Why University of Central Oklahoma

  • Location: Located on 210 acres in the heart of Edmond, Oklahoma, UCO offers students the opportunity to live on campus in safe, comfortable dorms just steps away from their classes, the library, student activities and the Wellness Center. Edmond is among the safest cities in the U.S. and the #1 best place to live in Oklahoma. Ranked one of the friendliest cities in the U.S., Oklahoma is the fourth most affordable State to live in.
  • Scholarships: More than $700,000 in scholarships awarded to international students each year. Whether someone is an incoming freshman, transfer student or a continuing Broncho, there are scholarship opportunities available to support their journey.
  • Career Development Center: The Career Development Center at the University of Central Oklahoma helps students actively design their future while offering guidance and support for majors and career exploration, graduate/professional school admission and job search preparation.
  • International Student Experience: The Community Responding to International Students Program (CRISP) is designed to bring international students together with American families. The goal of CRISP is for the families and international students to spend time together at least once a month. This gives international students a firsthand experience of how an American family lives, while American families can learn about other cultures and appreciate the diversity that is represented at UCO.

Master's · Education & Teaching

Education Specialist Degree (Ed.S.) – Education Specialist – School Psychology

4 years Next intake: Aug 2027 Apply by 1 Aug 2027

The Education Specialist degree (Ed.S.) at the University of Central Oklahoma is designed to prepare school psychologists who work at the specialist level with children, youth and families to prevent and solve individual, school- and family-related problems.

Housed within the UCO Department of Psychology, the Ed.S. is a postgraduate degree unique to the field of education, designed to provide knowledge and theory beyond a master's degree.

The school psychology program at UCO is one of three National Association of School Psychologists (NASP)-accredited programs in Oklahoma. Additionally, the Ed.S. program at UCO is the only Council for the Accreditation of Educator Preparation (CAEP)-accredited program leading to state and national certification as a specialist-level school psychologist

Master's · Social Sciences & Psychology

Master of Arts – Addictions Counseling

2 years Next intake: Aug 2027 Apply by 1 Aug 2027

The Master of Arts in Addictions Counseling is a transformative journey that sets you apart in the competitive landscape of addiction counseling programs. Our program is uniquely designed to empower students not only in therapeutic practices but also in the environment of social advocacy for enhanced treatment and facilities.

In the dynamic field of addictions counseling, staying up to date on the constantly changing standards of best practices, legal intricacies and political changes is crucial. Our curriculum places a strong emphasis on these areas, ensuring that our graduates are not only proficient therapists but also effective leaders capable of navigating the complex landscape of addiction treatment.

Recognizing the importance of well-prepared professionals, in 2004 the Oklahoma state legislature established specific requirements for licensed alcohol and drug counselors (LADC). The law mandates a master's degree in alcohol and substance abuse counseling or a master's degree in a behavioral science or counseling-related field that is "content-equivalent" to a graduate degree in alcohol and substance abuse counseling.

Master's · Social Sciences & Psychology

Master of Arts – Counseling Psychology

2 years Next intake: Aug 2027 Apply by 1 Aug 2027

Designed to meet the rigorous standards for licensure as a professional counselor in the state of Oklahoma, our Master of Science in Counseling Psychology program equips graduates with the knowledge and practical skills to become a licensed professional counselor (LPC) in the state of Oklahoma.

Faculty are motivating and supportive, helping you learn the skills needed to make a meaningful impact in diverse settings.

Our graduates don't just earn a degree; they gain access to a myriad of career opportunities. Graduates work in private practice, community mental health centers, youth and family service agencies, marriage and family counseling centers, child guidance clinics, university counseling centers, psychiatric hospitals, drug and alcohol treatment centers and shelters or centers for child abuse and domestic violence. Our graduates are also well-prepared to pursue further doctoral-level training at esteemed universities across the nation, expanding their expertise and career opportunities.

We empower students to tackle pressing societal issues by offering specialized training in areas such as child abuse, domestic violence and substance abuse. This unique focus sets our graduates apart, positioning them as advocates for change in their communities.

MBA · Business & Management

Master of Business Administration (MBA)

16 months Next intake: Aug 2027 Apply by 1 Aug 2027

Often referred to as our “General” MBA, this concentration blends coursework from our MBA core with some electives, with all coursework being developed by industry leaders and experienced, supportive faculty that focus on your success. Designed with flexibility in mind for adult learners, the General MBA program at UCO can be completed in as quickly as 16 months.

With the convenience and affordability of the master's program, the UCO MBA is ideal for students who want to develop the leadership and management skills required in today’s business world. All in-person courses are delivered at UCO's downtown Oklahoma City campus at Santa Fe Plaza.

The UCO College of Business and MBA program is accredited by the Association to Advance Collegiate Schools of Business (AACSB). This accreditation is held by less than five percent of business schools worldwide and demonstrates that our program meets rigorous standards for quality and is dedicated to continuous improvement.
