About University of Massachusetts Lowell

The University of Massachusetts Lowell is a nationally ranked public research university committed to excellence in teaching, career preparation and community engagement. The University provides a supportive, people-first environment that emphasizes inclusivity, well-being and academic success, ensuring its graduates thrive in a global environment.

UMass Lowell offers affordable, experience-based undergraduate and graduate academic programs taught by internationally recognized faculty who conduct research to expand the horizons of knowledge and sustainable practices. The programs span and interconnect the disciplines of business, education, engineering, fine arts, health, humanities, sciences and social sciences. The university continues to build on its founding tradition of innovation, entrepreneurship and partnerships with industry and the community to address challenges facing the region and the world.

UMass Lowell is located in historic mill city of Lowell, Mass., 45-minutes north of Boston.

Accommodation options

UMass Lowell welcomes all students to join the on-campus community regardless of where they live or whether they are a matriculated part-time or full-time student. The University's mission is to provide a safe, inclusive and welcoming experience for all of its students and we are here to answer any questions that students may have as they look to join this community.

Housing for New Freshmen

The Office of Residence Life is committed to making a student's first year experience as comfortable as possible. Most students choose to live in a Living Learning Community (LLC) which provide excellent opportunities to connect with faculty, staff and students who share similar academic interests.

The first year of college offers a wide range of new experiences: living with a roommate, managing money, and organizing free time to meet academic responsibilities. The University's Residence Life Team helps students develop the skills and insights they need to succeed academically with the exciting new opportunities and challenges of college.
