About Southeast Missouri State University

Southeast Missouri State University is a comprehensive university with numerous national and international academic accreditations. Located in Cape Girardeau, Missouri, Southeast Missouri State offers more than 145 undergraduate majors, 100 minors, and more than 75 graduate programs. Southeast continues to focus on staying ahead of changing market demands and students receive unparalleled personal support from their first time on campus to the day they receive their diploma. This instills a strong sense of confidence and lays the foundation for all of their achievements to come.

Southeast Missouri State University provides student‐centered education and experiential learning with a foundation of liberal arts and sciences, embracing a tradition of access, exceptional teaching, and commitment to student success that significantly contributes to the development of the region and beyond.

Whether students have known their career path before reading or are still unsure about what they want to dedicate their life doing, Southeast has the programs, facilities and faculty to help them find the right fit.

Why Southeast Missouri State University

At Southeast, students are not just in the classroom but also in practice. Whether it’s in a lab, the classroom, a stage, a research farm, at Catapult Creative House, in a gallery, around the world, or across the nation, students at Southeast get the necessary experience to excel in their professions. Southeast is renowned for its:

  • Campus Life: Overlooking Mark Twain's Mississipi, Southeast is located in Cape Girardeau, the largest city between St. Louis and Memphis and offers miles of trails, nightlife, and spas.
  • Hands-on experience: Students can take advantage of internships and experiential learning opportunities available in 100 percent of majors.
  • Scholarships and Costs: Southeast is one of the top 2 institutions in Missouri in accreditations and it's in the top 5 of the lowest cost universities in the state. Over $20 million in academic scholarships and 1,500 in endowed scholarships are offered each year.
  • Facilities: Students have access to a modern recreation centre and a one-of-a-kind student aquatic centre.
  • Student Culture and Diversity: Southeast has more than 238 special-interest student organizations and 630 international students from 65 different countries and has a 20:1 student-to-faculty ratio.

Master's · Science & Environment

Master of Science – Biology (Optional Thesis)

2 years Next intake: Aug 2027 Apply by 1 Jul 2027

  • A Master of Science Biology degree is focused on biology. Students will get to tailor the degree with a variety of interdisciplinary courses to choose from, plus the option of taking a thesis or non-thesis option. The curriculum allows students to pursue the area based on personal interest through coursework and independent research. Whether a student is interested in research questions that incorporate the study of genetics, cell biology, tissues, anatomy/physiology, wildlife/organismal biology, there is an advisor at Southeast that possesses the technical expertise to guide through a master’s degree in biology. Whether a student is interested in studying fungi, plants, insects, amphibians, reptiles, or mammals, there is an advisor at Southeast with familiarity with at least one of these groups.
  • There are two degree tracks for graduate studies in the biological sciences. Students can either choose the Plan A option (thesis option) or Plan B option (non-thesis option). The Plan A option culminates with the defense of the findings of an independent research project and a thesis describing the pertinent literature, methodology, and results of the research project. Students often publish aspects of their theses in peer-reviewed journals and present their findings at national and international conferences. The Plan B option culminates with a written critical evaluation on a specific area of study in biology and a written examination.
  • This program is housed within the College of STEM. The College prepares students to find creative solutions to problems. Curiosity and analysis merge, creating students ready to figure things out and then use that knowledge to make things better.

Student life & support

Work while studying

International students can work part-time alongside their studies (subject to visa conditions).

Co-op & placements

Co-op options are limited — ask us about placement-friendly alternatives.

On-campus housing

Students typically arrange off-campus or homestay accommodation.

Accommodation options

Students need to plan their accommodations well in advance of their arrival as all accommodation options fill up quickly, well before the start of the semester.

On-Campus Residence Accommodations

On-Campus residence accommodations are provided per discretion by the school.

Off-Campus Accommodations

The local area has a variety of off-campus rental housing options including single homes, duplexes, apartments and rooms for rent. Students wishing to live off-campus need to research availability on their own and should arrive well before the start of term to do so.

Homestay

There are a wide variety of homestay options available, and our partners do their best to match students and hosts according to their interests and preferences. Homestay hosts include single people, young couples with children and pets, and older couples. All homestay accommodations have been inspected, and all adults in the home have completed a required criminal reference check.
