About Alma College

Located in the city of Alma, a community of 10,000, Alma College features a walkable campus with historic and contemporary buildings. The campus is a block from downtown Alma and within two hours of Michigan beaches and ski areas.

In the more than 130 years since its founding, Alma College has stayed true to its roots by keeping its Scottish heritage alive. Scottish pride thrives in Alma, known affectionately as Scotland USA. Alma College serves as a community partner and the host to the annual Highland Festival, featuring traditional Scottish games, music and revelry during Memorial Day weekend. The festival, one of the largest games in North America, attracts more than 250 bagpipers, world champion highland dancers and world record-holding highland athletes.

An Alma College education seeks to instill a love of learning and celebrate the joy of discovery. Each Alma student will experience a deeply interdisciplinary education that provides opportunity for self-reflection, hands-on learning, and personal and professional development. It will embrace the principle of local, national and global learning communities, celebrate diverse identities, build upon strong mentoring relationships, and integrate rapidly changing technology essential for current and future career paths.

Alma College’s mission is to prepare graduates who think critically, serve generously, lead purposefully and live responsibly as stewards of the world they bequeath to future generations.

Why Alma College:

  • Student Success: 91% of 2021 Alma graduates working, enrolled in graduate school or participating in a service program within six months of graduation
  • Automatic Scholarships: Every student who is accepted will receive a scholarship of some kind
  • The Alma Commitment: If a student follows the path but doesn't graduate on time, Alma will pay their tuition for an additional term. Depending upon the major selected, the college’s commitment to on-time graduation may apply to either a four-year or a 4.5-year academic program.
  • Residence Life: All students are expected to live in college-owned or approved housing through our intentional experiential housing model.

Bachelor's · Business & Management

Bachelor of Arts or Science – Accounting

4 years Next intake: Sep 2027 Apply by 14 Aug 2027

Accounting is the language of business. Job opportunities in today’s business climate are better than ever for accountants, and an Alma College accounting degree provides an excellent foundation for any business professional.

Small classes—taught by experienced certified public accountants (CPAs)—will help students understand the complexities of balance sheets, government regulation, tax law and business strategy.

The field of accounting is rapidly changing in response to increased government regulations, frequent tax law changes, the globalization of business, and the ongoing downsizing and restructuring of corporations.

Accountants are assuming the roles of financial experts, system professionals, management consultants and budget analysts. In this increasingly complex and competitive business environment, accounting has become a dynamic field and new skills will be in great demand.

Graduates are employed in diverse and rewarding career paths including accounting, private industry, government and not-for-profit organizations.

The mission of the Alma College Computer Science program is to provide Alma College students access to the knowledge and skills needed to work effectively with and think critically about computation and data in the 21st century.

Alma College strives to become:

a key contributor to addressing local and regional problems;

a champion of computer science as integral to the liberal arts in the 21st century; and

Bachelor's · Health & Nursing

Bachelor of Science – Nursing

4 years Next intake: Sep 2027 Apply by 14 Aug 2027

In Alma’s Bachelor of Science in Nursing (BSN), students will build a cross-disciplinary foundation in the sciences and humanities. Then, they’ll put their expertise to the test through hands-on learning and clinical placements.

Students will study fundamentals in biology and chemistry — learning to create connections between the disciplines that medicine is grounded in. They’ll take practical and theoretical courses in nursing, along with integrative physiology and health science, psychology and healthcare administration. They’ll also dive into fields like history, political science and philosophy, where students will develop the critical thinking, communication and leadership skills needed to thrive in work and life.

Students will learn from accomplished faculty who share a passion for respectful, compassionate patient care. And they’ll apply everything they study in the classroom to practicums, laboratory work and clinical placements at home and abroad.

On-Campus Residence Accommodations

Alma is a four-year residential college. All students are expected to live in college-owned or approved housing through the intentional experiential housing model. First-year students reside in Bruske and Gelston, and sophomore students reside Newberry and Mitchell halls on North Campus. Juniors and Seniors live on South Campus in either suite-style or apartment living.
