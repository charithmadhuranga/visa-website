About Saint Leo University

Founded in 1889 as Florida's first Catholic institution, Saint Leo University has a rich history of providing students with a transformative, values-centered education. With over 40 majors and minors to choose from, the university's core mission is to inspire students to become their best selves and lead the way for those who follow.

Saint Leo truly is a university of international consequence. The global presence on Saint Leo’s campus has more than doubled over the past 10 years, and they are just getting started. Located in the rolling hills of Central Florida, Saint Leo is now home to more than 750 international students from around the world – that’s nearly one in four students on campus.

Why Saint Leo University

  • Career Services: Career Services connects students to thousands of internships and job opportunities each year. They host over 60 companies on campus and over 1,000 positions and 4,000 internships posted annually on Handshake.
  • Academic Opportunities: Students can enhance their academic journey with study abroad, ROTC, and on-campus employment. International education is a once-in-a-lifetime transformative experience integral to Saint Leo University's mission, and the university makes it easy and affordable, with options including a full semester abroad, shorter faculty-led trips, and service learning opportunities across the United States and the world.
  • Campus Facilities and Resources: Saint Leo offers diverse dining options, faith-based experiences, and creative outlets such as theatre, radio, and a student-run newspaper. There are 4 different food venue locations on the campus, opportunities for Faith and Spirituality Experiences, Music and Theatre Productions, Saint Leo University Radio and Podcast Experiences, and The Lions’ Pride student-run newspaper.
  • Community Engagement: Give back through meaningful service initiatives that strengthen leadership and social responsibility. The university's office has made a commitment to develop its students into servant leaders. They recognize the importance of civic engagement and believe the collegiate experience is maximized with community involvement.
  • Health, Wellness, and Recreation: Get involved and make a difference in the community. Stay active with intramural sports, group fitness classes, an infinity pool, and a state-of-the-art wellness center. Whether for fun or fitness, there are plenty of ways to stay engaged at Saint Leo University.
  • Student Organizations and Leadership: Join over 60+ student clubs and participate in 1,500+ annual events at Saint Leo. From leadership roles in Student Government to networking through the LEAD Scholars Program, there are endless ways to get involved.

Bachelor's · Health & Nursing

Bachelor of Science – Health Care Administration

4 years Next intake: Aug 2027 Apply by 30 Jul 2027

The Healthcare Administration program at Saint Leo University offers students a strategic pathway into the nation’s largest industry. Having recently undergone a comprehensive five-year review, the curriculum is meticulously aligned with current industry standards and emerging growth trends.

Program Overview

The degree provides a multidisciplinary education designed to prepare future leaders for the complexities of modern medical environments. Students engage in a rigorous course of study that includes:

  • Management & Law: Understanding the organizational structure and legal frameworks of medical facilities.
  • Financing & Policy: Analyzing the economic drivers and legislative influences that shape healthcare delivery.
  • Information Technology: Mastering the digital tools and data systems essential for modern patient care.
  • Global Perspective: Integrating international viewpoints into the analysis of healthcare systems.

Faculty and Real-World Experience

Courses are led by a distinguished faculty composed of experienced medical professionals, including current and former medical doctors and healthcare administrators. This ensures that the theoretical aspects of the degree are grounded in practical, real-world application.

The program culminates in a senior-year internship, providing students with intensive, hands-on exposure to the professional challenges and operational opportunities found within the healthcare sector.

MBA · Business & Management

Master of Business Administration (MBA) -Management

2 years Next intake: Jan 2027 Apply by 17 Dec 2026

The Master of Business Administration (MBA) – Management is a postgraduate program designed to develop advanced leadership, strategic, and managerial skills for today’s dynamic business environment. The course prepares students to effectively manage organizations, lead teams, and drive business success across a range of industries.

Students explore core areas such as strategic management, organizational behavior, leadership, marketing, operations, and financial decision-making. The program emphasizes practical application, critical thinking, and problem-solving, enabling students to make informed decisions in complex business situations.

Through case studies, group projects, and research-led learning, students develop strong communication, teamwork, and leadership skills. The course also focuses on innovation, global business perspectives, and ethical management practices.

Graduates are well-prepared for managerial and leadership roles in business, consultancy, entrepreneurship, and organizational development, or for further professional and academic advancement.

Accommodation options

Life on the Saint Leo University campus is pure bliss with sunny Florida skies, palm trees, and plenty of green space. Students will enjoy the breathtaking views of Lake Jovita that can be seen from many of Saint Leo's residence halls. Classrooms, laboratories, and other essential facilities are all within a short walking distance from the residence halls or only a brief shuttle ride away.

All full-time University Campus students are required to live on campus. Exceptions are allowed for those students living and commuting from home, married students, veterans, non-traditional students, or senior students who have earned more than 90 credit hours. The university does not inspect, endorse, or recommend any off-campus properties or external housing. The university thinks students will agree that the Saint Leo campus truly feels like home, and the friendships made in the residence halls at Saint Leo University will last a lifetime. Welcome!

Room and Board cost between $14,840 – $18,600 per academic year. All residents are required to have a meal plan, and the cost of meal plans ranges between $500 - $7,520.
