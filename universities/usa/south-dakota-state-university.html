About South Dakota State University

As South Dakota's largest, most comprehensive higher- education institution, South Dakota State University offers a rich academic experience in an environment of inclusion and access through inspired, student-centered education, creative activities and research, innovation and engagement that improve the quality of life in South Dakota, the region, the nation and the world.

South Dakota State University offers a diverse academic portfolio of majors, minors and specializations. They welcome students to their community of learners and encourage them to become part of the Jackrabbit family where they can discover their dreams and achieve their goals.

Why South Dakota State University?

  • Diversity: South Dakota State University has proudly become the regional center of rich academic and cultural diversity thanks to our large and growing international student population. The hardworking faculty and staff and the friendly community of Brookings continue to make SDSU a welcome home to students from around the world!
  • Location: South Dakota State University is located in Brookings, SD. Brookings is a small city located on the Eastern side of the State of South Dakota (located in the North Central part of the U.S.) and has an estimated population of 22,200 people. For three-straight years, South Dakota State University has been ranked the safest college campus in South Dakota by YourLocalSecurity.com, an ADT authorized provider and partner of SafeStreets.
  • Campus Life: The SDSU campus community is filled with 11,000+ students and staff from a multitude of backgrounds. The diverse campus is perpetually in motion, offering a plethora of events, activities and community involvement opportunities. University student life is abuzz thanks to the multitude of SDSU events and Brookings' status as a thriving college town.

Bachelor of Science – Biochemistry

4 years Next intake: Aug 2027 Apply by 30 Apr 2027

Life is a delicate, intricate series of biochemical reactions. Those who study such biochemical pathways to understand those reactions hold the key to providing solutions for some of humans biggest mysteries- the reason behind human aging and ways to decelerated that process. The causes of Alzheimer diseases and the chances for spreading cancer.

Biochemistry is one of the fastest growing scientific disciplines and also one of the youngest. It is the application of atomic and molecular principles to the function of life processes. Biochemistry is the science that studies the molecular basis for the processes that take place in living organisms-plant, animal or human.

The Bachelor of Science in Biochemistry at the South Dakota State University builds upon a solid foundation in chemistry, and incorporates selected aspects of biology, physics and mathematics to complete the undergraduate degree. Training in biochemistry at the undergraduate level positions students well for careers in medicine, research, pharmaceutical development, and graduate school.

Accommodation options

South Dakota State University offers multiple on-campus housing options, including over 13 residence halls. Living on campus offers many advantages and has been proven to enhance students’ success, which is why South Dakota State University has a two year live-on requirement. Campus housing is home to over 3,700 residents. All unmarried, full-time students within two years of high school graduation must live on campus, unless exempted, space is no longer available, or they are living with family. Residence halls are located close to most academic buildings, dining options, and recreational facilities. In addition to a furnished room, residents enjoy lounge areas, kitchens, and convenient laundry services.

SDSU also offers off-campus housing options such as university apartments and the Southeast University Neighborhood for upper division students (juniors, seniors, graduate students). The apartments provide quality and economical housing for eligible students.

For more information, please visit the Housing Options page.
