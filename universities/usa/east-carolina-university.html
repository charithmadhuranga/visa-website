Overview

About East Carolina University

Located in Greenville, North Carolina, East Carolina University offers a wide range of undergraduate degrees in high-demand disciplines, from STEM occupations to business and education. Their nationally recognized academic programs are taught by award-winning professors. The opportunities there are vast; students can earn a bachelor’s degree, participate in cutting-edge research, create stunning works of art, attend dental or medical school, study abroad and much more.

ECU is consistently recognized for the quality and value of our exceptional college experience. The ECU experience is a complete one – with a thriving student environment, exciting athletics and enriching cultural events. With students from all 100 counties in North Carolina, all 50 states in the US, and 44 foreign countries, they have a vibrant intellectual student community where innovative research and creative expression is encouraged.

Why East Carolina University

  • Student Success: Almost 90% of graduates are either employed or advancing their education within 6 months of graduating. ECU is a national leader in preparing professionals for some of the most-needed careers in the country.
  • Rankings and Recognition: The university has been recognized by the Princeton Review on its annual best colleges list and by Newsweek as the top online university in North Carolina. ECU has also been recognized for its ability to help their students achieve new heights, ranking in the top 10% of schools in the country on the social mobility index.
  • Location: Located in the heart of eastern North Carolina, Greenville is a thriving, student-supportive community widely recognized as a cultural, educational, economic and medical hub. The city’s expansive parks and tree-lined residential neighborhoods merit the “green” in Greenville. It’s location next to the Tar River and Pamlico Sound allows for boating, kayaking, fishing, and other water recreation.

The Department of Finance provides support in the areas of managerial finance, banking, financial services, business law and real estate. Accommodation options

East Carolina University freshmen (defined as students who have completed fewer than 24 hours of college credit – excluding AP, dual enrollment, or early college hours) and first-year students are required to live in college housing for at least two semesters, (as a part of a common experience to bond new students to the university and support them through the crucial first year of college).

Exempted from this policy are students who have reached their 21st birthday prior to the first day of classes; veterans of the U.S. Armed Forces; married students; students with dependent children (living with them); or students who commute to school daily from the permanent, legal residence of their parents (within a 35-mile radius of campus).

Student housing at East Carolina University is based in three neighborhoods: College Hill, West End, and Central. Please refer to the Campus Living page on the school website for more details.
