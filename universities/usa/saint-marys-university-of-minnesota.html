About Saint Mary's University of Minnesota

Saint Mary’s University of Minnesota’s mission and vision reflect its Lasallian Catholic identity, building upon the past and providing direction for the future. What was a local college providing education for young men in 1912 is today an international university serving women and men of every age, race, and nationality. At Saint Mary's, we believe in offering every student a personalized education. We are compassionate, committed, and eager to help you reach your career goals. Our faculty are experts in their fields, and you'll receive a practical education with hands-on experiences that you can bring into your career.

Why Saint Mary's University of Minnesota - Minneapolis?

  • Location: The Minneapolis metro area, known as the Twin Cities (Minneapolis and Saint Paul), offers international students a vibrant mix of academics, career opportunities, and quality of life. With a high standard of living, efficient transit, and a blend of urban and suburban housing, the area is easy to explore. Students enjoy winter sports, summer activities by lakes, global cuisines, and a lively arts scene with music, museums, and festivals. Minneapolis-Saint Paul International Airport (MSP) offers direct flights worldwide, making the Twin Cities an ideal destination for international students.
  • Courses and Faculty: The University offers advanced degrees in business and technology, health and human services, and education, with scholarships available for two programs: DIGA-BE and IT Project Management. Dedicated faculty value their students and provide personalized support throughout your program to help you grow and develop new skills so you can succeed after graduation.
  • Student Services: Support extends beyond the classroom as well. Our on-campus International Student Services office provides assistance for students navigating both academic and social challenges. We frequently host international student lunches, workshops, and events to bring our international students together, fostering friendships that cross the globe. Students receive additional support and resources from our Career Services, Library, Student Central and Writing Center teams.
  • Career Opportunities: The location of the campus offers numerous opportunities for international graduate students, including a strong job market, networking opportunities, and nearby Major Fortune 500 companies to enhance your career prospects. Career Services is there from day one to provide advice and assistance as you think about your professional future.

Master's · Computing & IT

Master of Science – Information Technology Project Management

2 years Next intake: Jan 2027 Apply by 2 Dec 2026

Earn a master’s degree in managing projects within the information technology industry, an area currently experiencing significant growth.

The MS Information Technology Project Management program is open to all applicants and specifically designed for international students, offering on-site coursework in a STEM-designated field and a full time 24-month program for completion.

Whether students' background is in technology or business, this program combines project management and IT courses, preparing them for over 22 million new jobs expected to be created globally in the next decade.

This new innovative program at Saint Mary’s University of Minnesota brings together courses from four key areas within the School of Business and Technology; Cybersecurity, Business Intelligence and Analytics, Data Intelligence and GeoAnalytics, and Project Management.

Learners will gain expertise in advanced IT project management tools and techniques crucial for thriving in today’s competitive market. They will be positioned to become a successful IT project manager who can deliver projects on time, within budget, and to the highest standards.

Eligible international students may receive a scholarship of $5,040, reducing the total program cost from $23,400 to $18,360.

NOTE: This is a STEM designated program.

MBA · Business & Management

Master of Business Administration (MBA)

1.5 years Next intake: Jan 2027 Apply by 2 Dec 2026

The MBA program at Saint Mary’s University of Minnesota is tailored to propel students into leadership roles not only in the business world but also beyond its boundaries. The University understand the importance of flexibility, which is why this program is designed to accommodate students' busy schedule, ensuring they can pursue their MBA without compromising their professional and personal commitments.

The University believes that an MBA should be an investment that pays off, both personally and financially. It’s a proven fact that MBA graduates earn significantly higher salaries than their counterparts with only a bachelor’s degree. This affordable program ensures that this MBA delivers an incredible return on investment, securing students' financial future.

But an MBA is more than just a boost to earning potential; it’s a transformative experience that shapes leadership perspective and equips students with a multidimensional understanding of business. At Saint Mary’s, MBA program goes beyond textbook knowledge. It instills critical thinking, managerial, and decision-making skills that are crucial for success in today’s dynamic business landscape.

Students immerse themselves in a collaborative and diverse community of business leaders, where they’ll gain a global perspective and cultivate an innovative mindset. The esteemed faculty brings a wealth of practical experience to the classroom, ensuring that the skills students learn today are immediately applicable in real-world scenarios. With their guidance and personalized support, the University is committed to students' success throughout the program and long after graduation. Students will also network with peers from Fortune 500 companies and start-ups, learning from each other as they collaborate on class projects.

What Students will Learn

  • Effective and professional communication in various forms
  • Ethical leadership with a focus on character and integrity
  • Evidence-based decision making and critical thinking
  • Leadership for the common good to build better organizations
  • Broad knowledge of business to contribute to organizational success
  • Quantitative analysis
