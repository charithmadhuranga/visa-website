About Northwood University

In 1959, Dr. Arthur E. Turner and Dr. R. Gary Stauffer founded Northwood Institute in Michigan, with a vision of incorporating the principles of American free-enterprise society into higher education, and over the years, it evolved into Northwood University, continuing to uphold these core values and expanding its programs and international presence. Northwood University emphasizes education as an investment in the future, promoting competitive effort, freedom, diversity, and the arts while advocating for equal opportunity and skepticism of large government.

Northwood undergraduates and alumni alike don’t just talk about the future; they actively shape it each day. What makes the Northwood network so strong is its members’ shared aim for excellence, in their classrooms and in their careers.

  • 91% of NU undergraduate students go on to employment or further education post-graduation.
  • 56,000+ alumni from nearly 120 countries across the globe call Northwood University their alma mater.
  • 19 top-ranked majors from College Factual.
  • $19M are granted in scholarships annually.

Why Northwood University

  • Michigan Campus: At Northwood, international students can develop their business and leadership skills by studying at Northwood's residential campus in the safe, welcoming and diverse community of Midland, Michigan.
  • Location: Midland provides unique experiences for every interest and background. Dive into thrilling athletics of all levels, from spectator baseball to recreational tennis leagues. Tap into creative arts with world-class theatrical performances and thought-provoking art galleries. Become part of Midland’s unified community with local outreach events and block parties. Connect with nature in stunning botanical environments and garden walks. A world of culture and adventure is only steps away from NU’s sprawling campus.
  • International Merit Scholarships: Northwood University provides more than $13,000,000 in scholarships to promote higher education and free enterprise.
  • Rankings: Northwood has annually ranked among the best online programs, including its bachelor’s in business degree in 2021 and 2022.
  • International Student Organization (ISO): The International Student Organization (ISO) of Northwood aims to share international cultures through various cultural activities and to help promote global awareness in its campus community. ISO provides a supportive environment for incoming international students and to provide peer support for Northwood's international friends.

Note: Admitted students will be required to pay a $500 commitment deposit, of which $300 is refundable with proof of visa denial. The remaining $200 is retained as the i20 processing fee. Once students pay the deposit and submit their financial support documents, Northwood University will send an I-20 document and information regarding obtaining a student visa, orientation, academic course registration and housing.

On-campus housing

Students typically arrange off-campus or homestay accommodation.

Accommodation options

All incoming students are expected to complete a total of four (4) semesters living in university residence halls (excluding summers) at Northwood University unless they live with a parent or relative within a 50-mile radius (other exemptions defined in the housing contract may apply).

On-Campus Residence Accommodations

Dubois and Miner Residence Halls

Northwood’s traditional, dorm room-style residence halls are available for all students. Incoming first-year students will live in Dubois or Miner Hall.

Naegele Village Apartments

Naegele Village offers single-style housing to returning Northwood students.

North Village Apartments

Northwood University’s newest on-campus housing, featuring modern apartments with one, two or four bedroom layouts – and also contains new amenities for Northwood students.

For more information, see Northwood's Housing webpage.
