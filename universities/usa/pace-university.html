About Pace University (in partnership with Kaplan International)

Since 1906, Pace University has produced thinking professionals by providing high-quality education for the professions with a firm base in liberal learning amid the advantages of the New York Metropolitan Area. At the heart of Pace’s academic values is a historic commitment to excellence in teaching, as manifested by small classes, an emphasis on skill development and critical thinking, special tutoring and support services, and academic advising. Academic experiences at Pace emphasize teaching from both a practical and theoretical perspective, drawing on the expertise of full-time and adjunct faculty members who balance academic preparation with professional experience to bring a unique dynamic to the classroom.

Why Pace University (in partnership with Kaplan International)

  • Diversity: A diverse culture with an endless list of things to explore and experience. New York City offers opportunities for international students they simply can’t get anywhere else.
  • Location: Located in New York City, the largest city in the USA. Pace is a leading university for employability, and an expert in helping international students get jobs after graduation. Students will study in the exciting heart of Lower Manhattan, next to the famous Brooklyn Bridge and just a 10 minute walk from Wall Street. Surrounded by amazing cultural, dining, and shopping venues, students can also access the rest of the city through New York's extensive public transportation system.
  • Selecting the USA as a Destination: With a diverse culture rich with opportunities, it’s no wonder the USA is the top destination in the world for international students. Degrees earned in the USA are recognized all around the world for their prestige and value.

Student life & support

Work while studying

International students can work part-time alongside their studies (subject to visa conditions).

Co-op & placements

Offers co-op or work-placement options in selected programs.

On-campus housing

Students typically arrange off-campus or homestay accommodation.

Accommodation options

Students need to plan their accommodations well in advance of their arrival as all accommodation options fill up quickly, well before the start of the semester.

On-Campus Residence Accommodations

On-Campus residence accommodations are provided per discretion by the school.

Off-Campus Accommodations

The local area has a variety of off-campus rental housing options including single homes, duplexes, apartments and rooms for rent. Students wishing to live off-campus need to research availability on their own and should arrive well before the start of term to do so.

Homestay

There are a wide variety of homestay options available, and our partners do their best to match students and hosts according to their interests and preferences. Homestay hosts include single people, young couples with children and pets, and older couples. All homestay accommodations have been inspected, and all adults in the home have completed a required criminal reference check.
