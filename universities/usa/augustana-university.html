About Augustana University

The institution which was to become Augustana College began with the foundation of Hillsboro Academy in Hillsboro, Illinois, in 1835. By 1846, a Scandinavian Lutheran group had changed the name to “The Literary and Theological Institute of The Lutheran Church of the Far West.”

In 2015 the name Augustana College changed to Augustana University.

Inspired by Lutheran scholarly tradition and the liberal arts, Augustana provides an education of enduring worth that challenges the intellect, fosters integrity, and integrates faith with learning and service in a diverse world.

Their academic programs and location in Sioux Falls, South Dakota, are top reasons why students choose Augustana. With over 50 majors and pre-professional programs and a city rich with internship and research opportunities, cultural activities, shopping, dining, and recreation, it’s no surprise that so many international students call Augustana home.

Why Augustana University

  • Campus Life: Their thriving campus life is bolstered by the larger Sioux Falls community. A thriving metropolitan center, Sioux Falls is consistently ranked among the best places in America to live thanks to its safety, economy, and variety of industry. Augustana is proud to be part of such a progressive city and tries to give as much to it as it has given to AU. On campus, students and Sioux Falls residents alike will find opportunities for research, athletic competition, and seasonal events such as their annual Viking Days celebration.
  • Student Life: Consider the friendships that could be built with classmates from 42 states and 35 countries around the globe. Picture the memories that will be made when involved in intramurals, campus clubs and organizations, the performing and visual arts, student government and campus ministry. Imagine what’s possible when the world is the classroom.
  • Learn. Play. Live. Stay.: Sioux Falls is South Dakota's largest city, with an estimated population of 202,600 — with 6,750 new residents in 2021. Featuring a robust culture and economy, as well as countless options for adventure, entertainment, food and sought-after internship and clinical opportunities, Sioux Falls is a great place to learn, live, play and stay.

Bachelor of Arts – Computer Information Systems

4 years Next intake: Sep 2027 Apply by 1 May 2027

The Computer Information Systems (CIS) major is designed to incorporate the tools and techniques of management with advanced computer technology. The goal of this program is to enable students to analyze, design, implement, evaluate, control, and manage computer-based information systems for businesses, government, and other organizations. The major is designed to prepare students for positions as CIS consultants, management services advisors, systems analysts and designers, programming managers, managers of information services and data processing departments, and other similar positions.

Augustana University’s CIS major has been designed to follow the underlying philosophy of the model curricula that have been constructed by the professional associations Association for Computing Machinery and Data Processing Management Association.

On-Campus Residence Accommodations

Residency Requirement

As a residential university, Augustana affirms the value of living in community and therefore requires students to have an on-campus living experience. Augustana’s two-year* residency policy requires students who are are enrolled full-time to reside in campus housing and purchase a residential meal plan during their first and second year* unless the student:

  1. Identifies as a part-time student during the academic year covered by this housing agreement (enrolls in less than 12 credits in both fall and spring semesters), or
  2. Is married or has parent/guardian status for a dependent child, or
  3. Resides at the same address in the Sioux Falls area with a parent or legal guardian, or
  4. Graduated from high school at least two years before the commencement of this academic year.

*Residency requirement is based upon the number of years a student has attended college or experienced other post-high school experiences as opposed to academic credits earned.

  • Permission to be waived from the residency requirement is granted by the Student Affairs office
  • Meal plan requirement: First and second year students living in campus housing are required to participate in a residential meal plan. Third and fourth year students must contact the Student Affairs office to waive the meal plan requirement or enroll in an approved meal plan.

Off-Campus Accommodations

Augustana requires full-time students to live in on-campus housing through the end of their second year. The high school graduation date, not the academic classification based upon credits, is used to determine their classification in relation to the two-year residency requirement.
