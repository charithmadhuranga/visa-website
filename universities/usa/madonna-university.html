About Madonna University

In 1937, Madonna University (then known as Presentation of the Blessed Virgin Mary Junior College) was established by Mother Mary DeSales Tocka and her council in the Felician Central Convent in Livonia, with a first year enrollment of 18 Sisters. Madonna University instituted master's-level programs in 1982 and became a university in 1991.

Through this growth and development, the institution has become one of the nation's largest Franciscan universities with a combined undergraduate and graduate student body of approximately 3,000 students. Madonna University exemplifies the fine tradition of Catholic and Franciscan scholarship that has contributed significantly to the intellectual and professional development in society. With alumni on every continent except Antarctica, Madonna University graduates apply their knowledge and skills in meaningful service around the world – improving the lives of others, while excelling in their own professions.

Why Madonna University

  • Facilities: A testament to the University’s reverence for creation, the Franciscan Center for Science and Media achieved Gold status for Leadership in Energy and Environmental Design, first in Livonia. A showcase of sustainability, this gorgeous green building houses state-of-the-art television studios and science labs. Technology permeates Madonna’s campus in smart classrooms, all-digital editing suites, mobile learning tools, social networking, and electronic signage.
  • Community Engagement: Students at Madonna incorporate community service with civic learning to gain experiences beyond the classroom, volunteering more than 27,000 hours each year from Detroit to South Carolina to Latin America. Madonna was the first private university in Michigan to be awarded the Community Engagement Classification by the Carnegie Foundation for the Advancement of Teaching. The University has been named to the President’s Higher Education Community Service Honor Roll since 2006.
  • Student Success: Student achievement is recognized locally and nationally with awards from the American Red Cross, Michigan Campus Compact, the State Dept. of Education, and others. Madonna alumni compose a veritable “who’s who” of authors, business owners, executives, religious leaders, doctors, lawyers, hospital and school administrators, fire chiefs, police chiefs (more than 80), nurses, artists, and teachers.

MBA · Business & Management

Master of Business Administration (MBA) – Business Administration

2 years Next intake: May 2027 Apply by 31 Jan 2027

Madonna University’s Master of Business Administration (MBA) program is intended for students who desire a broad understanding of business and how it relates to the social, political, and economic forces confronting it.

Whether advancing a career in business, healthcare, finance, supply chain, and the for-profit and non-profit sectors, Madonna University’s ACBSP-accredited MBA program provides the tools needed to take students to the next level of their career.

The rigorous MBA curriculum dives deep into advanced business concepts, fostering expertise and accelerated growth in leadership ability. The coursework provides opportunities for hands-on learning through applied learning in case studies and projects.

Graduates of Madonna University’s MBA program:

  • demonstrate a knowledge of core business fields of study by: obtaining a thorough understanding of concepts, terminology, models, and perspectives; identifying problems and generating solutions; utilizing interdisciplinary critical thinking skills; and applying a cross-cultural perspective.
  • exhibit well-developed communication skills that are expected and desired of a business professional working in a culturally and intellectually diverse global environment.
  • integrate quantitative information and the use of technology into the decision making process.
  • apply and critically analyze the strategic decision making process, including financial forecasting and operational planning, for policy making in both domestic and international companies.
  • identify ethical and corporate social responsibility issues and design better approaches to these issues.

Throughout the program, students interact closely with faculty and fellow classmates, expanding their networks of like minded, forward-thinking business professionals.

On-Campus Residence Accommodations

Madonna University students are highly recommended to stay in Madonna's On-Campus residence accommodations, provided per discretion by the school.

Madonna University offers these types of residence accommodations (all require a meal-plan):

  • Apartment Living Center: These are five bedroom, two bath air-conditioned apartment units. All units are single gender. Bedrooms are fully furnished, including a bed frame, twin XL mattress, desk, desk chair, nightstand, closet, and dresser. The living space includes a couch, love seat, two end tables, coffee table, entertainment stand, dining room table, and four dining room chairs.
  • Living Learning Community Center: This Center contains double and single rooms. All floors contain a male and female wing. Bedrooms are fully furnished, including a bed frame, twin XL mattress, desk, desk chair, nightstand, closet, and dresser. Each wing contains four semi-private bathrooms, shared between residents of the same gender on each floor. Each floor also includes a common area and a kitchen.
  • University Center: The University Center contains small single, large single, double, and quad rooms. Bedrooms are fully furnished, including a bed frame, twin XL mattress, desk, desk chair, sink, closet, and dresser. Each wing contains community bathrooms, shared between residents of the same gender on each floor, a common area, and a kitchenette with a microwave, full-size refrigerator and sink. A full kitchen is located on the ground floor, which includes a full-size stove/oven, microwave, full-size refrigerator, sink, vending machines, tables, and chairs.

Off-Campus Accommodations

Students are not encouraged to find off-campus accommodations because local transit is limited and makes it difficult to get to campus for classes. Students wishing to live off-campus need to research availability on their own and should arrive well before the start of term to do so.
