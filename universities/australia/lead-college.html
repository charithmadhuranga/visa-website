About Lead College

Lead College, a leader in vocational education and training, is a Registered Training Organisation (RTO) that provides quality training and education services to domestic and international students, focusing on the fields of Business, Health, Community Services, Hospitality and Information Technology. Lead College empowers students in their pursuit of learning, discovery, leadership, and engagement. They prepare students to be successful leaders for a changing world through its distinctive commitments to hands-on learning and faculty-student mentoring.

Why Lead College - Melbourne:

Quality: Lead College provides quality services and products to their staff, learners and clients. They spend the time, money and effort to ensure that their trainers are experienced and skillful, using quality resources and tools in modern facilities.

Learner Oriented Approach: LEAD College listens to students and their needs to help each student achieve their best during and after the course. Teaching methods can be customized to be more efficient with the various groups yet compliant with LEAD College policies and procedures.

Teamwork: Lead College is a strong believer in teamwork where people can present different ideas and techniques to come up with the best scenario possible for all stakeholders. They believe that the ‘whole’ of the organisation is greater than the sum of its parts.

Bachelor's · Hospitality & Tourism

College Certificate III – Commercial Cookery + College Certificate IV – Commercial Cookery + College Diploma – Hospitality Management + Advanced College Diploma – Hospitality Management

31 months Next intake: Jan 2027 Apply by 1 Jan 2027

Packaging of courses is popular with international students. The advantages are it allows students to combine two or more courses to gain the knowledge and skills that will provide a pathway for their future careers and studies.

A Certificate III in Commercial Cookery reflects the role of commercial cooks who use a wide range of well-developed cookery skills and sound knowledge of kitchen operations to prepare food and menu items. Using discretion and judgement, they work with some independence and under limited supervision using plans, policies and procedures to guide work activities.

The College Certificate IV in Commercial Cookery qualification reflects the role of commercial cooks who have a supervisory or team leading role in the kitchen. They operate independently or with limited guidance from others and use discretion to solve non-routine problems.

The Diploma of Hospitality Management qualification reflects the role of highly skilled senior operators who use a broad range of hospitality skills combined with managerial skills and sound knowledge of industry to coordinate hospitality operations. They operate independently, have responsibility for others and make a range of operational business decisions.

The Advanced Diploma of Hospitality Management reflects the role of highly skilled senior managers who use a broad range of hospitality skills combined with specialised managerial skills and substantial knowledge of industry to coordinate hospitality operations. They operate with significant autonomy and are responsible for making strategic business management decisions.

This qualification provides a pathway to work in any hospitality industry sector and for a diversity of employers including restaurants, hotels, motels, catering operations, clubs, pubs, cafés, and coffee shops.

This qualification allows for multi-skilling and for specialisation in accommodation services, cookery, food and beverage and gaming.

Student life & support

Work while studying

International students can work part-time alongside their studies (subject to visa conditions).

Co-op & placements

Co-op options are limited — ask us about placement-friendly alternatives.

On-campus housing

Students typically arrange off-campus or homestay accommodation.

Accommodation options

Students need to plan their accommodations well in advance of their arrival as all accommodation options fill up quickly, well before the start of the semester.

Off-Campus Accommodations

The local area has a variety of off-campus rental housing options including single homes, duplexes, apartments and rooms for rent. Students wishing to live off-campus need to research availability on their own and should arrive well before the start of term to do so.

Homestay

There are a wide variety of homestay options available, and our partners do their best to match students and hosts according to their interests and preferences. Homestay hosts include single people, young couples with children and pets, and older couples. All homestay accommodations have been inspected, and all adults in the home have completed a required criminal reference check.
