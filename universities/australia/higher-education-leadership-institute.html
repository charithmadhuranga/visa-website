About Higher Education Leadership Institute

Higher Education Leadership Institute is a dynamic Australian Institute of Higher Education offering postgraduate degrees in Melbourne. They help students every trimester transition from study to rewarding careers through their courses.

Their primary objective is to provide, foster and promote academic rigor and professional attributes in each individual student. They design engaging courses so their students can develop the capabilities and skills to assume positions of responsibility in both organizations and society. Their belief is that education and professional development are the keys to sustained success in the changing business and social contexts of today.

In addition, they are focused on providing students with a safe and nurturing environment that encourages creativity and growth. At HELI, they understand the transformative process students experience engaging in higher education and how that impacts their career and wider aspirations. They endeavor to provide their students with the education, advice and capabilities to flourish wherever they choose to work and live. Why HELI:

  • Campus: Higher Education Leadership Institute’s campus is centrally located at Level 1, 399 at Lonsdale street. The campus boasts of fully- equipped classrooms with modern technology, student recreation area and kitchen. The study areas are quiet, with wifi access.
  • Culture: For those not familiar with Melbourne, it is considered the arts capital of Australia and offers a sophisticated, metropolitan lifestyle. There is a thriving laneway culture with a range of cafes, restaurants and bars to explore.
  • An escape from city life is never far away with the Yarra Valley wine region and the Great Ocean Road only a few hours drive away.
  • Location: The HELI Melbourne campus is located in the heart of the city. Melbourne has an extensive train, bus and tram network with discounts available to full-time students

Offers co-op or work-placement options in selected programs.

On-campus housing

Students typically arrange off-campus or homestay accommodation.

Accommodation options

Students need to plan their accommodations well in advance of their arrival as all accommodation options fill up quickly, well before the start of the semester.

On-Campus Residence Accommodations

On-Campus residence accommodations are provided per discretion by the school.

Off-Campus Accommodations

The local area has a variety of off-campus rental housing options including single homes, duplexes, apartments and rooms for rent. Students wishing to live off-campus need to research availability on their own and should arrive well before the start of term to do so.

Homestay

There are a wide variety of homestay options available, and our partners do their best to match students and hosts according to their interests and preferences. Homestay hosts include single people, young couples with children and pets, and older couples. All homestay accommodations have been inspected, and all adults in the home have completed a required criminal reference check.
