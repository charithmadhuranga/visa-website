About Sydney Metropolitan Institute of Technology (Sydney Met)

Sydney Metropolitan Institute of Technology (Sydney Met) is a leading private higher education provider in Australia, distinguished by its vision for socially responsible, culturally engaged, and high-quality education.

Sydney Met is driven to provide transformative educational opportunities that are responsive to the changing needs of our culturally diverse students, enabling them to realise their full potential and nurturing them into future leaders to tackle challenges and generate transformative impacts as active participants in shaping a better world.

Sydney Met is registered by the Australian Government Agency, TEQSA (Tertiary Education Quality and Standards Authority). It offers TEQSA-accredited undergraduate degrees: the Bachelor of Business (Entrepreneurship), the Bachelor of Information Technology (BIT), and the Bachelor of Social Work (BSW), and Undergraduate Certificates in Information Technology and Business. Our courses are registered with the Australian Government's CRICOS, enabling Sydney Met to teach international students.

Why Sydney Met College

  • Campus Life: The Campus Building is conveniently located, just 100 meters away from Townhall Train Station, and close to Shopping centers, Light rail, Darling Harbour and entertainment areas.
  • Facilities: On campus, the Sydney Met College offers spacious classrooms, reading and meeting rooms, a computer room with printing facilities, a library with reading areas, a seminar hall, a kitchen and recreational area, and campus-wide Wi-Fi, together with an outstanding campus support staff and network.
  • Enhanced teaching environment: The Sydney Met College provides a transformational educational experience to students, blending high-quality and research-informed teaching and learning, with culturally engaged support and services, encouraging impactful research and scholarship of staff and students, and developing motivation and drive for social responsibility and impacts.

Bachelor's · Computing & IT

Bachelor of Information Technology (BIT)

3 years Next intake: Feb 2027 Apply by 1 Feb 2027

The Bachelor of Information Technology (BIT) will build a student’s knowledge and skills in the theoretical, technical, and practical aspects of information technology. The Bachelor of Information Technology (BIT) offers two distinct majors—Information Systems and Cyber Security—both designed to equip students with specialized knowledge and practical skills to excel in today’s technology-driven world. The Information Systems major focuses on enhancing business processes and decision-making through the design, implementation, and management of modern information systems. Students gain a strong foundation in theoretical and practical aspects, enabling them to solve complex business problems using technology. On the other hand, the Cyber Security major delves into safeguarding digital environments and protecting critical infrastructure from cyber threats. Students develop expertise in cyber security protocols, risk management, and threat mitigation while addressing contemporary security challenges.

Both programs emphasize critical thinking, creativity, and innovation, preparing graduates for diverse career opportunities and entrepreneurial ventures. With a focus on contributing to societal advancements, graduates of these majors are well-equipped to leverage their skills to drive social, cultural, and economic progress in their respective fields.

Master's · Social Sciences & Psychology

Graduate Diploma in Human and Community Services ((GDHCS) + Master of Social Work (MSW)

3 years Next intake: Feb 2027 Apply by 1 Feb 2027

The Graduate Diploma in Human and Community Services (GDHCS) is an AQF Level 8 qualification. It provides a pathway for professionals seeking to enhance their skills and knowledge in human and community services, community engagement and support. This course emphasises the principles of social justice, human rights, and inclusive practice to address the needs of diverse and vulnerable populations.

This course provides theoretical and practical insights into the human and community services field, enabling graduates to make meaningful contributions to service delivery, advocacy, and community development. Students will learn about issues and opportunities for working in human services in the Australian community. Students will be exposed to various areas to sociology, policy making, indigenous studies and inter-cultural communication. At the end of this course graduate will be able to apply knowledge and skills across a wide range of human and community services issues.

The Master of Social Work (MSW) at Sydney Met is an AQF Level 9 program designed to meet the Australian Social Work Education and Accreditation Standards (ASWEAS 2023). Developed in consultation with industry experts, the course emphasizes a career-focused approach, preparing students for competency in social work through rigorous academic and practical training.

The program spans two years of full-time study, though students have the option to accelerate completion through Sydney Met’s trimester system. A crucial component of the MSW is the 1,000 hours of integrated field education, which provides hands-on experience in real-world social work settings. This combination of theoretical learning and practical application ensures that graduates are equipped to contribute meaningfully to the social work sector, addressing diverse community needs and promoting social justice.

Master's · Computing & IT

Graduate Diploma in Information Technology (GDIT) + Master of Information Technology (MIT)

3 years Next intake: Feb 2027 Apply by 1 Feb 2027

The Graduate Diploma in Information Technology has a strong technical focus that will open doors to advancing your career in this rapidly evolving industry—whether you are new to the sector or wish to refresh your skills to get ahead. Industry leaders teach the course, giving you the skills and knowledge to advance your technical career.

The information technology sector offers strong employment prospects to people with appropriate skills and qualifications. To work in this dynamic and complex industry, students refresh their knowledge to keep pace with change. In this course, students will develop foundational programming skills with our hands-on units and tailor their studies to meet their career and professional development goals. In particular, students will develop and extend their foundational IT skills and knowledge in cyber security, AI, and database development to ensure they can add maximum value in the workplace.

Articulated Program: The course is part of an articulated program of study and allows students to build on their studies to achieve the right level of qualification for them.

The Master of Information Technology (MIT) at Sydney Met, an AQF Level 9 course, is crafted to align with the Australian Computer Society’s professional standards, emphasizing both technical expertise and ethical IT practices. Developed with industry consultation, the program prepares students for careers in the digital economy, focusing on high-demand fields such as Cyber Security, Networking, Data Science, and Information Systems. Students can complete the program in 2 years (full-time) or 1.5 years (accelerated). Practical experience is embedded through capstone projects and work-integrated learning. For added flexibility, students who cannot complete the MIT may exit with a Graduate Certificate or Graduate Diploma in Information Technology.

MBA · Business & Management

Graduate Diploma in Business Administration (GDBA) + Master of Business Administration (MBA)

3 years Next intake: Feb 2027 Apply by 1 Feb 2027

The Graduate Diploma in Business Administration at Sydney Met is designed for tomorrow's entrepreneurs, business leaders, and those who want to consolidate work experience or advance their careers in the exciting field of entrepreneurship.

The course introduces students to key entrepreneurship and business concepts grounded in theory. Core subjects are from our MBA program and provide an immersive experience with students from diverse industry, cultural and educational backgrounds. Students will learn the fundamentals of business to gain an understanding of the core aspects of entrepreneurship and business administration and build their strategic business capabilities. They also gain market insights and acquire key knowledge and skills to make informed decisions. Students will gain a holistic strategic business point-of-view required to operate effectively in an increasingly competitive and changing global business world.

The Graduate Diploma in Business Administration is designed by academics and entrepreneurs who are committed to leading their careers and businesses to new heights. Students will learn how to develop critical strategies to guide their entrepreneurial and business plans by developing and employing dynamic leadership skills.

Accreditation: This course is accredited by TEQSA (Tertiary Education and Quality Standards Agency) and is consistent with the AQF (Australian Qualifications Framework). TEQSA regulates qualifications in the Australian education system, meaning Sydney Met is recognised as providing quality education that aligns with Australia's Higher Education Standards.

The Master of Business Administration (MBA) at Sydney Met is designed to meet AQF Level 9 standards, preparing graduates for senior roles by building advanced skills in areas such as economics, marketing, leadership, and accounting. Industry consultation has informed its curriculum, which offers flexibility through core units and the choice of majors and electives, enabling students to tailor their studies. With options for real-world problem-solving and industry networking, the MBA is structured for completion in a standard pathway. Students may opt to exit early with a Graduate Diploma or Graduate Certificate, providing alternative qualification pathways based on progression.
