About The University of Newcastle College of International Education (Kaplan Australia)

The University of Newcastle College of International Education serves as the official on-campus pathway college to the University of Newcastle, facilitating entry to over 130 undergraduate and postgraduate degrees at a world-class institution. Its primary objective is to prepare students for success in their academic pursuits. Students enrolled at UNCIE will benefit from small class sizes and personalized support, enabling them to acquire the English language and academic skills necessary to excel at university and complete their degrees with confidence. Additionally, students will have access to the exceptional facilities and services offered by the University of Newcastle, which include seven state-of-the-art libraries housing 1.3 million resources, over 150 clubs and societies, complimentary career counseling, and a Centre for Teaching and Learning that provides drop-in sessions and academic workshops.

Why Choose the University of Newcastle College of International Education

  • Pathway to Higher Education: The University of Newcastle College of International Education provides an alternative pathway to over 130 degrees at the University of Newcastle, which was ranked among the top 200 universities globally in 2020.
  • Campus: The new campus building, situated in the heart of the city, is renowned for its state-of-the-art facilities and industry-standard equipment. Students will become part of a welcoming and vibrant student community, and with ongoing support from faculty and student services staff, they will have a solid foundation to achieve their educational and career aspirations.
  • City: Newcastle is a picturesque harbor city, celebrated for its expansive sandy beaches and numerous coastal walks and bike rides, with cafes and bars located along the waterfront, all within a five-minute walk from the campus. Furthermore, accommodation in Newcastle is significantly more affordable compared to other cities across Australia.

Student life & support

Work while studying

International students can work part-time alongside their studies (subject to visa conditions).

Co-op & placements

Offers co-op or work-placement options in selected programs.

On-campus housing

Students typically arrange off-campus or homestay accommodation.

Accommodation options

Students need to plan their accommodations well in advance of their arrival as all accommodation options fill up quickly, well before the start of the semester.

On-Campus Residence Accommodations

On-Campus residence accommodations are provided per discretion by the school.

Off-Campus Accommodations

The local area has a variety of off-campus rental housing options including single homes, duplexes, apartments and rooms for rent. Students wishing to live off-campus need to research availability on their own and should arrive well before the start of term to do so.

Homestay

There are a wide variety of homestay options available, and our partners do their best to match students and hosts according to their interests and preferences. Homestay hosts include single people, young couples with children and pets, and older couples. All homestay accommodations have been inspected, and all adults in the home have completed a required criminal reference check.
