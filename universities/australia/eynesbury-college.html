Overview

About Eynesbury College

Eynesbury College offers one of the most comprehensive range of pathway programs in South Australia, which provides maximum flexibility in assisting students to gain entry to their chosen degree pathway and in preparing them for academic success at University. As a leading pathway provider for more than 30 years, we have successfully prepared thousands of students for university in Australia. Our specialist approach combines small class sizes, experienced teachers and close academic guidance to support students in academic preparation for university.

Within the city of Adelaide, Eynesbury has an excellent reputation. Eynesbury students are expected to be independent learners, but also enjoy significant academic support which prepares Foundation Program Studies students for direct entry into their program at The University of Adelaide, or University of South Australia, or any other university in Australia.

Why Eynesbury College - Adelaide

  • Campus: Eynesbury College is located in the centre of Adelaide city, near Chinatown and the Central Market precinct. It’s a short walk, or a free tram ride, to both The University of Adelaide and University of South Australia.
  • Facilities: The campus has five levels of modern, purpose-built student facilities, equipment and learning resources. Accessible student support can be found at reception located on the Ground floor, where our Student and Academic Services team are located. Diploma staff offices are on Level Two, ELICOS staff offices are on Level Three and FSP staff offices are on Level Four. Level Five is home to our Resource Centre, where a staff member is also available to assist. Computer rooms with access to photocopying, printing and scanning can be found on Ground Level, Level Two, Level Three, Level Four and in the Resource Centre on Level Five. Student kitchen facilities including access to hot water, microwaves, fridges, toasted sandwich makers and vending machines exist on Level One, Level Two, Level Three and Level Four.
  • Culture: Adelaide is a safe, friendly and affordable city for students. We’re the 3rd most livable city in the world (Economist Intelligence Unit 2021) with a great lifestyle, fantastic weather and a very affordable cost of living. The iGraduate survey revealed our students’ contentment with life in Adelaide; 96% were satisfied with both their arrival to Adelaide, and their living arrangements in our beautiful city.
  • Other: Eynesbury College Diploma and Foundation programs attract students from many different countries around the world

Student life & support

Work while studying

International students can work part-time alongside their studies (subject to visa conditions).

Co-op & placements

Co-op options are limited — ask us about placement-friendly alternatives.

On-campus housing

Students typically arrange off-campus or homestay accommodation.

Accommodation options

Students need to plan their accommodations well in advance of their arrival as all accommodation options fill up quickly, well before the start of the semester.

On-Campus Residence Accommodations

On-Campus residence accommodations are provided per discretion by the school.

Off-Campus Accommodations

The local area has a variety of off-campus rental housing options including single homes, duplexes, apartments and rooms for rent. Students wishing to live off-campus need to research availability on their own and should arrive well before the start of term to do so.

Homestay

There are a wide variety of homestay options available, and our partners do their best to match students and hosts according to their interests and preferences. Homestay hosts include single people, young couples with children and pets, and older couples. All homestay accommodations have been inspected, and all adults in the home have completed a required criminal reference check.
