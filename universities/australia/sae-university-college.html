Overview

About SAE University College

SAE Creative Media Institute has been delivering outstanding industry-focused education since 1976. They have set the benchmark for Higher Education and VET qualifications in creative media because they know these industries and they know how to train industry-ready graduates. SAE is the world’s leading educator in creative industries because they work closely with their industry partners, they employ specialist teachers with real-world experience, and they deliver personalised education in world-class facilities. SAE has 47 campuses in 23 countries. Their students and teachers are a unique, diverse and creative community who come together for a collective purpose – outstanding education opportunities. SAE courses are Australian Government accredited and internationally recognised.

Why SAE Creative Media Institute - Sydney

  • Industry-Informed Coursework: Their specialised courses in creative media are responsive to the dynamic and evolving nature of these industries. They keep their finger on the pulse and think outside the square to help students reach their potential and realise their dreams. The curriculum is rigorous and based on practical and technical experiences, and grounded in theory and critical analysis.
  • Practical Training: Recognizing the need for individual, practical training, SAE pioneered their hands-on, industry focused, and personalised curriculum, delivered to students in state-of-the-art facilities.

Bachelor's · Arts, Media & Design

Bachelor of Creative Industries

2 years – 3 years Next intake: Feb 2027 Apply by 1 Feb 2027

The Bachelor of Creative Industries allows students to combine their skills across various media and creative technologies, preparing them for diverse career pathways. The program brings together a variety of units across SAE’s disciplines in film, music, audio, animation, design, virtual reality, augmented reality, and games development.

Alongside specialist creative skills in multiple disciplines, students will craft their communication, project management, and entrepreneurial abilities. They will work with immersive audio-visual content, virtual and real events, and craft a self-directed industry-connected project, pushing students to think innovatively, creatively, and technically within a complex industry ecosystem.

With a Bachelor of Creative Industries, graduates will be ready for cutting-edge industry roles using modern creative business concepts and strategies. Career options include Digital Content Producer, Event Producer, Arts Administrator, or Multimedia Entrepreneur.

Students have the opportunity to cross-collaborate with students from all disciplines. The cross-collaboration of Creative Industries students with other Animation, Audio, Games Development, and Design students allows for highly sophisticated project-based learning and applications. Working with teams of diverse skills and backgrounds also simulates real-world industry and provides experience in project management and team management.

In addition to project-based learning, Bachelor of Creative Industries students are provided the opportunity to undertake workplace internships with companies in a variety of industries and work with industry mentors to develop career readiness.

Master's · Computing & IT

Master of Information Technology

2 years Next intake: Feb 2027 Apply by 1 Feb 2027

SAE’s Master of Information Technology (MIT) is a unique course shaped by our creative legacy, blending innovation and cutting-edge technology to empower the next generation of digital pioneers.

Developed in line with ACS standards, this course will see you sharpen your technical skills in mobile and software development, artificial intelligence applications, data analysis, and cyber security. You’ll also develop in-demand soft skills like workflow management and teamwork, critical thinking and effective communication.

The practical, project-based approach to learning lets the students explore the challenging, industry-based briefs that you’ll find in the workforce. Implement software development solutions for real-world scenarios, develop team-based skills using project management tools, and facilitate problem identification and assessment of your solutions.

Students will also have the opportunity to apply their expertise to dynamic, cross-discipline projects with their SAE peers. These projects will showcase the students' abilities as a reflective IT professional and will form the basis of their portfolio of work, fit to impress prospective employers or clients.

Round off the studies with a capstone project of the students' choice, designed to demonstrate their mastery of the skills you’ve developed throughout their studies. Curate an entrepreneurial or industry project, where they’ll produce comprehensive documentation that addresses the project development, deployment, and maintenance lifecycle.

Student life & support

Work while studying

International students can work part-time alongside their studies (subject to visa conditions).

Co-op & placements

Offers co-op or work-placement options in selected programs.

On-campus housing

Students typically arrange off-campus or homestay accommodation.

Accommodation options

Students need to plan their accommodations well in advance of their arrival as all accommodation options fill up quickly, well before the start of the semester.

On-Campus Residence Accommodations

On-Campus residence accommodations are provided per discretion by the school.

Off-Campus Accommodations

The local area has a variety of off-campus rental housing options including single homes, duplexes, apartments and rooms for rent. Students wishing to live off-campus need to research availability on their own and should arrive well before the start of term to do so.

Homestay

There are a wide variety of homestay options available, and our partners do their best to match students and hosts according to their interests and preferences. Homestay hosts include single people, young couples with children and pets, and older couples. All homestay accommodations have been inspected, and all adults in the home have completed a required criminal reference check.
