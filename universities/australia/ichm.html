Overview

About ICHM

ICHM, established in 1992, is a new breed business school, here to school new breed thinkers. ICHM champions ‘learning forward’- focusing on the learning of tomorrow as industry ready grads. Giving young talent the skills, networks and hands on experience to hit the ground running.

ICHM has 30+ years success as a private higher education provider, with its renowned hospitality in business school. They have also broadened their focus with a Bachelor of Business, Bachelor of Business (Marketing) and Entrepreneurship), as well as the established Hospitality Bachelors.

Why ICHM

  • Facilities: ICHM's facilities are tailored to your education and include a wine education space, restaurant service training facilities, a practice bar, and computer suite, digital library and resources.
  • Innovative Learning: With over 30 years in the business, ICHM knows that business evolves, and so does the way we learn. ICHM offers innovative study options that fit around students' lives.
  • Work Integrated Learning (WIL): ICHM prides itself on the quality and number of WIL placements available to its students. WIL form an integral part of all the ICHM degree programs and is designed to ensure you leave your degree industry ready and able to hit the ground running.

Bachelor's · Hospitality & Tourism

Bachelor of Business (Hospitality Management)

3 years Next intake: Mar 2027 Apply by 1 Mar 2027

The ICHM Bachelor of Business (Hospitality Management) prepares students for an international career in a wide variety of hospitality businesses. Students will progress from an operational base through to a management perspective.

Each student's knowledge is expanded each year with new industry relevant concepts and business applications. ICHM’s academic year consists of six months full time study, followed by a six month Work Integrated Learning (WIL) placement throughout Australia, or possibly overseas.

ICHM graduates gain a comprehensive understanding of the international hospitality environment, and as a result, have outstanding career prospects with many obtaining highly sought after positions around the world.

Master's · Hospitality & Tourism

Graduate Certificate of International Hotel Management Transfer to Master of International Hotel Management

2 years Next intake: Mar 2027 Apply by 1 Mar 2027

Students who are interested in taking the Master of International Hotel Management (MIHM), but don't quite have the credentials for direct entry can start in the Graduate Certificate portion. This means taking some English classes alongside the Masters programs. The best part is, students will still finish with their Master of International Hotel Management within 2 years, just like their direct-entry counterparts!

The Master of International Hotel Management is a two year (four semester) full-time course, designed to provide participants with the opportunity to develop advanced knowledge and cognitive skills to understand and manage international hotel operations at a senior level.

Whilst the course focus is on international hotels, participants could choose to undertake their career paths in other areas of the international tourism and hospitality industry. The course comprises ten core subjects and three electives delivered over eighteen months, plus a final Professional Practice and Research semester of six months in industry, as a capstone subject to complete the course.

Graduates from hotel schools/universities/colleges, holding business or hospitality related degrees, may receive Recognition of Prior Learning (RPL).

Student life & support

Work while studying

International students can work part-time alongside their studies (subject to visa conditions).

Co-op & placements

Offers co-op or work-placement options in selected programs.

On-campus housing

Students typically arrange off-campus or homestay accommodation.

Accommodation options

Students need to plan their accommodations well in advance of their arrival as all accommodation options fill up quickly, well before the start of the semester.

Near-Campus Residence Accommodations

ICHM connects interested students to Scape Waymouth if they are looking for accommodations. Just a short 5 minute walk away from the ICHM campus, Scape Waymouth is an easy option students will love. They can choose from studio or shared apartments that include an ultra-comfy double bed, large study desk, super-fast Wi-Fi, a private or shared bathrooms, a kitchenette and plenty of storage space throughout.

Off-Campus Accommodations

The local area has a variety of off-campus rental housing options including single homes, duplexes, apartments and rooms for rent. Students wishing to live off-campus need to research availability on their own and should arrive well before the start of term to do so.

Homestay

There are a wide variety of homestay options available, and our partners do their best to match students and hosts according to their interests and preferences. Homestay hosts include single people, young couples with children and pets, and older couples. All homestay accommodations have been inspected, and all adults in the home have completed a required criminal reference check.
