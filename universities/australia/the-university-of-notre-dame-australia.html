About The University of Notre Dame Australia (UNDA)

The University of Notre Dame Australia is a multi-campus university, with international campus in the historic City of Fremantle, Perth.

Notre Dame has a strong commitment to honouring the individual, and recognises that each student is graced with their own gifts and talents. Notre Dame believes that higher education should prepare students for a rich, fulfilling and reflective life, not just a career path.

Notre Dame has been rated 5 stars in the Good Universities Guide 2024, undergraduate and postgraduate categories, for Learner Engagement, Skills Development, Graduate Salary and Full-Time Employment.

Why The University of Notre Dame Australia (OIEG) - Perth?

  • Campus: The University’s Broadway Campus in perth provides a welcoming social hub in the heart of a bustling city, featuring heritage buildings and leafy courtyards. The University’s Darlinghurst Campus in perth is based in one of the most exciting and prestigious medical and research precincts in Australia.
  • History: The University of Notre Dame Australia (UNDA) in Perth, Western Australia was established in 1989 with support from the University of Notre Dame in the United States. The university's Perth campus is located in Fremantle, a historic city with well-preserved colonial streets.
  • Facility: The University of Notre Dame Australia (UNDA) has a facility in Fremantle, Perth, Australia. The facility includes the Prindiville Hall, a library, and teaching spaces.

Bachelor's · Business & Management

Bachelor of Accounting

3 years Next intake: Feb 2027 Apply by 1 Feb 2027

Bachelor of Accounting at The University of Notre Dame Australia, student will receive a thorough grounding in all business areas. Embracing the theoretical and practical skills needed to ensure their success, students program will cover everything from business law and IT to corporate accounting and auditing.

As part of their degree, they must undertake 150 hours of practical experience in a workplace of student's choice. This is not work experience but an embedded internship – indeed, many of their students later find full-time employment with the company where they took their practicum. According to the current QILT (Quality Indicators for Learning and Teaching) survey of Australian academic institutions, 76.7 per cent of their graduates found full-time employment after completing their business program at Notre Dame – more than 3 percent above the national average.

Bachelor of Arts

The Bachelor of Arts has a unique Major in Social Justice. The courses in this Major are open to students studying any discipline. The concept of social justice challenges students to step out of traditional mindsets and become involved in making a difference in their chosen career and school's world. Student life & support

Work while studying

International students can work part-time alongside their studies (subject to visa conditions).

Co-op & placements

Offers co-op or work-placement options in selected programs.

On-campus housing

Students typically arrange off-campus or homestay accommodation.

Accommodation options

Students need to plan their accommodations well in advance of their arrival as all accommodation options fill up quickly, well before the start of the semester.

On-Campus Residence Accommodations

On-Campus residence accommodations are provided per discretion by the school.

Off-Campus Accommodations

The local area has a variety of off-campus rental housing options including single homes, duplexes, apartments and rooms for rent. Students wishing to live off-campus need to research availability on their own and should arrive well before the start of term to do so.
