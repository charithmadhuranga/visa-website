Overview

About Murdoch College

Murdoch College offers various English courses and pathways into Murdoch University undergraduate and postgraduate programs, designed to enhance students' English and academic skills for a smooth transition to the university.

On-campus study integrates students into the University community from day one, providing access to world-class facilities and services. They can interact with current students to better prepare for their degree transition.

Why Murdoch College

  • University Syllabus: Murdoch College collaborates with Murdoch University staff to develop courses aligned with the university syllabus and teaching style, ensuring academic success.
  • Individual Attention: Experienced academics focus on each student's growth, benefiting from small class sizes for personalized support.
  • Personal Support: The Murdoch College Student Support team provides confidential assistance for any challenges affecting students' studies.
  • Packaged Enrolments: International students can apply to both Murdoch College and Murdoch University simultaneously, ensuring direct entry into the university upon successful graduation from the college (subject to progression requirements).

Student life & support

Work while studying

International students can work part-time alongside their studies (subject to visa conditions).

Co-op & placements

Offers co-op or work-placement options in selected programs.

On-campus housing

Students typically arrange off-campus or homestay accommodation.

Accommodation options

Students need to plan their accommodations well in advance of their arrival as all accommodation options fill up quickly, well before the start of the semester.

On-Campus Residence Accommodations

On-Campus residence accommodations are provided per discretion by the school.

Off-Campus Accommodations

The local area has a variety of off-campus rental housing options including single homes, duplexes, apartments and rooms for rent. Students wishing to live off-campus need to research availability on their own and should arrive well before the start of term to do so.

Homestay

There are a wide variety of homestay options available, and our partners do their best to match students and hosts according to their interests and preferences. Homestay hosts include single people, young couples with children and pets, and older couples. All homestay accommodations have been inspected, and all adults in the home have completed a required criminal reference check.

Note: For more information, please visit the Murdoch College's Accommodation page here.
