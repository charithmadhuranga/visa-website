Overview

About La Trobe University Sydney Campus (LTUSC)

Established in 1967, La Trobe University has been one of Australia's pioneering universities for more than 50 years. The $10 million, state-of-the-art Sydney Campus is designed with students in mind. Located in the centre of the city with views over Hyde Park, La Trobe University Sydney Campus is close to work and internship opportunities and just minutes’ walk to major transport hubs, vibrant café districts, shopping centres and Sydney’s world-renowned icons.

Why La Trobe University Sydney Campus (LTUSC)

  • Top Ranked Student City: Considered one of the best cities in the world to live and study, Sydney is a city of natural beauty which also boasts a strong economy, every type of cuisine one could desire, and a busy calendar of music and cultural festivals
  • Facilities: Technology enhanced teaching spaces, dynamic classrooms that seamlessly adapt to different learning styles, quiet study hubs and collaboration zones designed to encourage creativity and learning, computer labs with the latest technology and software
  • Library: Top floor library with stunning views across Sydney, Australia's largest city, known for icons like the Harbour Bridge and the Sydney Opera House.

Bachelor's · Business & Management

Bachelor of Business (BB)

3 years Next intake: Mar 2027 Apply by 1 Mar 2027

The modern business world is changing at breakneck speed. Ongoing digital and operational disruption present unique challenges – as well as opportunities. Society needs new leaders who are agile, entrepreneurial and, above all, able to innovate under the toughest of conditions.

Students will develop solid foundations in modern business with La Trobe's Bachelor of Business. They will prepare to thrive in a digitally disrupted world with this highly flexible degree and start building a dream career in business.

Students will explore multiple business disciplines in the first year and develop foundational knowledge and enterprise skills in areas such as management, organisational structures, data analytics and ethical business practices.

Students will choose from a wide range of majors, minors and optional second majors. They can also pair their major with complementary study from another discipline.

No matter how students specialise, they'll get hands-on experience and learn the latest digital skills while developing essential leadership, communication and critical-thinking skills.

Students will develop professional business skills as they work in diverse teams, present business strategies and use the software that's essential in modern business.

They'll graduate with the perfect blend of business fundamentals and technical expertise so they can change the business world.

Students will learn:

  • Digital literacy: Use a wide range of digital and data-processing tools to analyse business activity and become a more effective business professional.
  • Management and business structures: Understand how to communicate, collaborate and connect with business peers and stakeholders.
  • Economics: Discover how business and society connect. Understand economic principles and use them to create business solutions.
  • Sustainability: Learn about environmental, corporate and governance responsibility. Develop sustainable solutions that prioritise individual and societal wellbeing.

Bachelor's · Computing & IT

Bachelor of Cybersecurity

3 years Next intake: Mar 2027 Apply by 1 Mar 2027

La Trobe's Bachelor of Cybersecurity offers students the opportunity to step into the rapidly evolving digital world of cybersecurity.

Students will build a highly specialised technical skillset as they learn about the history of hacking, build their coding expertise, and access industry-based projects and mentoring opportunities with major corporations.

Students will also have the opportunity to complement their industry-focused learning with a choice of advanced electives in IT, business, law, policy and strategic communication.

Students will learn

  • Cybersecurity fundamentals
  • Learn the basic principles and best practices of cybersecurity. Understand how it mitigates cyber vulnerabilities and protects confidential information.
  • Ethical hacking and defence
  • Gain evaluation techniques to assess the security of network configuration and apply these skills to defend against network-based threats.
  • Programming and cyber algorithms
  • Explore the widely used Python programming language and learn how algorithms are used to solve cybersecurity challenges.
  • Network engineering
  • Study the architecture of computer networks and the internet, and examine protocols and services used in the defence of online information.
  • Cyber governance and risk management
  • Learn the frameworks for cybersecurity governance and risk mitigation based on your emerging understanding of business strategy and risk appetite.

Bachelor of Information Technology (BIT)

3 years Next intake: Mar 2027 Apply by 1 Mar 2027

The world is drawing closer together. As the speed and quality of digital technologies continues to evolve, the world needs more passionate professionals who can create meaningful solutions and connections.

Designed to help build a digital future, La Trobe's Bachelor of Information Technology equips students with skills across programming, networking, databases, systems analysis and design, and project management.

Student life & support

Work while studying

International students can work part-time alongside their studies (subject to visa conditions).

Co-op & placements

Offers co-op or work-placement options in selected programs.

On-campus housing

Students typically arrange off-campus or homestay accommodation.

Accommodation options

Students need to plan their accommodations well in advance of their arrival as all accommodation options fill up quickly, well before the start of the semester.

On-Campus Residence Accommodations

On-Campus residence accommodations are provided per discretion by the school.

Off-Campus Accommodations

The local area has a variety of off-campus rental housing options including single homes, duplexes, apartments and rooms for rent. Students wishing to live off-campus need to research availability on their own and should arrive well before the start of term to do so.

Homestay

There are a wide variety of homestay options available, and our partners do their best to match students and hosts according to their interests and preferences. Homestay hosts include single people, young couples with children and pets, and older couples. All homestay accommodations have been inspected, and all adults in the home have completed a required criminal reference check.
