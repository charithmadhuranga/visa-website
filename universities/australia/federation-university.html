About Federation University

Federation University is one of Australia’s oldest universities. The University is known today for its modern approach to teaching and learning. For 150 years, Federation University has been reaching out to new communities, steadily building a generation of independent thinkers united in the knowledge that they are greater together.

Ballarat is the historic home of Federation University Australia. Today, Federation University is a central focus of the city, with major campuses and strong connections to local industry, culture and community. The flagship Ballarat campus is set on 110 hectares of natural bushland, Mt Helen combines a beautiful location with cutting-edge teaching and research facilities.

Why Federation University - Mount Helen (Ballarat)

  • Campus Life: Students will have every opportunity to get involved in Federation life through clubs, societies, sports teams, recreational trips and activities. They will have the chance to meet new people, get inspired and build lifelong connections.
  • Facilities: The Mount Helen Campus boasts a state-of-the-art science and engineering building, immersive nursing labs, a new health and sports precinct, lecture theatres, 24-hour access computer labs, a library, spiritual facilities, student lounges, a café, eating areas and a children’s centre. With beautiful walking trails around the grounds, students should keep an eye out for koalas and kangaroos!
  • Continued Support: Even after students graduate, they are still part of the Federation community. With strong relationships with industry partners, students' degrees will provide opportunities that are set to grow.
  • Life-Changing Research: Federation University research is ranked well above world standard. Federation collaborates with communities and industries across Australia and overseas, and this work has a measurable impact on society.

The Bachelor of Business is a contemporary degree developed in collaboration with industry and designed to meet the needs of businesses now and in the future. Businesses are facing an unprecedented number of challenges and opportunities stemming from digital disruption and the rapid adoption of new technologies. There has never been a more exciting time to start a career in business, whether students are looking for a career in the corporate world or aspiring to be an entrepreneur.

The Bachelor of Science (Environmental Science) is an undergraduate program that explores the scientific principles underlying environmental systems and sustainability. It prepares students to understand, assess, and address environmental challenges such as climate change, pollution, biodiversity loss, and resource management.

Students study core areas including ecology, environmental chemistry, geology, hydrology, climate science, and environmental policy, gaining both theoretical knowledge and practical skills. The curriculum emphasizes fieldwork, laboratory analysis, data interpretation, and environmental monitoring, enabling students to evaluate environmental impacts and develop sustainable solutions.

The program integrates interdisciplinary approaches, combining natural sciences with policy and management perspectives to address complex environmental issues at local, national, and global levels.

Graduates are prepared for careers in environmental consulting, conservation, government agencies, sustainability management, and resource planning, or for further study in environmental science, engineering, or policy.

Master's · Social Sciences & Psychology

Master of Social Work – Qualifying

2 years Next intake: Mar 2027 Apply by 30 Dec 2026

Program Description

Be an advocate for social justice and human rights. Make a real difference to the lives of people in need. Excel in their career in one of the fastest growing professions. Students might be a new graduate looking for a clear career path or an experienced social worker wanting to take the next step in their professional life. Or perhaps students are ready for a career change and want to step into social work.

Whatever their background, faculty will prepare students to work across human and community services in government bodies, non-government organisations, community and health services and more. They'll become confident working with migrants, people with alcohol, drug and mental health problems and in palliative care, child protection, youth services and much more.

They'll be exposed to challenging real life situations through 140 days during your studies in social work settings, being mentored by experienced professionals in the field. Then, once they graduate, they can go on to be a qualified social worker, take up management positions or work in national or international policy development. This is an Australian Association of Social Workers (AASW) accredited qualification, and is an entry qualification into the social work profession. It has been deemed to meet the Australian Social Work Education and Accreditation Standards.
