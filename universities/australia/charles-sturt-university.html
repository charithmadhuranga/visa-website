About Charles Sturt University

Perfectly positioned in the vibrant hub of North Sydney, the new Charles Sturt University Sydney campus offers an exciting and rewarding student experience in the stunning harbour city that’s ranked #7 on the QS World University Ranking of student-friendly cities.

Everything Sydney’s famous for is within easy reach, from the Opera House to world-class beaches, shopping, dining and theatre. With the city’s community including people from over 180 nations, there’s a diverse and spectacular calendar of cultural events year-round. The campus has convenient access to transport, including the famous ferry network, and the CBD is just a short train or bus ride or stroll across the iconic Sydney Harbour Bridge.

Study business, accounting or IT with Australia's leading university for graduate employment, #1 for undergraduate starting salaries and top 10 for postgraduate starting salaries*. With the perfect blend of modern amenities and academic excellence, students will have all the expert support they need to succeed in their career, making lifelong connections and memories along the way.

Master's · Health & Nursing

Master of Nursing (Pre-registration)

2 years Next intake: Mar 2027 Apply by 15 Jan 2027

The Master of Nursing (Pre-registration) is designed for graduates from non-nursing disciplines who wish to enter the nursing profession. The programme combines nursing theory, clinical skills development, evidence-based practice, and supervised professional placements to prepare students to provide safe, person-centred care in diverse healthcare environments.

Areas of study may include anatomy and physiology, health assessment, clinical nursing practice, pharmacology and medication safety, acute and chronic care, mental health, community health, Indigenous health, research, healthcare leadership, and professional and ethical practice. Simulation activities and clinical placements allow students to apply theoretical knowledge while developing communication, critical-thinking, teamwork, and clinical decision-making skills.

Graduates may be eligible to apply for registration as a nurse in Australia, subject to the programme’s accreditation and meeting all requirements of the Nursing and Midwifery Board of Australia (NMBA) and the Australian Health Practitioner Regulation Agency (Ahpra). Clinical placements may require immunisation evidence, police and working-with-children checks, first-aid certification, and other compliance documents. Exact modules, placement arrangements, accreditation status, and registration outcomes should be confirmed with the university.
