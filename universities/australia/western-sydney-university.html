Overview

About Western Sydney University

Western Sydney University is an institution ranked amongst the top two per cent of universities in the world with a high standard for academic excellence that prepares students for success, giving a global perspective to their studies – and more opportunity for the future. Western Sydney University - Sydney City offers undergraduate and postgraduate areas of study. Western Sydney University - Sydney City is ranked in the Top 100 young universities in the world and delivers its programs in Sydney City, providing students the Western experience right in the heart of Australia's most iconic city. Western Sydney University - Sydney City offers a range of study options within the arts and humanities, social sciences, computing, and entrepreneurial business and management.

Why Western Sydney University (WSUSCC) - Sydney City

  • Location: The Sydney City campus brings the same Western Sydney University experience to the heart of Australia's most famous city. The one-of-a-kind location is opposite Sydney’s iconic Hyde Park, and means students have front-row access to Sydney’s best shops, restaurants and entertainment venues, with landmarks like the Sydney Harbour Bridge and Opera House just around the corner.
  • Facilities: The vibrant, inspiring community of students at the Sydney City campus have access to state-of-the-art facilities in a thriving, diverse and innovative learning environment – in the centre of the city. This includes: state-of-the-art computer laboratories, innovative learning space, high-speed Wi-Fi, multiple vibrant areas for social activities and a comprehensive online library.
  • Student Support: At Western Sydney University, the welfare of students is the number one priority. It happily provides multiple support services to ensure student's transition to university goes smoothly. This includes access to student learning advisors, student welfare and counselling services, and accessibility support.

Student life & support

Work while studying

International students can work part-time alongside their studies (subject to visa conditions).

Co-op & placements

Offers co-op or work-placement options in selected programs.

On-campus housing

Students typically arrange off-campus or homestay accommodation.

Accommodation options

Students need to plan their accommodations well in advance of their arrival as all accommodation options fill up quickly, well before the start of the semester.

On-Campus Residence Accommodations

On-Campus residence accommodations are provided per discretion by the school.

Off-Campus Accommodations

The local area has a variety of off-campus rental housing options including single homes, duplexes, apartments and rooms for rent. Students wishing to live off-campus need to research availability on their own and should arrive well before the start of term to do so.

Homestay

There are a wide variety of homestay options available, and our partners do their best to match students and hosts according to their interests and preferences. Homestay hosts include single people, young couples with children and pets, and older couples. All homestay accommodations have been inspected, and all adults in the home have completed a required criminal reference check.
