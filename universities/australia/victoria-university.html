About Victoria University (VU)

Victoria University - Sydney (VU) is ranked in the top 2% of universities worldwide, and is revolutionising education in Australia with a new way to do university. This includes the VU Block Model, where each unit is taught one at a time, rather than several at once. Courses are tailored to meet industry needs, and students undertake real-world projects, as well as meaningful and engaging internships and work placements. Graduates have the creative, problem-solving and technical skills required to thrive in a fast-changing world. Practical learning, designed with industry partners, is reinforced through industry application and supported by skills in communication, collaboration and leadership. VU’s teaching spaces are optimised for contemporary learning, and students will be supported by a team of experienced academics who are committed to ensuring students graduate with the skills and knowledge to succeed in and beyond their degree. Students from over 45 countries currently study at VU Sydney. Students studying at Victoria University will be part of a global community of scholars, and will forge lifelong relationships. By achieving high academic goals, students will be prepared for a successful career after they graduate

Why The Education Centre of Australia - Victoria University (VU) - Sydney

  • The VU Block Model: The award-winning VU Block Model has improved student results and the student experience. Students will study one subject over a four week 'block', or two subjects over eight weeks, depending on the course. This allows students to focus on one or two subjects and receive their results before moving onto the next block. International students at VU Sydney are performing particularly well under the Block Model, achieving average pass rates of 90%.
  • Location: A melting pot of cultures, cuisines and international events, Sydney prides itself on being a multicultural metropolis. Besides its culturally diverse reputation, Sydney is famed for its spectacular harbour and beaches; internationally renowned sights such as the Sydney Opera House and Sydney Harbour Bridge; world-class restaurants; and bustling nightlife. Students at VU Sydney will never be short of things to see and do between classes.
  • Safe and Supportive Community: Victoria University - Sydney is a welcoming and inclusive university in one of Australia’s highest-ranked student cities. Australia is also ranked the safest country in the world for women, according to the 2019 Global Wealth Migration Review. VU celebrates cultural diversity and provides opportunities for all students to take part in community and social activities. VU has a vibrant social calendar, programs to promote student leadership, and activities to support community engagement.

Master's · Computing & IT

Master of Artificial Intelligence

2 years Next intake: Feb 2027 Apply by 25 Dec 2026

The Master of Artificial Intelligence at Victoria University develops practical, future-focused expertise to address challenges across industry and society. The postgraduate programme combines strong technical foundations with hands-on development, preparing students to design, implement, and evaluate AI solutions responsibly.

Students build advanced knowledge in machine learning and deep learning, natural language processing, data mining, and intelligent decision-making. They also develop specialised skills in generative AI to create industry-ready products, workflows, and decision-support tools. The curriculum considers important deployment factors, including data quality, privacy, security, sustainability, and community consent.

The programme develops students’ ability to critically examine foundational and emerging developments in artificial intelligence, including neural networks and predictive analytics. They learn to design robust AI solutions and scalable data-driven applications, analyse structured and unstructured data, and generate defensible insights for professional and business decision-making. Students also evaluate human-centred AI applications in relation to user, stakeholder, and organisational needs, including their potential to enhance human capability, productivity, and employability.

Ethics and responsible practice are central to the programme. Students examine bias, privacy, security, transparency, explainability, sustainability, and the broader societal effects of AI design, development, and deployment. The curriculum explicitly addresses Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander data sovereignty and Indigenous data governance, considering how AI systems may affect communities, Country, cultural heritage, public services, health, education, environmental management, and decision-making. Students learn to assess AI systems and data practices through Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander data and perspectives.

Students also develop the ability to communicate AI concepts, methods, model outputs, and project outcomes to specialist and non-specialist audiences. They undertake a substantial research-based or industry-focused project, applying appropriate research methods, project management, and professional judgement to a complex real-world problem.

The programme is suitable for students seeking specialist AI qualifications, professionals and managers looking to develop their skills or move into AI-related roles, and graduates from computing and related disciplines. Applicants from other backgrounds may also be considered, with foundational study in programming and core IT concepts available to support those without a strong computing background. Applicants with prior computing or IT qualifications may be eligible for credit for selected foundational units, enabling them to progress more quickly to advanced AI study.

Flexible learning supports intensive, skills-based study and authentic assessment. Project-oriented learning uses contemporary tools and development practices to help students produce portfolio-ready work. Students can choose an industry project, a research pathway, or a combination of the two to align their studies with employment goals or further academic development. The programme prepares graduates to contribute professionally and ethically in local and global contexts.

Student life & support

Work while studying

International students can work part-time alongside their studies (subject to visa conditions).

Co-op & placements

Co-op options are limited — ask us about placement-friendly alternatives.

On-campus housing

Students typically arrange off-campus or homestay accommodation.

Accommodation options

Students need to plan their accommodations well in advance of their arrival as all accommodation options fill up quickly, well before the start of the semester.

On-Campus Residence Accommodations

On-Campus residence accommodations are provided per discretion by the school.

Off-Campus Accommodations

The local area has a variety of off-campus rental housing options including single homes, duplexes, apartments and rooms for rent. Students wishing to live off-campus need to research availability on their own and should arrive well before the start of term to do so.

Homestay

There are a wide variety of homestay options available, and our partners do their best to match students and hosts according to their interests and preferences. Homestay hosts include single people, young couples with children and pets, and older couples. All homestay accommodations have been inspected, and all adults in the home have completed a required criminal reference check.
