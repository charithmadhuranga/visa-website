Overview

About Danford Higher Education

Danford Higher Education offers courses in Digital Marketing, Accounting, Information Technology, and Business. All their courses are accredited to the Australian Qualifications Framework, and credits are recognised by other higher education institutions and universities that also follow the framework.‍

Danford Higher Education is also a member of educational institutions that have been operating for 17 years in Melbourne. In this time, more than 6000 students have studied with them.

Students will study at DHE's beautiful and modern Melbourne city campus, which can be easily accessed via public transport. There is also a lot of parking near the CBD that is within walking distance.

Why Danford Higher Education

  • Small Classes: This allows Danford Higher Education's professors and lecturers to give students personalised attention and help with their learning.
  • Contact Hours: Classes are 2 hours of lectures and 1 hour of tutorials + unsupervised work. Lecturers are also available for consultations when students have questions.
  • Study Assistance: DHE offers a variety of support to help keep students' studies on track. This includes workshops, student learning advisors, mentors, and support services.
  • Focus on workspace skills: What sets Danford Higher Education apart is its focus on getting students workplace-ready. Professors and lecturers have professional experience and practice in their field.
  • Modern and Accessible Facilities: DHE's campus facilities are newly renovated and located centrally in Melbourne and are close to major metro stations.

Bachelor's · Business & Management

Bachelor of Business – Accounting

3 years Next intake: Jan 2027 Apply by 15 Dec 2026

The Bachelor of Business (Accounting) is a three-year full-time program that meets the requirements of AQF level 7. This course is specifically designed to provide students with a comprehensive understanding of business and accounting concepts, as well as the necessary skills and practical application required to succeed as professional practitioners in various business work environments. The program aims to equip students with the knowledge and expertise needed to effectively operate and excel in their roles, ensuring they are well-prepared for a successful career in the field of accounting.

This course is accredited by Certified Practising Accountants (CPA) Australia and by Chartered Accountants Australia and New Zealand (CAANZ). Completing an accredited accounting degree with DHE, including specified accredited units of study, can provide a pathway to a career in professional accounting, and associate membership in CPA Australia or CAANZ.
