Overview

About The University of Adelaide College

The University of Adelaide is the Preferred Pathway Provider, ensuring students are well-prepared for university. The College partners with the University to offer optimal learning opportunities, with programs designed to meet the University’s standards.

The syllabus is regularly updated with the latest university content, providing a university-level education. With world-class teachers, small class sizes, and supportive environments, students receive high-quality learning. The College also offers personal support to help students excel academically and socially, preparing them for university and future careers.

Why The University of Adelaide College - Adelaide

  • Campus Life and Surrounding: Student life isn’t just about study. In Adelaide, students have got it all – from the Australian beach lifestyle to cosmopolitan city culture. Living in the South Australian capital is safe, affordable, and most importantly a lot of fun.
  • Facilities: Due to the College's special relationship with the University of Adelaide, students will be part of the University and have access to all the state-of-the-art facilities they have on offer, including science laboratories, the Sports Hub Fitness Centre, computer and learning facilities at Hub Central, and an extensive library of resources.
  • Flexible Options: With three intakes per year, students are sure to find a program option that works for them. Plus, with a wide variety of subjects and courses on offer, students have the chance to learn content relevant to their degree before they are even enrolled.
  • Additional English Language Support Services: The College has students from all over the world. To make sure that everyone has the best chance to reach their potential, a range of different language support services are available to assist students with their studies across subjects.

Bachelor's · Health & Nursing

Degree Transfer – Extended + Bachelor of Biomedical and Health Sciences – Clinical Trials

40 months Next intake: Mar 2027 Apply by 1 Mar 2027

The Degree Transfer Program includes a bridging semester followed by first-year courses from the University of Adelaide. Degree Transfer students study the same courses as first-year students but in a more intensive format with features such as:

  • Seamless transition and guaranteed entry to year 2 upon graduation.
  • A wide variety of courses identical to those offered by the University of Adelaide, sometimes taught by their staff.
  • Small class sizes with individual guidance and support.
  • Lower fees than first-year university fees and flexible English language requirements.
  • No need to provide IELTS evidence for those who complete the program successfully.
  • Opportunity to study alongside and take exams with university students on campus.
  • A pathway to programs like engineering and science, often hard to access at Group of Eight universities.
  • After semester two, students progress into the second year of the bachelor degree with local students.

Students passing the Degree Transfer Program on the first attempt will meet the English language entry requirement without needing to submit an approved English test.

Master's · Business & Management

Pre-Master's Pathway – 1 Semester – Master of Accounting

23 months Next intake: Mar 2027 Apply by 1 Mar 2027

The College is the first educational institution in Australia to offer a postgraduate bridging course to a Group of Eight University. Upon successful completion of the Pre-Master’s Program (PMP), students are guaranteed entry into the postgraduate degree of their choice at the University of Adelaide.

Reasons to consider the Pre-Master’s Program include:

  • Flexible entry and English language requirements, allowing students access to an exclusive Group of Eight university.
  • The Bridging to Pre-Master’s semester model enables students to commence their master’s subjects after just one semester, subsequently progressing directly into the second semester.
  • Students benefit from additional support in developing their English language proficiency and academic study skills.

Student life & support

Work while studying

International students can work part-time alongside their studies (subject to visa conditions).

Co-op & placements

Co-op options are limited — ask us about placement-friendly alternatives.

On-campus housing

Students typically arrange off-campus or homestay accommodation.

Accommodation options

Students need to plan their accommodations well in advance of their arrival as all accommodation options fill up quickly, well before the start of the semester.

On-Campus Residence Accommodations

On-Campus residence accommodations are provided per discretion by the school.

Off-Campus Accommodations

The local area has a variety of off-campus rental housing options including single homes, duplexes, apartments and rooms for rent. Students wishing to live off-campus need to research availability on their own and should arrive well before the start of term to do so.

Homestay

There are a wide variety of homestay options available, and our partners do their best to match students and hosts according to their interests and preferences. Homestay hosts include single people, young couples with children and pets, and older couples. All homestay accommodations have been inspected, and all adults in the home have completed a required criminal reference check.
