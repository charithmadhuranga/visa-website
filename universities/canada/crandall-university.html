About Crandall University

Crandall University is Atlantic Canada’s leading Liberal Arts University rooted in the Christian faith, offering undergraduate and graduate studies. Founded in 1949, Crandall is a private university in Moncton, New Brunswick, Canada. Crandall is home to over 1800 students, including students from over seventy different countries. Situated on a picturesque, 200-acre site in beautiful Moncton, New Brunswick, Crandall’s campus offers a welcoming atmosphere, a commitment to students, academic excellence and a commitment to the Christian faith.

Why Crandall University

  • Small Classes: Small classes allow you to get to know your professors and classmates.
  • Faculty: Recognized and reputable professors with strong academic backgrounds.
  • Culture: A safe, welcoming, and diverse environment focused on strong academic achievements, with opportunities for practical experience.
  • Athletics: Championship athletics: basketball, boxing, cross-country, soccer and volleyball.
  • Tradition: A university rooted in the Christian faith where students from all backgrounds are welcome.

Master's · Business & Management

Master of Management

2 years Next intake: May 2027 Apply by 15 Dec 2026

The Master of Management program at Crandall University prepares students for leadership roles in today’s dynamic global business environment. Designed for professionals from diverse backgrounds, the program emphasizes advanced management skills, ethical decision-making, and strategic thinking.

With a robust curriculum covering organizational behavior, financial decision-making, marketing, and more, students gain practical knowledge to excel in various industries.

Final Semester Options

Students customize their learning experience by choosing one of the following pathways:

  • Internship: A 15-week professional placement to apply knowledge and gain industry experience.
  • Human Resource Concentration: Specialized courses accredited by the Chartered Professionals in Human Resources New Brunswick.
  • Elective Courses: Additional courses to expand expertise and complete the program.
  • Applied Research Project: An individual research project guided by an academic supervisor.

Graduates are equipped for leadership in business, government, and non-profit sectors, both in Canada and globally.

Master's · Business & Management

Master of Organizational Management

1 year Next intake: May 2027 Apply by 15 Dec 2026

The Master of Organizational Management (MOrgM) ­– International pathway is a 12-month program offered to international students as an alternative to the MBA. It focuses more on working with, and through, people than on the technical aspects of business administration. The program is currently offered on a condensed one course per month basis to allow students a singular subject focus.

The value of obtaining the Master of Organizational Management is that all organizations require effective management. The program uses an interdisciplinary approach and draws on the academic strength of faculty from a variety of disciplines who are excellent educators and typically build strong relationships with students.
