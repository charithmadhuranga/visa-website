About University of New Brunswick

The University of New Brunswick knows that its students want more than a traditional university degree. They want an education that prepares them for a global future – a future that demands critical thinking, innovation and resourcefulness. The University believes that every future matters. This belief fuels the experience offered to its students. While it is Canada’s oldest English university – founded in 1785, the school is older than the country itself – they are most excited about what lies ahead. The University believes in innovation as a pathway to success. Innovation propels societies forward, improves lives, spurs discovery and advances higher knowledge. The University of New Brunswick is big enough to offer a global education and to embrace world-class research. Yet, the campuses are small enough to offer an education that is trusted and personal, where professors know students' name and their potential.

Why the University of New Brunswick

  • Location: Saint John and Fredricton are vibrant industry-based cities with a dynamic business community and an increasingly multicultural mix of people. The cities' uptown centre are filled with shopping centres, sporting events, seasonal festivals and restaurants.
  • Extracurricular Activities: There is no shortage of clubs and societies to choose from at the University, including the African Caribbean Society, the Women in Business society the Mathematical Society, and more.
  • Facilities: The Fredricton URec Centre is an excellent place to stay active while studying. Services include group fitness classes, aquatics classes, personal training, and so much more.

Bachelor of Arts – Biology (Optional Co-op)

4 years Next intake: Jan 2027 Apply by 1 Jan 2027

Program Description

Biology is the study of life. At UNB Saint John, Arts students can major in Biology, studying botany, ecology, zoology, organic chemistry and microbiology. Students will be able to explore the world of environmental science, sustainable development and public policies towards land and water use.

In a student's first year they will take introductory studies in organic chemistry, ecology cells and organisms and biology lab work. In second year they will add botany, microbiology, zoology and statistics to their course work and at the end of the year they may apply to major in Biology. Upper-level studies in Biology may include conservation biology, environmental law and animal behaviour.

A student's biology degree will teach them to analyze how living things work and interact with other organisms. students will learn to think critically and solve problems through observation and evaluation.

Master's · Social Sciences & Psychology

Master of Applied Psychology Research and Evaluation (MAPRE)

1 year Next intake: Sep 2027 Apply by 1 Sep 2027

Governments, non-profit organizations, and businesses invest significant time, money, and materials to implement policies and introduce programs that achieve their long-term strategic goals and address the needs of individuals.

For individuals interested in developing foundational research skills to address real-world questions across various applied settings, this program is suitable. In the MAPRE course and practicum-focused program, participants will have the opportunity to supplement their coursework with an extended practicum in a community setting.

A program accessible to all students

Students are not required to have an undergraduate degree in psychology. The MAPRE course and practicum-based program accommodates individuals from diverse academic backgrounds, equipping them with the skills necessary to conduct applied research in a variety of settings. In the program, recent university graduates and current professionals will collaborate and learn how to approach program evaluation and research through a team-based approach. Students can also select elective courses aligned with their personal interests and needs.

An emphasis on experiential learning

The program’s core focus is on experiential, hands-on learning. Throughout this one-year program, participants will have opportunities to integrate theoretical knowledge with practical experiences. Additionally, they will learn how to design program evaluations and gain knowledge about statistics and psychological testing to support their evaluations.

In the fall, students will choose where to complete their summer practicum. In the winter, they will work with the program coordinator and their community partner to develop a program evaluation proposal. After completing their coursework, students will then have the opportunity to work at their partner organization to conduct their program evaluation.

Unique to the Maritimes

The MAPRE program is the first of its kind focused on evaluation in the Maritimes. No other universities in the region offer graduate degrees in program evaluation, meaning residents of New Brunswick, Prince Edward Island, and Nova Scotia often have to travel to other provinces for education in this field. The development of this program was driven by a clear regional need.

Community partners have reported to the MAPRE committee that they frequently send their employees out of province for evaluation training. This degree allows students to stay in the Maritimes while pursuing their graduate studies.

Master's · Education & Teaching

Master of Education – Councelling

16 months – 2 years Next intake: Sep 2027 Apply by 1 Dec 2026

This degree is tailored for future master’s-level practitioners, such as school counselors or counseling therapists working in community agencies or private practice. The MEd in Counselling also provides the option of a combined research and practice-based degree, which can serve as a foundation for pursuing doctoral studies in counseling psychology.

It is important to note that the MEd in Counselling is not designed to qualify individuals for licensure as psychologists.

Counselors work with individuals, groups, and families to address a wide range of issues related to daily life and development, including:

  • Career choice and adjustment
  • Successfully making life transitions
  • Functioning in interpersonal relationships
  • Emotional and behavioral problems

Faculty members conduct research on topics such as:

  • Career and vocational development
  • Counseling processes
  • Therapeutic relationships
  • Growth psychology
  • The practice of counseling and counseling psychology in Canada

The program offers two routes for students:

  • MEd course route: 10 courses (33 credit hours), including a six-credit-hour practicum
  • MEd thesis route: 10 courses plus a thesis (48 credit hours)

Master of Engineering

1 year – 2 years Next intake: Sep 2027 Apply by 1 Mar 2027

The MEng degree is designed for students who wish to study one or more fields of geomatics engineering at an advanced level, gaining exposure to new geospatial technologies and a variety of application domains such as Remote Sensing and Computer Vision, Spatial Data Sciences, Geodesy and Positioning, and Hydrospatial Engineering. The course-based MEng program consists of 30 credit hours of coursework, selected in consultation with the Director of Graduate Studies.

The degree is intended to be completed in three or four academic terms, depending on the student’s background. Co-op work terms are also encouraged.

The Engineering Co-op Program allows students to develop as professionals and build critical transferrable skills, providing a competitive edge in the job market. Participation in the co-op program enables students to apply the theory and knowledge gained in coursework to a professional work environment. Students are advised to consult with their department and the co-op office to determine the length of co-op they qualify for under current regulations; however, all relevant work experience is considered beneficial and can support employment prospects after graduation.

Master of Business Administration (Immersive MBA – 16 months)

16 months Next intake: Sep 2027 Apply by 1 Sep 2027

The University of New Brunswick Saint John’s Accelerated Master of Business Administration (MBA) program pairs a world-class education with a personal, welcoming experience.

  • Immersive MBA (iMBA): In addition to completing the aMBA’s five modules, students selecting the iMBA, complete an additional four-month immersive experience designed to strengthen applied learning and market readiness. Students choose from one of three options, including a paid-internship, a faculty-supervised research project, or the development of a new entrepreneurial business venture.

This pathway provide a rigorous, applied and AACSB-accredited graduate business education.

Through advanced coursework, case analysis, simulations, applied business consulting practicums, and professional development, students gain the knowledge and competencies required to address complex organizational challenges and exercise effective leadership.

Students benefit from small class sizes, a diverse international cohort, accessible faculty, dedicated program staff and strong connections with the business community.

The curriculum emphasizes evidence-based decision-making, critical thinking, persuasive communication, ethical leadership and the integration of knowledge across business disciplines.

Student life & support

Work while studying

International students can work part-time alongside their studies (subject to visa conditions).

Co-op & placements

Offers co-op or work-placement options in selected programs.

On-campus housing

On-campus residence is available.

Accommodation options

Students need to plan their accommodations well in advance of their arrival as all accommodation options fill up quickly, well before the start of the semester.

On-Campus Residence Accommodations

On-Campus residence accommodations are provided per discretion by the school.

Off-Campus Accommodations

The local area has a variety of off-campus rental housing options including single homes, duplexes, apartments and rooms for rent. Students wishing to live off-campus need to research availability on their own and should arrive well before the start of term to do so.

Homestay

There are a wide variety of homestay options available, and our partners do their best to match students and hosts according to their interests and preferences. Homestay hosts include single people, young couples with children and pets, and older couples. All homestay accommodations have been inspected, and all adults in the home have completed a required criminal reference check.
