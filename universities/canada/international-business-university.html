Overview

About International Business University (IBU)

Located in Toronto, Canada, the International Business University was founded in 2020 with the vision to establish an independent teaching oriented and student centred university in Canada. The school is a student-focused not-for-profit university that offers a growing set of undergraduate and graduate academic and professional degree programs that are career-relevant, can be completed in an accelerated time frame. Students will graduate with knowledge, technical and interpersonal skills, and problem-solving experience. They will be prepared for roles in business and the private sector, community and not-for-profit organizations, and public service.

At International Business University, faculty devote themselves to educating and opening doors for students. By crafting learning experiences both in and out of the classroom, IBU creates an environment that encourages an exchange of diverse perspectives, provides a wide range of collaborative opportunities and grounds students learning in a structure that ensures they meet their fullest potential. Currently, students can expect to receive a letter of acceptance in under 10 days.

Why International Business University?

  • Personal Education Experience: As Ontario's first independent, not-for-profit university, IBU focuses on each and every student, by having a teaching oriented, student focused education. Class size is 30 students, with a personal academic advisor for each student. One-on-one mentoring and tutoring by professors is also available.
  • Campus: IBU is located in Canada's business capital of Toronto. The city's downtown core gives students instant access and networking opportunities as well as enriching cultural experiences and activities.
  • Degree Length: Students can complete a 4 year bachelor's degree in just under 3 years and a 2 year Masters of Business Administration in 16 months.

Bachelor of Commerce (Honours) – International Management and Technology

32 months Next intake: May 2027 Apply by 28 Feb 2027

International Business University's Bachelor of Commerce (Honours) in International Management and Technology degree responds to the needs of Canadian and international employers for graduates who combine business knowledge, soft skills and technical skills and who are able to transition seamlessly into the workplace

Equipped with business knowledge together with the most relevant technologies, students will develop the knowledge, skills, attitudes and behaviours that employers are looking for today. IBU's BComm programs have been accredited by National Committee for Management Association (NCMA) in Canada. The Master of Science in Applied AI – Data Engineering Specialization is a postgraduate program designed to provide students with advanced expertise in artificial intelligence, with a strong emphasis on data engineering and scalable data systems. The program prepares graduates to design, develop, and manage data infrastructures that power modern AI-driven applications across a wide range of industries.

Students gain in-depth knowledge in areas such as big data technologies, data pipelines, cloud computing, database systems, and machine learning. The curriculum focuses on integrating AI models with efficient data processing frameworks, enabling students to work with large, complex datasets and deliver data-driven solutions.

The program combines theoretical foundations with practical, hands-on experience through projects and applied learning, allowing students to build and optimize data architectures and implement AI solutions in real-world contexts. It also addresses important considerations such as data governance, ethics, and security in AI systems.

The course culminates in a research project or thesis, where students can specialize in a chosen area within applied AI and data engineering. Graduates are well-prepared for roles such as data engineer, machine learning engineer, AI specialist, and data architect, as well as for further academic research or doctoral study.

Student life & support

Work while studying

International students can work part-time alongside their studies (subject to visa conditions).

Co-op & placements

Co-op options are limited — ask us about placement-friendly alternatives.

On-campus housing

Students typically arrange off-campus or homestay accommodation.

Accommodation options

Students need to plan their accommodations well in advance of their arrival as all accommodation options fill up quickly, well before the start of the semester.

Off-Campus Accommodations

The local area has a variety of off-campus rental housing options including single homes, duplexes, apartments and rooms for rent. Students wishing to live off-campus need to research availability on their own and should arrive well before the start of term to do so.

Students can reach out to Student Support Services for help and assistance for accommodation.
