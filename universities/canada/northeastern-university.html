About Northeastern University

Founded in 1898, Northeastern University is renowned for its experiential learning model, high-impact research, deep partnerships, and worldwide reach. From day one, Northeastern has pursued innovative ways of teaching and research that place a premium on experience and engagement with the world. Today, the University's signature approach erases traditional boundaries, empowering not only students, but faculty, alumni, partners, and innovators to solve problems and pursue impact.

Northeastern University's founding campus, Boston, and its dynamic neighborhoods and industries serve to enrich the student experience Northeastern University students choose from a wide range of majors, combined majors, and undergraduate, graduate, and PhD degrees. Students will collaborate with world-class universities, institutes, hospitals, and tech and life sciences sectors on research for global impact. And discover all the ways to create positive change through an extensive network of community relationships.

Why Northeastern University - Boston

  • Location: Northeastern’s vibrant campus is a one-of-a-kind sanctuary of lush green spaces and tree-lined pathways located right in the heart of Boston. It truly is a campus within a city. Boston is one of the country’s greatest walking cities, Students can take a short walk or hop on the easy-to-use public transportation system (the “T”) to explore popular nearby destinations.
  • Campus and city life: Students can join a dynamic, urban campus community seamlessly connected to all that Boston has to offer. Students can tour the public art, explore how to make the student life experience their own, and take in a packed Huskies DogHouse on game nights.
  • Experiential learning: Northeastern University's programs engage students with the world through professional work, research, and service on seven continents. They learn how to transform ideas into impact and become global citizens with successful careers.
  • Research: Research at Northeastern focuses on uncovering solutions to global challenges in health, security, and sustainability. Northeastern has 30-plus research centers and eight university wide institutes. Each draws experts together from across the university, the country, and the world, in areas from coastal sustainability to cybersecurity and privacy to robotics.

Accommodation options

FIRST YEAR STUDENTS

Undergraduates who enter as first-year students to Boston through fall admission and NUin will live in university housing during their first and second years, unless a global experience or co-op experience takes them outside of the city of Boston. The First Year Experience (FYE) program has been developed to help Northeastern University’s first year students transition into this new and exciting chapter of their lives – college life.

The university reserves the right to alter these designations at any time. During certain terms, First Year students could be assigned to any university residence hall, even those without the First Year designation. Upon the return from their NUin semester, NUin Students are often placed in second year or upperclassmen accommodations based on availability for the Spring of their first year.

Refer to the Housing and Residential Life webpage to learn more.

GRADUATE AND LAW STUDENTS

There will not be University Housing for new or returning Graduate Students or Law Students for the 2023-2024 academic year.

Students are encouraged to utilize the following Northeastern University resources in their search for off-campus housing in the Boston area:

1. Contact Off Campus Support and Engagement Services by calling 617-373-8480, emailing offcampus@northeastern.edu, or viewing their website for referrals to area realtors, and advice on tenant rights and what to expect in a lease.

2. Register to use Northeastern’s Off-Campus Housing Online database or use the Roommate Feature to search for roommates and sublets. The database is continuously updated with property listings by area realtors and landlords.
