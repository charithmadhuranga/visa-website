Overview

About Western International College (WesternIC)

At Western International College, the student journey begins with more than just a pathway to a professional master’s or undergraduate degree – it’s the start of a transformative academic and personal experience.

Western International College students benefit from personalized support and mentorship from faculty and staff, build meaningful connections with peers and have the chance to study at one of Canada’s top research-intensive universities—renowned for its stunning campus and unparalleled student experience.

Located on Western University’s vibrant campus, Western International College welcomes students into the community from day one. Students will engage in hands-on learning — in the classroom, the lab and the wider community — gaining experiences that prepare them to make a real impact in the world. Join a university that’s shaping the future — today.

Why Western International College

  • Campus: Western International College is one of the most picturesque campuses in North America, located on the beautiful Thames River in London, Ontario.
  • Top-Quality Residence System: The University's residence system has continually been ranked as one of the best residence experiences among large Canadian universities.
  • Employment Opportunities: Over 2,000 undergraduate work study and paid positions are available right on campus. There are many internship, co-op, job shadow, fieldwork and other volunteer opportunities available off campus as well.

Master's · Data & Analytics

Advanced Graduate Pathway (AGP) – Transfer to Master of Data Analytics (MDA)

20 months Next intake: Jan 2027 Apply by 1 Jan 2027

The Master of Data Analytics (MDA) in Western’s Faculty of Science is a full-time, professional graduate program that prepares students with the technical expertise, professional skills and real-world experience needed to succeed in today’s data-driven workforce.

As an institution ranked in the top 1% globally, Western offers a dynamic environment where academic excellence, real-world application and career development come together. The MDA program combines advanced coursework, professional skills development and an Experiential Learning Opportunity (ELO), ensuring graduates are equipped to meet the complex analytical challenges across sectors.

Students in the Master of Data Analytics program choose one of three Specialty Fields:

  • Aritificial Intelligence
  • Finance, Banking and Insurance
  • Generalist

Students in the Pathway program will complete two terms of study at WesternIC and upon successfully meeting all progression requirements will complete the remaining three terms for the Master of Data Analytics.

Master's · Engineering & Construction

Master’s Qualifying Pathway – Master of Engineering – Chemical and Biochemical Engineering

16 months Next intake: Jan 2027 Apply by 1 Jan 2027

Western Engineering provides unique learning experiences including access to state-of-the-art research facilities, topics that impact real-life scenarios, and mentorship by internationally recognized leaders in Engineering.

Western is home to more than 600 graduate students studying in different research-based and professional-based degree programs in a range of fields and interests. The Master of Engineering (MEng) program is structured to assist qualified engineers in the advancement of their professional careers, and to provide students with the skills necessary to address key technological challenges. WIC offers various technical streams within the MEng program to allow students to receive specialized knowledge in the specific area that interests them.

Students in the Pathway program will complete one term of study at WesternIC and upon successfully meeting all progression requirements will complete the remaining three terms for the MEng degree at the Faculty of Engineering.
