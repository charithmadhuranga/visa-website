About Briercrest College and Seminary

Briercrest is a community of learning that calls students to seek the kingdom of God, to be shaped profoundly by the scriptures, and to be formed spiritually and intellectually for lives of service. Briercrest has been preparing leaders for over 80 years. Briercest College's ministry emerged from a community of believers who valued the Word of God and wanted to provide Bible training. That commitment remains at the heart of what they do and why they exist. Their aim is to call students to become fully devoted followers of Jesus Christ, and their approach to reaching this goal is increasingly unique in the educational landscape.

Briercrest believes that education and calling shouldn't be disconnected. That's why they work to offer students programs in an environment that prioritizes spiritual and intellectual transformation, where students have rich, faith-building encounters with Scripture, and prepare not only for the career ahead, but for their calling: a life of service to Christ.

Briercrest College is a Christian post-secondary institution built on three pillars: a biblical/theological foundation, the formation of Christian life and service competencies, and reflection of these in arts, science, and marketplace learning.

Briercrest Seminary is an innovative centre for accredited graduate level ministry training, leadership development, and theological education, utilizing modular formats to broaden the scope of educational opportunities.

Why Briercrest College and Seminary

  • Campus: The centrepiece of our campus is the 2,400-seat Hildebrand Chapel; the largest church auditorium in the province.
  • Facilities: The campus includes a range of facilities that support our students’ learning, growth, and enjoyment. Facilities also include a 25,000-square foot library, nine student dormitories, a hotel, recently renovated dining hall, double-court gymnasium, ice rink, numerous sports fields, Subway restaurant, and two coffee shops.
  • Co-curricular activities: There are many different events happening on campus throughout the year. Such as the Week of Welcome, the Briercrest Olympics, the RETHINK Conference, Day of Prayer, Voices from the Front, A Briercrest Christmas, Camp Days, Youth Quake, and more.
  • Culture: Life at Briercrest means being part of a tight-knit community where together they pursue what it means to follow Jesus. The Student Development team and student leaders work together to facilitate an environment for spiritual growth that extends into all aspects of life.

Student life & support

Work while studying

International students can work part-time alongside their studies (subject to visa conditions).

Co-op & placements

Co-op options are limited — ask us about placement-friendly alternatives.

On-campus housing

Students typically arrange off-campus or homestay accommodation.

Accommodation options

Students need to plan their accommodations well in advance of their arrival as all accommodation options fill up quickly, well before the start of the semester.

On-Campus Residence Accommodations

The dorm community is an integral aspect of the student experience and is a highlight for many of the students. The dorms provide a unique opportunity to build close relationships, be part of a community focused on discipleship, and have a ton of fun.

Each dorm has a paid staff member, a Residence Director (RD), that provides a pastoral presence in the dorm.

The RD works alongside Residence Assistants (RA), student leaders, in each hall to support and encourage students’ growth and success.

Off-Campus Accommodations

The local area has a variety of off-campus rental housing options including single homes, duplexes, apartments and rooms for rent. Students wishing to live off-campus need to research availability on their own and should arrive well before the start of term to do so.

Homestay

Briercrest offers housing options for mature students and families listed below. Mature students are those who are: 23 years of age or older at the beginning of the semester requested, and in their 4th year and have spent at least six semesters living in dorm.

Before they will be considered for any school-owned housing, students must be accepted into the college or seminary and pay their confirmation deposit. Once they have paid, the Admissions staff will put them in touch with the housing manager, who can provide information regarding school owned rental options. All single students wishing to live out of dorm must first be approved for Community Housing through the Student Development office before moving into any of the housing options below.

  • School-owned rental units: The school offers a number of rental units that provide student families with comfortable accommodation during their time at Briercrest. All units are subject to availability.
  • Houses: Students may also purchase privately owned houses on campus. Houses or basement suites may be rented (when available) by private arrangement with the owner.
  • Privately owned mobile homes: Students may purchase mobile homes to avoid rental costs during their time on campus.
