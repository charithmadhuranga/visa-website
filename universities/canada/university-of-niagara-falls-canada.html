About University of Niagara Falls Canada

The University of Niagara Falls Canada (UNF) is situated in the heart of Niagara Falls and is committed to cultivating an academic community that prepares graduates for leadership in a digital world. Through an immersive learning setting that promotes interactive and experiential education, UNF takes pride in delivering a distinctive academic journey that readies prospective graduates for success in their chosen fields.

UNF will be offering a host of distinctive undergraduate and graduate programs to meet the needs of today’s employers, including emerging technology, data analytics, business and digital media.

Informed by labour market needs of the region and local employers, UNF received consent to offer five programs:

  • Honours Bachelor of Science – Biomedical Sciences.
  • Honours Bachelor of Business Administration – Majors in Digital Economy and Digital Marketing.
  • Master of Data Analytics – Specializations in Marketing Analytics and Operations Analytics.
  • Master of Management – Specializations in Emerging Technology and Entrepreneurship.
  • Master of Arts in Digital Media and Global Communications.

All academic programs will have a professional focus that prepares learners for a digital world.

Why University of Niagara Falls Canada

  • Small Class Sizes: UNF prides itself on small class sizes and first-class student supports.
  • Flexibility: Flexibility at UNF means students can study when and how they want. The vision of the university is that every academic program will be offered in three formats: face-to-face, online, and hybrid.
  • Accessible: With multiple intakes each year, UNF helps student mix work and study to suit their personal circumstances.

Master's · Social Sciences & Psychology

Master of Arts – Applied Social Psychology

1.5 years Next intake: Jan 2027 Apply by 1 Dec 2026

The Master of Arts in Applied Social Psychology program is structured to provide an integrated, comprehensive curriculum that prepares graduates for careers demanding extensive knowledge and expertise in the complex interplay between social factors and human behavior. The program focuses on examining how social influences, social perception, and social interaction affect the health and well-being of individuals, groups, and communities.

Its mission is to offer students with a background in psychology the opportunity to gain advanced knowledge and competency in applied social psychology. Participants learn how thoughts, feelings, and actions are shaped by the actual, imagined, or implied presence of others.

The program provides a solid foundation in areas such as social cognition, social identity, social influence, group dynamics, and interpersonal relationships. The core curriculum includes topics such as attribution, persuasion, self-concept, implicit bias and prejudice, conformity, bullying and aggression, and explores how these factors impact individual, group, and societal well-being. Applications to areas such as criminal activity, substance abuse, and domestic abuse are also examined. In addition, the program aligns with institutional learning outcomes that emphasize a digital mindset, collaboration and problem-solving, and proficiency in big data analytics.

Through the study of these topics, students learn to relate and apply their understanding of the fundamental principles of the discipline to contemporary issues and challenges. The purposeful integration of curriculum elements enables them to apply foundational concepts in the field to real-world problems. Multiple group and peer teaching opportunities are built into the program, allowing students to develop team-based communication and collaboration skills—essential competencies in careers in social psychology. Ultimately, students cultivate intellectual curiosity and a commitment to lifelong learning, both of which contribute to their long-term professional success.
