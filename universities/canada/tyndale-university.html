About Tyndale University

Located in Toronto, Tyndale University has been training Christian leaders since 1894 and offers a wide range of programs at the undergraduate, seminary and graduate levels. Tyndale Seminary is Canada’s largest seminary and is located in Toronto — Canada’s largest city. Tyndale Seminary encourages its students to embrace a holistic approach to their education by putting into practice what they learn and faculty embrace the same approach.

Tyndale University is dedicated to the pursuit of truth, to excellence in teaching, learning and research, for the enriching of mind, heart and character, to serve the church and the world for the glory of God.

Why Tyndale?

  • Fully Accredited Programs: Tyndale University is accredited by the Ontario Ministry of Training, Colleges and Universities and the Association of Theological Schools.
  • Diversity: Students at Tyndale represent 60 ethnic backgrounds and 40 different Christian denominations. Diverse faculty have studied and taught around the world, bringing the perspective of global Christian ministry into the classroom
  • Canada’s Largest Seminary: Tyndale University is Canada’s largest seminary, their size allows them to provide a wide-ranging education.
  • The Centre for Academic Excellence: The Centre offers free services that will give students practical tools to achieve their potential. Students of all abilities have benefitted from personal academic advising, writing workshops and seminars offered.

Master's · Social Sciences & Psychology

Master of Arts – Thanatology

2 years Next intake: Sep 2027 Apply by 1 Sep 2027

Thanatology is the study of grief, bereavement, death and dying. This growing discipline explores the grieving process, ethical issues surrounding death and dying, cultural, gender and societal attitudes towards death, the issues facing both the dying and the bereaved, and the place of ritual and memorialization.

Many Thanatology students find that ministering to others through grief or loss is rewarding, benefiting the recipient through a difficult period in their life. The program will equip students with the knowledge and skills for assisting those facing or coping with grief and death from the perspective of both the dying and the bereaved. The course will also direct students toward biblical and spiritual resources in grief and bereavement.

On-campus housing

Students typically arrange off-campus or homestay accommodation.

Accommodation options

Students need to plan their accommodations well in advance of their arrival as all accommodation options fill up quickly, well before the start of the semester.

On-Campus Residence Accommodations

On-Campus residence accommodations are provided per discretion by the school.

Off-Campus Accommodations

The local area has a variety of off-campus rental housing options including single homes, duplexes, apartments and rooms for rent. Students wishing to live off-campus need to research availability on their own and should arrive well before the start of term to do so.
