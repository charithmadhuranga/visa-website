About Yorkville University

Yorkville University began with a simple idea: offer flexible, rigorous, and career-focused programs that lead to personal and professional transformation. Since 2004, Yorkville University has successfully grown to encompass three campuses from coast to coast in Canada.

Our graduates have gone on to launch, change or advance their careers through programs in business, interior design, creative arts, behavioral sciences, and social sciences.

Yorkville University’s programs are designed with the students’ needs first. We equip graduates with the skills that employers value, so that each student can make their own progress. Our approach to education has helped us become Canada’s largest private university, and the university of choice for students from more than 100 countries.

Why Yorkville University?

  • Career-focused degrees to get you job ready
  • Eligibility to apply for a Post-Graduation Work Permit
  • Up to $20,000 CAD tuition bursary and scholarship
  • Accelerated degrees so you can start working sooner
  • Flexible class schedule so you can work part-time while studying
  • Personalized learning with an average of 20 students per class
  • Students without Grade 12 Mathematics are eligible to apply
  • Dedicated program advisors and a Student Success Team provide health, wellness, and career support

Bachelor's · Business & Management

Bachelor of Business Administration – Accounting

4 years Next intake: Jan 2027 Apply by 23 Nov 2026

The Bachelor of Business Administration degree with a Specialization in Accounting equips students with the professional knowledge, skills and designation prerequisites to succeed in today’s job market.

The industry-driven program uniquely develops analytical and interpersonal skills, including teamwork, communication and problem solving, and was designed to meet the needs of employers.

The program culminates in a capstone project, which allows students to integrate and apply their financial and managerial accounting knowledge in a practical business scenario. This comprehensive project requires students to demonstrate their expertise by completing an auditing project, reflecting the skills and knowledge acquired throughout the program.

Students also gain job-ready competencies, such as ethical leadership and financial decision-making, equipping them to succeed in both entry-level and advanced roles in the field of accounting.

English Proficiency:

  • IELTS 6.5+ (or equivalent): Direct enrollment in the regular academic program.
  • IELTS 6.0 to <6.5 (or equivalent): Enrollment in the regular program along with the English for Academic Purposes (EAP) course in Term 1.
  • The EAP course counts toward the degree and incurs no additional cost.

Bachelor's · Business & Management

Bachelor of Business Administration – General (2.5 years)

2.5 years Next intake: Jan 2027 Apply by 23 Nov 2026

Although diploma or vocational programs are sufficient to acquire entry-level positions, many management level positions require a bachelors degree. Yorkville University has developed a Bachelor of Business Administration (BBA) that provides a broad-spectrum business education to working professionals and entrepreneurs who seek career advancement. The BBA program is flexible and accessible, allowing students to transfer previous academic credits towards elective courses and finish in as few as 2.5 years depending on their course load and/or number of transfer credits from previous post-secondary study.

The BBA at Yorkville University is not a self-directed program, where students study in isolation. Highly qualified instructors bring practical, collaborative, and up-to-date business knowledge to the online community of this program. The small class sizes and virtual classrooms allow for meaningful interactions so students develop strong networks with their fellow students and faculty.

Yorkville University’s delivery model encourages students to stay motivated, finish their degree faster, and to realize the benefits of having a degree in terms of increased salary and increased opportunities for leadership roles.

Since many of our students are working adults, the BBA program builds upon the skills acquired from a diploma and experiences obtained in the workplace, giving our graduates the managerial capabilities and problem-solving skills that employers require of their managers and business leaders. Courses are structured to help students understand a range of theoretical and functional applications in business through the following key features:

BBA students become well-versed in theoretical business concepts and skills and also gain experience applying these concepts and skills to real-world contexts.

English Proficiency

Students with an IELTS score of 6.5 (or equivalent) will enroll in Yorkville University’s regular academic program without condition. Students with an IELTS score (or equivalent) of 6.0 but below 6.5 will enroll in the regular academic program but will enroll in an English for Academic Purposes (EAP) course in Term 1. (This course is a credit course that counts towards the completion of the degree. There is no additional charge for the EAP course.)

Note: Students applying after completing a diploma program or degree program will be considered for ‘block transfer’, which means the entire program is assessed and, if approved, 36 transfer credits will be awarded against the Bachelor of Business Administration Business elective courses. Students whose diploma or degree program included courses in business administration may be awarded up to a total of 60 transfer credits (50% of total credits required in the program, excluding project-based courses). The maximum of 60 transfer credits includes the block transfer. If a student wishes for a ‘block transfer’, or to transfer credits over to the BBA they must provide descriptions for each of the courses they have previously taken.

Master of Business Administration

16 months – 2 years Next intake: May 2027 Apply by 1 May 2027

The Master of Business Administration (MBA) program at Yorkville University is designed to prepare international students with the skills and mindset to become ethical, purpose-driven leaders in today’s fast-changing global economy.

This dynamic and flexible MBA is ideal for those looking to:

  • Drive meaningful change – Address critical environmental and social challenges with innovative business strategies.
  • Accelerate career growth in Canada – Build a strong foundation in responsible leadership while studying in one of the world’s most diverse countries.
  • Balance learning and life – Enjoy a curriculum that accommodates your professional and personal commitments.

Grounded in sustainability and real-world impact, the program empowers students to grow confidently, lead responsibly, and thrive globally.

Assuming an average course load, the tuition cost per term is $9,450.

By selecting to take more or fewer courses per term, costs will be updated accordingly. A typical course is 3 credits. Students must complete 45 study credits. Total program tuition may vary based on actual credit transfers and course equivalencies.
