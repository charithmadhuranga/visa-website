Post-Study Work Options: Australia, Canada, UK, NZ & USA

15 April 2026 LGA Research Desk 8 min read
Home Blog Destinations

Post-study work options can be one factor when comparing destinations, but eligibility and duration depend on your course, qualification, circumstances and application date. These routes do not guarantee employment, permanent residence or another visa. Rules change, so use the official sources linked below before making plans.

Australia — Temporary Graduate (subclass 485)

The Temporary Graduate visa has different streams. Eligibility and stay period depend on factors including your qualification and the stream you apply under. Check the current Australian Department of Home Affairs subclass 485 guidance.

Canada — PGWP

A Post-Graduation Work Permit is available only to eligible graduates and programs; a school's DLI status alone does not establish program eligibility. Permit duration depends on the program and other requirements. Review the current IRCC PGWP guidance.

United Kingdom — Graduate Route

Under current GOV.UK rules, the Graduate visa lasts 2 years for applications made on or before 31 December 2026, and 18 months for applications made on or after 1 January 2027. Doctoral graduates can get 3 years. See the latest UK Graduate visa eligibility and duration rules.

New Zealand — Post-Study Work Visa

Eligible graduates may be able to stay and work for up to 3 years, depending on qualification level and study duration. Conditions differ by qualification and can restrict the work you do. Check Immigration New Zealand's Post-Study Work Visa guidance.

USA — OPT & STEM OPT

Eligible F-1 students may apply for up to 12 months of Optional Practical Training related to their major; some qualifying STEM graduates may apply for a 24-month extension subject to additional requirements. See USCIS OPT guidance.

How to choose

Compare the course and institution, total costs, work-route eligibility and your longer-term plans. Confirm requirements with the relevant government authority and university before committing; only immigration authorities decide visa applications.

Last reviewed: 19 November 2026. Rules and requirements change — confirm current details on official government websites.
