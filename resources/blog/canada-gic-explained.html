Canada GIC Explained: Proof of Funds Without the Stress

22 May 2026 LGA Counselling Team 5 min read
Home Blog Finances

A GIC (Guaranteed Investment Certificate) is a bank investment product that may be used as part of the financial evidence for a Canadian study permit application. It does not guarantee approval, and the financial documents required depend on the current rules and your circumstances. The Student Direct Stream (SDS) ended in November 2024; check current application instructions before relying on any older SDS checklist.

What it actually is

A GIC is an investment held with a financial institution under its product terms. The required amount, eligible issuers, account activation process and payout schedule can vary. Do not rely on a fixed amount or payout description without checking directly with the bank and the current government instructions.

How it may support your application

  • The issuer provides documentation about the investment and funds.
  • It can be considered alongside tuition, living costs, travel and other financial evidence.
  • Immigration officers assess the complete application; a GIC does not ensure a faster decision or visa approval.

What to check before arranging one

  1. Read the current IRCC proof-of-financial-support requirements and any local visa office instructions.
  2. Confirm that the financial institution accepts applicants in your situation and review its current terms.
  3. Check the amount, transfer method, documents, account activation steps and payout schedule directly with the issuer.
  4. Keep the issuer's confirmation and include it with the other evidence requested for your application.

A GIC is only one possible part of your financial evidence. Requirements and amounts can change, so confirm them on the official IRCC proof of financial support page and with your financial institution before applying.

Last reviewed: 19 November 2026. Rules and requirements change — confirm current details on official government websites.
